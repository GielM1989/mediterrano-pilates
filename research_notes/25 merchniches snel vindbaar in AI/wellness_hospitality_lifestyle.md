# Wellness, hospitality and lifestyle venues as B2B markets for branded resale merchandise (Spain first, Europe second)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: 13 candidate niches evaluated for the "venue puts its logo on a product, its customers buy it" model of Mediterrano Pilates (woven/embroidered logo, MOQ 50, free mockup in 24 h, free worldwide shipping). Note on method: the session's web-search budget ran out after ~55 searches/fetches; everything below is sourced, and everything that could not be sourced is listed under Gaps rather than estimated.

## Key question 1: How many venues exist per niche in Spain and Europe?

### Takeaway
The largest addressable pools in Spain are hair salons/barbershops (~33,900 companies), rural accommodations (16,288), artisan bakeries (11,000-11,500) and tattoo studios (2,800-3,000); the wine-tourism, coworking, craft-beer and balneario pools are small (hundreds) but institutionally organised and easy to list. No reliable counts exist for specialty coffee shops, beach clubs, cooking schools, recovery studios or meditation centres; those must be treated as estimate-only niches.

### Cited Findings

**Boutique / rural hotels (hoteles boutique, casas rurales)**
- Spain has 16,288 rural-tourism accommodations with 161,849 bed-places; 12.87 million overnight stays in 2025; average annual occupancy 21.6%, half of all nights in June-September — [Hosteltur, citing INE 2025](https://www.hosteltur.com/176121_el-boom-del-turismo-rural-se-frena-en-espana-las-pernoctaciones-solo-crecen-el-12.html)
- Rural-tourism overnight stays grew 1.2% in 2025; December 2025 occupancy 17.8% (29.6% at weekends); Castilla y León leads with 1.8 million nights — [INE, EOAT December 2025](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EOAT1225.htm)
- Spanish hotels recorded 366.7 million overnight stays in 2025, ADR 127.7 EUR, RevPAR 89.7 EUR — [INE, Coyuntura Turística Hotelera 2025](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/CTH1225.htm)
- Chain hotels ("sector organizado") hold almost three quarters of analysed Spanish hotel supply (626,000+ units as of 1 Jan 2025); the independent segment is roughly one quarter; "digital specialization enables the creation of chains focused on small establishments, which previously operated mostly independently" — [Alimarket, Informe 2025 Grupos Hoteleros](https://www.alimarket.es/hoteles/informe/400411/informe-2025-de-grupos-hoteleros-por-establecimientos-en-espana)
- EU-27: 681,000 tourist-accommodation establishments in 2024 (+44,500 vs 2023) with 29.7 million bed-places; of hotels with size data, 56.7% have fewer than 25 bedrooms (the "boutique-sized" segment), 34.3% have 25-99, 9.1% have 100+; Spain is the EU's largest destination with 505 million nights and 62% hotel bed-place occupancy — [Eurostat, Tourism statistics - annual results for the accommodation sector](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Tourism_statistics_-_annual_results_for_the_accommodation_sector)
- Spain accounts for 3,847,619 tourist bed-places in 2024, third in the EU after Italy and France — [Eurostat indicator tin00181 via eupublicdata.eu](https://eupublicdata.eu/data/indicators/tin00181/)

**Coworking spaces**

### Inferences
- By raw venue count, salons/barbershops (33,882) and rural accommodations (16,288) dwarf the pilates/yoga studio market; but rural accommodations average under 10 beds (161,849 plazas / 16,288 = ~9.9 beds per property) and 21.6% occupancy, so per-venue sell-through of a 50-unit MOQ is slow.
- The niches with an official, enumerable list (60 ANBAL balnearios; ~1,069 coworkings with 14 audited operators; 37-38 Rutas del Vino with member bodegas; AECAI member breweries) are the easiest to prospect and to name in AI-facing content ("proveedor de merchandising para bodegas de la Ruta del Vino de ...").
- Europe multiplies the hotel pool ~40x (681,000 establishments, 56.7% of hotels under 25 rooms) and the brewery pool ~30x (~12,848 breweries), so English-language pages for hotels and breweries have much larger headroom than Spanish-only pages.

### Gaps
- Total number of open hotel establishments in Spain (INE "establecimientos abiertos estimados") could not be retrieved: the INE table fetch returned HTTP 500 and the monthly press releases only give nights/ADR/RevPAR. The Statista dossier exists but was not opened — [Statista hotel sector dossier](https://es.statista.com/estudio/32252/el-sector-hotelero-en-espana-dossier-de-statista); INE table 2066 — [INE](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/Tabla.htm?t=2066&L=0).
- No national count for specialty coffee shops, beach clubs (national), cooking schools, recovery studios or meditation centres; SCA Spain / Fórum Café data were not reachable before the search budget ran out.
- No count of bodegas that actually have a visitor centre/shop (ACEVIN reports visitors, not venues).
- Craft brewery count is unresolved (282 vs 420 vs 522 depending on source and year); the number with a taproom is unknown.

## Key question 2: Existing merch culture and evidence per niche, typical items and price points

### Takeaway
Strong, documented resale-merch cultures exist in specialty coffee (Spanish roasters sell caps at 30 EUR, totes at 18-38 EUR, tumblers at 11-25 EUR), luxury/boutique hotels (UK/US press calls logoed goods "the top retail profit centre"), Ibiza beach clubs (Nikki Beach, O Beach run full web shops) and, by inference, breweries; wineries have a gift-shop culture but dominated by wine accessories; salons, tattoo studios, balnearios, coworkings, bakeries and retreats have thin or undocumented resale-merch cultures.

### Cited Findings

**Specialty coffee (strongest Spanish evidence)**
- Hola Coffee Roasters (Madrid) sells 14 merch SKUs: cap 30 EUR, "Roasted in Madrid" T-shirt 26 EUR, artist-collab T-shirt 28 EUR, tote bag 18 EUR, ICE tumbler 11 EUR, glass 11 EUR, Fellow Carter thermos 39.50 EUR (sold out), keyring/badge 6 EUR — [Hola Coffee merchandising](https://hola.coffee/en/collections/merchandising)
- Nomad Coffee (Barcelona) sells heavyweight tote 38 EUR, T-shirts 35-40 EUR, mug 13 EUR, thermos 25 EUR, kids' hat 31.60 EUR, tasting spoon 15 EUR, knife collab 36 EUR, coaster 70 EUR, body scrub 34 EUR, plus an "exclusive product for coffee shops" at 71.50 EUR — [Nomad Coffee merchandising](https://nomadcoffee.es/en/collections/merchandising); design-led tote by Julia Esqué/Basora — [Nomad tote](https://nomadcoffee.es/en/products/nomad-tote-bag)
- Forbes (Feb 2026) describes "cafécore": merchandise-engaged customers show a "mid-single to low-double digit lift in annual customer value"; reusable cups/mugs get the highest engagement because of daily use; merch carries higher margins than beverages and no shelf-life pressure; examples Cha Cha Matcha, Ralph's Coffee, Pura Vida, Intelligentsia (all US) — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/katehardcastle/2026/02/16/cafcore-and-the-rise-of-coffee-shop-merchandise/)
- European roaster example Spojka Roastery (Prešov, Slovakia, founded 2022) sells caps, beanies, tumblers, clothing, prints and a skateboard-deck collab — [Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2025/12/coffee-roasters-packaging-merchandise-stand-out/)
- US promotional pieces cite hoodie 40 USD and tote 25 USD as typical café merch prices; no revenue-share data, US-only — [Diskko](https://www.diskko.com/blog/how-coffee-shops-use-branded-merchandise-to-grow-revenue-and-build-loyal-communities)

**Boutique hotels**
- Boutique Hotelier (4 March 2026): Leven (Manchester) sells branded socks, T-shirts, bucket hats, umbrellas and dog bowls, with Pride rainbow socks a bestseller; Hotel Café Royal (London) is building a retail line for Selfridges; Gleneagles sells caps; The Boca Raton CEO Daniel Hostettler: logoed pieces "outperform third-party goods by roughly five times on margin" and "Logoed products are consistently the top retail profit centre. When the design is restrained and the quality is high, they sustain premium economics"; collaborations Ritz Paris x Frame, Burberry x The Newt — [Boutique Hotelier](https://www.boutiquehotelier.com/branded-hotel-merchandise-trend-analysis/)
- Fashion and retail brands are opening hotels (brand-merch convergence) — [Hotel Management](https://www.hotelmanagement.net/development/fashion-retail-brands-branch-out-into-hotels)
- UK retail-fixtures supplier positions "boutique hotel stores" as a distinct retail category — [The Retail Factory](https://theretailfactory.co.uk/top-retail-supplies-for-boutique-hotel-stores-what-you-need/)
- Paradores (Spain, 90+ state hotels) opened its first "tienda de experiencias" — [Paradores blog](https://paradores.es/es/blog/paradores-abre-su-primera-tienda-de-experiencias) (page content could not be fetched; existence confirmed by [Vidapremium](https://vidapremium.com/tiempo-libre/792660629/Paradores-abre-su-tienda-de-experiencias.html))

**Beach clubs**
- Nikki Beach runs a global web shop; Ibiza collection has 43 products from 55 to 389 USD: hats 110-115 USD, bags 180-240 USD (Mercedes Salazar collab), dresses 156-350 USD, bikinis 188-290 USD — [Nikki Beach shop, Ibiza collection](https://shop.nikkibeach.com/collections/ibiza); brand story — [About Nikki Beach shop](https://shop.nikkibeach.com/pages/about-us)

**Breweries**

US-only evidence; no European brewery-merch specialist surfaced.

**Salons / barbershops**
- Spanish offer is framed as "regalos para clientes de peluquería" and uniforms (giveaways, not resale) — [Grupo Billingham](https://www.grupobillingham.com/regalos-peluquerias), [Euroserigrafía](https://euroserigrafia.com/es/content/183-merchandising-personalizada-para-peluquer%C3%ADas-), [Coarte](https://www.coarte.net/regalos-para-peluquerias/)

**Hotels (Spain, supplier framing)**
- Withlogo's hotel page frames items as welcome amenities/giveaways: "el huésped lleva el artículo a casa y lo usa semanas después del check-out"; recycled-cotton tote 0.43 EUR (MOQ 100), steel bottle 500 ml 2.49 EUR (MOQ 100) — [Withlogo hoteles](https://withlogo.es/merchandising-con-tu-logo-a/merchandising-para-hoteles/)
- Gift Campaign's hotel page (bathrobes, towels, slippers, toothbrushes, sleep masks, notepads) is likewise positioned as welcome gifts; prices 0.16-18.97 EUR, MOQs from 5 to 100; techniques include embroidery; socks are not offered — [Gift Campaign regalos hoteles](https://www.giftcampaign.es/regalos-hoteles.html)

### Inferences
- Spanish specialty coffee already prices merch at pilates-studio levels (cap 30 EUR, tote 18-38 EUR, tumbler 11-25 EUR), so the Mediterrano margin story transfers without re-education.
- In hotels, the Spanish supply side talks exclusively about amenities/giveaways; the resale framing ("tienda del hotel", "top retail profit centre") appears only in UK/US press. That is a positioning gap Mediterrano can own in Spanish ("merchandising para vender, no para regalar").
- Beach-club merch is fashion-priced (hats 110 USD) and design-led; a supplier offering woven/embroidered logos on caps, towels and totes fits, but the buyer will compare against fashion collabs, not promo catalogues.
- Winery gift shops are already served by wine-accessory specialists; textiles (tote, cap, apron) are secondary there.

### Gaps
- No Spanish hotel, balneario, coworking, tattoo, bakery or retreat venue web shop with branded resale merch was found within the session; absence of evidence in a ~55-call session is not proof of absence.
- No revenue-share or margin data for merch in any European venue type; the "5x margin" quote is a single US resort CEO.

## Key question 3: Best-fitting products; what transfers from the current range (tumblers, totes, socks, rings) and what would be needed

### Takeaway
Tumblers and totes transfer to every niche with a takeaway or gift moment (coffee, hotels, beach clubs, wineries, coworkings, breweries); socks transfer only where feet are bare or the item is a joke/loyalty piece (spas, retreats, recovery studios, boutique hotels with a playful brand); most hospitality niches need towels, robes, caps/bucket hats, glassware/ceramics and aprons, which are not in the current catalogue.

### Cited Findings
- Coffee: bestsellers seen at Spanish roasters are caps, T-shirts, totes, tumblers/glasses, mugs, keyrings — [Hola Coffee](https://hola.coffee/en/collections/merchandising), [Nomad](https://nomadcoffee.es/en/collections/merchandising); reusable cups/mugs show the highest engagement because of daily use — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/katehardcastle/2026/02/16/cafcore-and-the-rise-of-coffee-shop-merchandise/)
- Boutique hotels: socks, T-shirts, bucket hats, umbrellas, dog bowls, caps — [Boutique Hotelier](https://www.boutiquehotelier.com/branded-hotel-merchandise-trend-analysis/); hotel textile suppliers exist for robes/towels (Itexa, Vayoil/Hottels, Vistiendo Hotel, Fresmarvi, Sucdecotto) — [Itexa](https://itexa.es/albornoces-hotel/), [Hottels by Vayoil](https://hottels.es/), [Vistiendo Hotel](https://www.vistiendohotel.com/), [Fresmarvi](https://www.fresmarvi.es/tienda/albornoz), [Sucdecotto](https://sucdecotto.com/albornoz-hosteleria)
- Beach clubs: hats/bucket hats, bags, towels (implied by the category), apparel, fans, mugs, bottles — [Nikki Beach](https://shop.nikkibeach.com/collections/ibiza), [O Beach](https://store.obeachibiza.com/)
- Wineries: bottle bags, corkscrews, coolers, glasses, stoppers, boards — [Gift Campaign](https://www.giftcampaign.es/blog/merchandising-enoturismo/); Koala Spain supplies HORECA/bodega accessories (corkscrews, coolers, pourers) at MOQs 48-2,500 — [Koala Spain](https://koalaspain.com/en/cat/profesionales/)
- Breweries (US pattern): glassware, caps, patches, T-shirts, koozies, coasters — [Makers Merch](https://makers-merch.com/brewery-custom-merchandise), [All Breweries Merch](https://www.allbreweriesmerch.com/)
- Hotels (Spanish promo suppliers): totes, steel bottles, towels, keychains, notebooks, slippers, sleep masks — [Withlogo](https://withlogo.es/merchandising-con-tu-logo-a/merchandising-para-hoteles/), [Gift Campaign](https://www.giftcampaign.es/regalos-hoteles.html)

### Inferences
- Transfer matrix (inference from the item lists above):
  - Tumbler: coffee (core), coworking (core), hotels (yes), beach clubs (yes), breweries (partial; glassware preferred), wineries (weak), salons/tattoo (weak).
  - Tote bag: coffee (core), bakeries (core, bread bag), wineries (bottle tote variant), hotels/rural (yes), beach clubs (yes, beach tote), coworking (yes), retreats (yes).
  - Grip socks: retreats and yoga-surf camps (yes), spas/balnearios and recovery studios (yes, as slipper socks), boutique hotels (niche, "hotel socks"), all others (no).
  - Rings/jewellery: beach clubs and retreats (possible), others (no).
- Needed additions, ranked by how many niches ask for them: caps/bucket hats (coffee, breweries, beach clubs, hotels, tattoo, surf), embroidered towels (beach clubs, spas, hotels, retreats, barbers), robes/slippers (spas, hotels), ceramics/mugs (coffee, bakeries, coworking, hotels), aprons (cooking schools, bakeries, wineries, barbers), glassware (breweries, wineries). Caps and towels are the two additions that unlock the most niches and keep the "woven or embroidered, never printed" rule intact.
- Bakeries are the only niche where a single existing product (tote) is the natural hero item.

### Gaps
- No sell-through or bestseller data by product for any European venue type; the transfer matrix is inference from assortments, not sales.

## Key question 4: Existing specialised suppliers in Spain/Europe; share of generic promo/POD vs specialists; saturation

### Takeaway
Spain has no visible specialist in resale merchandise for hospitality or wellness venues; the field is generic promotional-gift houses (Gift Campaign, Withlogo, Regalopublicidad, Pongomilogo, PMK Ideas, Grupo Billingham) with sector landing pages, plus HORECA-accessory and hotel-textile specialists. The only dense specialist tier found is US brewery/coffee merch, and one competitor (Withlogo) already copies the "free mockup in 24 h + free shipping" offer.
