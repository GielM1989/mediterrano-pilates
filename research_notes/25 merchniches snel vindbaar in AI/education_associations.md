# Education and membership-organisation niches as B2B markets for branded resale merchandise (Spain first, Europe second)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Method note for the report writer: the session's shared web-search budget was exhausted after the first ~15 queries and most direct fetches to supplier/association sites (El Corte Inglés, Valero, CEAPA, CONCAPA, Unión Profesional, CSD, tienda.ucm.es, store.ie.edu, BOE) were blocked by the fetch tool's provenance gate. Everything under "Cited Findings" was verified from a fetched page. Supplier names and rules that could not be fetched are listed under "Gaps" as unverified leads, never as facts. No Europe-wide organisation counts could be verified except for international schools.

Product context for fit assessments (from the Mediterrano project brief, not external): logo woven or embroidered, never printed; grip socks (wholesale approx. 9 EUR, resale 17.95–21.95 EUR in Europe), tumbler (approx. 11 EUR), tote bag (approx. 6.50 EUR), pilates ring (approx. 7 EUR); minimum 50 pairs for socks; free mockup within 24 h; production and shipping 4–7 weeks; free worldwide shipping.

## Niche 1: Private and concertado schools (colegios) and their AMPAs

### Takeaway
Spain has roughly 9,300–9,400 private/concertado schools (about a third of all general-education centres) with 2.76 million pupils and 14.4 billion EUR annual revenue; uniform culture is strong in this segment, but no data was found on "spirit wear" resale, and the AMPA count could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Spain had 28,832 centres of "Enseñanzas de Régimen General no universitarias" in 2023-24: 19,399 public and 9,433 concertado + private — [La Moncloa, Datos y cifras curso escolar 2024-2025 (PDF)](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/serviciosdeprensa/notasprensa/educacion-fp-deportes/Documents/2024/110924-%20datos-y-cifras-curso-escolar-2024-2025.pdf)
- Share of general-secondary pupils by centre type (2023-24): 66.8% public, 24.5% concertado, 8.6% non-concertado private; total projected pupils 2024-25: 8,348,030 — [La Moncloa, Datos y cifras 2024-2025](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/serviciosdeprensa/notasprensa/educacion-fp-deportes/Documents/2024/110924-%20datos-y-cifras-curso-escolar-2024-2025.pdf)
- 9,330 private centres in 2024-25, 60% of them concertado; 2.76 million pupils (2.05 million concertado, 714,000 non-concertado private); sector revenue 14,400 million EUR/year, of which concertado 10,900 million EUR; 85% of revenue from tuition and teaching services (source: Observatorio Sectorial DBK de Informa) — [Infobae, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.infobae.com/espana/2026/03/05/los-colegios-privados-y-concertados-alcanzan-14400-millones-en-ingresos-mientras-la-publica-pierde-alumnos-y-cierra-centros/)
- Statista tracks number of infant-education centres by type in Spain 2016-2024 (paywalled detail) — [Statista](https://es.statista.com/estadisticas/478466/centros-que-imparten-educacion-infantil-por-tipo-en-espana)
- Camaloon (EU-wide personalised merchandise, 2,500+ items incl. bags, apparel, drinkware; in-house production; 6–7 day customisation + delivery; operates in Spain, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal) explicitly lists schools among its target customers — [Camaloon](https://www.camaloon.com/)

### Inferences
- The addressable set for "school puts logo on product, parents buy it" is the 9,330 private/concertado centres, not the 19,399 public ones: private centres control their own procurement and already sell uniforms, so an extra branded line (socks, tote, bottle) fits an existing parent-purchase habit. Basis: centre counts above plus the 85%-tuition revenue structure (they are used to charging families directly).
- Decision maker: school director / gerente (private), or the AMPA board for end-of-stage items (graduation hoodies, "sudadera de promoción"); order size per school likely 100–500 units for a single item across a school of several hundred pupils. Estimate, not sourced.
- Woven/embroidered logos match the uniform aesthetic (embroidered crests on polos) better than printed print-on-demand goods, which is a differentiator versus Camaloon-type platforms.
- Grip socks are a natural fit only where the school has a PE/psychomotricity or dance programme; tote bags (library/lunch bag) and tumblers (water bottle policies) transfer more broadly. New products likely needed: hoodies, caps, backpacks (not verified against supplier capability).

### Gaps
- Number of AMPAs in Spain and confederation membership (CEAPA for public, CONCAPA for concertado): fetches to ceapa.es and concapa.org were blocked; no verified figure.
- Uniform supplier landscape: El Corte Inglés uniformes-escolares page (https://www.elcorteingles.es/uniformes-escolares/) and Valero (valero.es) could not be fetched; "Trevira" (named in the brief) is a polyester fibre brand, not a Spanish uniform retailer as far as I can tell; unverified. No saturation assessment possible from sources.
- No evidence found either way for US-style "spirit wear" resale in Spanish schools; the only merch-adjacent evidence is the uniform channel. Price points for school-branded non-uniform items: not found.
- Europe-wide count of private schools: not researched (search budget exhausted).

## Niche 2: International schools in Spain

### Takeaway
Spain has 423 international schools (ISC Research, Jan 2025), of which 143 British schools on 180 campuses with about 75,000 pupils (87% Spanish nationals); this is the segment with the clearest imported "house / school shop" culture, but no Spanish school-shop pricing could be verified.

### Cited Findings
- 423 international schools in Spain; 10% growth in number of schools 2020–2025; expansion concentrated in Madrid, Valencia and the Costa del Sol (ISC Research white paper, Jan 2025) — [ICEF Monitor, May 2025](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/05/international-schools-segment-registers-impressive-five-year-growth-numbers/)
- Global context: approaching 15,000 international schools, nearly 7.5 million students, annual fee income above 67 billion USD (ISC Research, Jan 2025) — [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/05/international-schools-segment-registers-impressive-five-year-growth-numbers/)
- 143 British schools across 180 campuses in Spain; approx. 75,000 students, 87% Spanish nationals; +16% students and +7% schools over five years; British schools are over 40% of Spain's international-school offer and outnumber those in France, Italy and Portugal combined; NABSS has 84 member schools; report "British Schools in Spain 2024–25" by ISC Research, funded by British Council, NABSS and British Chamber of Commerce in Spain, published 8 Oct 2025 — [British Council Spain press release](https://www.britishcouncil.es/about/press/los-colegios-britanicos-en-espa%C3%B1a-crecen-un-16-en-alumnado-y-refuerzan-su-papel)
- The same report notes these schools "contract local suppliers for transportation, food, and supplies" — [British Council Spain](https://www.britishcouncil.es/about/press/los-colegios-britanicos-en-espa%C3%B1a-crecen-un-16-en-alumnado-y-refuerzan-su-papel)
- ISC Research sells a Spain Market Intelligence Summary (schools, enrolments, staff, curricula, exams) for 495 GBP — [ISC Research](https://iscresearch.com/reports/spain-market-intelligence-summary/)
- NABSS (Asociación Nacional de Colegios Británicos en España) site exists for membership lists — [NABSS](https://nabss.org/es/los-mejores-colegios-britanicos-en-espana-nabss/) (page timed out; content not verified)

### Inferences
- Average British school size is roughly 75,000 / 143 ≈ 520 pupils (arithmetic on cited figures), which makes a 50-unit minimum trivial and 200–500-unit orders plausible per item.
- British/American curriculum schools import "house" systems and PE kit culture; embroidered crests are standard on their uniforms, so a woven/embroidered logo requirement matches expectations. Decision maker: Head/Bursar or the uniform-shop manager, sometimes the PTA. English-language buying, so English landing pages matter for this niche.
- Fee-paying, majority-Spanish families with international aspirations are the highest willingness-to-pay group in Spanish K-12; premium positioning fits.

### Gaps
- No verified example of a Spanish international school's online shop with prices (fetch to school shops not attempted after gate blocks).
- Number of international schools in Europe as a whole: not disclosed in the ICEF summary; ISC paywalled.
- Whether schools operate their own uniform/merch shop vs outsourcing to a uniform retailer: not verified.

## Niche 3: Universities, faculties and student associations

### Takeaway
Spain has 96 universities (50 public, 46 private) with private enrolment around 300,000; public universities carry 70% of students but are subject to public procurement rules, while private universities and student associations are the practical entry point. University merch shops exist in Spain but none could be fetched for pricing.

### Cited Findings
- 96 universities: 50 public and 46 private (Fundación CYD, "Radiografía del Sistema Universitario Español 1. Docencia", 2025); private universities have reached 300,000 students; public universities have lost over 315,000 undergraduate students (over 22%) since 1991; Madrid has 19 universities (6 public, 13 private), Andalucía 14 (9/5), Cataluña 12 (7/5); 79.7% of graduates from public, 52.5% of master's graduates from private — [El Debate, 26 Jun 2025](https://www.eldebate.com/educacion/20250626/espana-tiene-casi-tantas-universidades-privadas-como-publicas-asi-cambiado-mapa-universitario_311247.html)
- CRUE "La universidad española en cifras 2023-2024": 50 public (47 presencial, 1 non-presencial, 2 special) and 41 private universities; private universities grew from 4 in 1980 to 41 in 2023-24; public universities enrol 70.2% of all university students — [CRUE UEC 2023-2024 (PDF)](https://www.crue.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/UEC_2023-2024.pdf)
- 7,163 degree programmes (grado, máster, doctorado) offered in 2024-25, 71% by public universities (Estadística de Universidades, Centros y Titulaciones) — [Ministerio de Ciencia, Innovación y Universidades, Jun 2025](https://www.ciencia.gob.es/Noticias/2025/junio/estadistica-universidades-centros-titulaciones-2024-2025.html)
- Infobae (Jul 2025) reports the growth of private universities versus underfunded public ones — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/espana/2025/07/29/la-universidad-privada-crece-en-espana-frente-a-una-universidad-publica-limitada-por-la-falta-de-financiacion/) (not fetched; headline only)

### Inferences
- Two sub-markets: (a) the 46 private universities (often with a marketing department and campus store, buying centrally); (b) faculty-level student associations, delegaciones de alumnos, and colleges mayores at public universities, which buy in small lots for events, orientation weeks and graduations without going through a tender. The 50-unit minimum suits (b); (a) can order in the hundreds.
- Products that transfer: tote bags (welcome packs, congresses), tumblers (sustainability policies on campus), grip socks only for sports/wellness services or university pilates/yoga programmes. Hoodies and caps would be the expected core items and are not in the current range.
- Buying moments: September welcome/orientation, graduation (May–July), anniversaries, alumni events.

### Gaps
- University merch shop operators and price points in Spain (tienda.ucm.es and others) could not be fetched; unverified leads to check manually: Universidad Complutense (tienda.ucm.es), Universidad de Navarra, Universidad de Sevilla, UB (Botiga UB), UPV. No saturation assessment possible.
- Number of student associations / colegios mayores in Spain: not found.
- European university count and merch operators (e.g., UK-style union shops): not researched.

## Niche 4: Business schools and MBA alumni networks

### Takeaway
AEEN alone lists about 120 member business schools, but no total count for Spain or Europe was verified and the flagship schools' merchandise stores could not be fetched; treat as a small, high-value niche with unknown supplier saturation.

### Cited Findings
- AEEN (Asociación Española de Escuelas de Negocios) lists approximately 120+ member schools alphabetically (ACE Education to ZOWA Education), including AFI Escuela, Barcelona Executive Business School, CEREM, EALDE, ENEB, IEBS; the page gives no total for the Spanish sector — [AEEN, escuelas asociadas](https://www.aeen.org/escuelas-de-negocios-asociadas/)
- 52.5% of Spain's master's graduates come from private centres (Fundación CYD 2025) — [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/educacion/20250626/espana-tiene-casi-tantas-universidades-privadas-como-publicas-asi-cambiado-mapa-universitario_311247.html)

### Inferences
- Merch use case here is gifting (welcome kits for incoming cohorts, alumni-club events, executive-education programmes) more than resale; tumbler and tote bag transfer directly, grip socks only for wellness/leadership retreats. Decision maker: admissions/marketing or the alumni-relations office. Order sizes track cohort size (tens to a few hundred).
- Fewer, wealthier buyers; AEEN's list is a ready prospect list of ~120 institutions.

### Gaps
- Total number of business schools in Spain: not found. IE Store (store.ie.edu), ESADE and IESE shops could not be fetched; existence and prices unverified.
- No data on alumni-network merchandise programmes.

## Niche 5: Language academies

### Takeaway
Spain has 3,952 private language centres with about 2.03 million students (average 514 per centre), a fragmented owner-operated sector with a strong September/October intake; no specialised merchandise supplier for academies was identified.

### Cited Findings
- 3,952 private language schools in Spain; approx. 2,030,000 students per year; average 514 students per centre; size distribution: 34.5% of centres about 200 students, 24.1% about 400, 16.4% about 625, 6.9% about 875, 15.5% about 1,200, 2.6% about 100; public Escuelas Oficiales de Idiomas: about 470 centres and 331,000 students; private sector 86% of the market; 80% of centres held or grew enrolment, 65.5% raised prices (typically 3–5%); English dominates, Spanish for foreigners in 30% of centres; FECEI study with 200+ affiliated companies, published 6 Jul 2026 — [Diario en Positivo](https://www.diarioenpositivo.com/educacion/espana-tiene-3952-centros-privados-idiomas-que-estudian-mas-dos-millones-alumnos-fecei/20260706132913085729.html)
- FECEI (Federación Española de Centros de Enseñanza de Idiomas, founded 1980) groups regional associations (e.g., ACEIA, ACADE's FECEI section) and individual centres; partners include Cambridge English, Trinity College London, LanguageCert — [FECEI](https://www.fecei.org/en/); [FECEI/ACADE](https://www.fecei.org/acade/)
- FECEI offers a public academy finder — [FECEI buscador de centros](https://www.fecei.org/en/centros/buscador-centros/)

### Inferences
- Structurally similar to pilates studios (owner-operated, 100–1,200 clients, seasonal intake), so the existing "merch as revenue, not cost" pitch transfers with minimal change. Best-fitting products: tote bag (books), tumbler; grip socks do not fit. New products needed: notebooks, pens, hoodies for summer-camp/immersion programmes.
- Buying moments: September–October enrolment, summer immersion camps (June–July), Cambridge exam seasons.
- Decision maker: owner/director; typical order 50–300 units.

### Gaps
- No specialised merchandise supplier for language academies found; saturation unknown.
- Europe-wide count of private language schools: not researched.

## Niche 6: Music schools and conservatories

### Takeaway
About 1,100 music-teaching centres (conservatories and escuelas de música) are listed in Spain, including 23 conservatorios superiores; the sector is majority public/municipal, which limits resale merch to parent associations and private schools.

### Cited Findings
- A national directory lists 1,142 music centres in Spain, filterable by public/private and by level (escuelas de música, grado elemental, grado profesional, estudios superiores, máster) — [Encuentramúsico, centros de música en España](https://encuentramusico.es/centros-musica-en-espana)
- 23 conservatorios superiores de música (CSM) in Spain; Madrid's 13 municipal music schools were privatised into management by "Escuela de Música Creativa" and "Soto Mesa", removing a 70% municipal subsidy; only 7.8% of Spaniards play an instrument actively and 2.4% sing in choirs (Encuesta de hábitos y prácticas culturales 2014-2015) — [La terapia del arte, Radiografía de la enseñanza de la Música en España](https://laterapiadelarte.com/numero-14/reportajes/radiografia-de-la-ensenanza-de-la-musica-en-espana/)
- Andalucía states it has "la red de conservatorios más extensa y completa de España con 80 centros" (2024-25) — [Junta de Andalucía](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/presidencia/portavoz/educacion/196889/Educacion/cursoescolar20242025/ensenanzasdeRegimenEspecial/Musica/Danza/ArteDramatico/Diseno/Idiomas/Deportivas/conservatorios/Escuelasdearte/Escuelasoficialesdeidioma/andalucia/JuntadeAndalucia) (headline; page too long to fetch), also reported by [Libertad Digital, Feb 2026](https://www.libertaddigital.com/andalucia/2026-02-22/andalucia-cuenta-con-la-red-de-conservatorios-mas-extensa-y-completa-de-espana-7362864/)
- AEDOM maintains a map of conservatorios superiores — [AEDOM](https://aedom.org/mapa-de-conservatorios-superiores-de-musica-en-espana/)
- The Ministry hosts a centres directory for enseñanzas artísticas (fetch blocked by robots) — [infoartisticas.gob.es](https://www.infoartisticas.gob.es/comunidad-educativa/comunidad-educativa-centros.html)

### Inferences
- Public conservatories cannot easily resell branded goods; the buyer is the AMPA/asociación de alumnos or the private escuela de música. Products: tote bag (sheet music, the most natural fit of the current range), tumbler; grip socks no. Order sizes small (50–150).
- Low priority versus niches 1, 2 and 5: small centres, public ownership, weak merch culture evidence.

### Gaps
- Official Ministry count of música/danza centres by titularidad could not be fetched (educacionfpydeportes.gob.es blocked). The 1,142 figure is a private directory, not official.
- No supplier or price evidence for music-school merchandise.

## Niche 7: Dance academies (ballet, flamenco, urban)

### Takeaway
No verified count or merch evidence was obtained for private dance academies in Spain; grip socks are the single best product fit of the entire brief (barefoot/ballet-adjacent floor work), so this niche deserves manual verification despite the data gap.

### Cited Findings
- Dance is taught as an "enseñanza de régimen especial" alongside music in conservatorios de danza (Andalucía groups música and danza in its 80-centre conservatory network) — [Junta de Andalucía](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/presidencia/portavoz/educacion/196889/Educacion/cursoescolar20242025/ensenanzasdeRegimenEspecial/Musica/Danza/ArteDramatico/Diseno/Idiomas/Deportivas/conservatorios/Escuelasdearte/Escuelasoficialesdeidioma/andalucia/JuntadeAndalucia)

### Inferences

Gala season (June) and September intake are the buying moments. This is the most direct extension of the current pilates offer.

## Niche 8: Children's summer camps (campamentos)

### Takeaway
The Spanish youth-camp sector has about 729 companies with 177 million EUR revenue and low concentration (top 10 = 24.5%), and camp T-shirts are a norm; it is a seasonal, price-sensitive niche where printed cotton tees dominate.

### Cited Findings
- 729 companies in the "campamentos y ocio juvenil" cluster, 177.0 million EUR annual revenue, 1,161 direct jobs, average 327.8k EUR revenue and 14.2 employees per company; top 3 companies hold 17.2% and top 10 hold 24.5% of the market (Claris Data, from BORME/registry data, page updated Nov 2024) — [Claris Data](https://clarisdata.es/products/campamentos-y-ocio-juvenil-tier-3)
- Empresite/El Economista lists companies under "Campamentos", "Campamento de verano" and "Campamentos verano" activities (count not retrievable; page redirected) — [Empresite](https://empresite.eleconomista.es/Actividad/CAMPAMENTOS/)
- A "Colectivo Campamentos de Verano" exists with a public manifesto — [Colectivo Campamentos de Verano](http://colectivocampamentosdeverano.com/blog/manifiesto/)
- Camaloon delivers customised apparel/bags/drinkware in 6–7 days with bulk discounts, positioning for events and schools — [Camaloon](https://www.camaloon.com/)

### Inferences
- Camp merch is usually included in the price (a printed T-shirt) and ordered per season in the spring; a 4–7 week woven/embroidered lead time and premium price sit awkwardly against fast printed tees. Tumblers (reusable bottles) and tote bags could fit "premium camp" or international language camps. Low-to-medium priority.
- Decision maker: camp director; order size = campers per season (often 100–1,000).

### Gaps
- No verified camp T-shirt price points or specialised camp-merch printers (search budget exhausted).
- Language-immersion camp operators (ASEPROCE members) not researched.

## Niche 9: Scouts and youth movements

### Takeaway
Spanish scouting comprises roughly 900+ groups and 80,000+ members across ASDE (308 groups, 34,426 members), Scouts MSC (422 groups, ~28,000 members) and the Catalan FCEG (~200 groups, ~20,000); uniforms (camisa, pañoleta) are core, and each federation runs its own shop, which makes the group level the realistic buyer.

### Cited Findings
- ASDE Federación de Scouts-Exploradores de España: 308 groups across 15 autonomous communities plus Ceuta and Melilla; 34,426 socios; 7,652 volunteers (2023); 2024 expenses 1,312,350 EUR and income 1,257,523 EUR — [Fundación Lealtad, ASDE](https://fundacionlealtad.org/ong/asde-federacion-de-scouts-exploradores-de-espana/)
- ASDE: "más de 34.000 socios y socias en activo", 7,000+ volunteers, 305+ groups, 17 autonomous organisations; Scouts MSC (Catholic): 28,000+ people, ~12,000 families, 422 groups in 11 autonomous communities; FCEG (Catalonia): ~20,000 people, ~200 agrupaments (MEG, Escoltes Catalans, AEC); no single national census exists — [Jambore, Scouts en España](https://jambore.es/scout-espana/)
- Federación de Escultismo en España (WOSM member since 1978) had 58,744 scouts as of 2004; guiding umbrella 7,154 members (2003) — [Wikipedia EN, Scouting and Guiding in Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scouting_and_Guiding_in_Spain); federation history and "más de 30 000 socios / más de 300 grupos" for ASDE — [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federaci%C3%B3n_de_Asociaciones_de_Scouts_de_Espa%C3%B1a)
- ASDE Scouts de Andalucía: about 7,000 members in 75+ groups — [Scouts de Andalucía](https://scoutsdeandalucia.org/quienes-somos/)
- Directory of Spanish scout group websites — [SoyScout.es](http://www.soyscout.es/webs/webs.php?tip=todos&ver=si&cat=asde&orden=numero)

### Inferences
- Average group size ≈ 34,426 / 308 ≈ 112 members (ASDE) and ≈ 28,000 / 422 ≈ 66 (MSC), so a 50-unit minimum is at the edge for the smallest groups but fine for most. Group treasurers fund purchases via family fees and fundraising; embroidered group badges/pañoletas are the established custom, so embroidery quality is a selling point. Best fits: tote bags, tumblers (outdoor use); grip socks do not fit. New products: caps, buffs, embroidered patches.
- Federation shops (uniform shirts, pañoletas) already exist, so the federation level is likely served; the ~900 local groups are the open market. Buying moments: October start of the "ronda solar", summer camp (July), group anniversaries.

### Gaps
- Federation shop URLs (ASDE and MSC tiendas) could not be fetched; existence and range unverified.
- Europe: WOSM/WAGGGS European totals not researched.

## Cross-cutting: procurement rules, buying moments, decision makers, search phrases

### Takeaway
Public centres (19,399 schools, 50 universities, public conservatories) are governed by public-procurement law and cannot resell goods to families in the same way, which makes private/concertado schools, international schools, private universities, academies and youth groups the workable segments; the calendar clusters around September intake, June galas/graduations and December gifts.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Procurement: for public bodies, small purchases below the "contrato menor" threshold (commonly cited as 15,000 EUR excl. VAT for supplies/services under Ley 9/2017; unverified in this session) can be bought directly, but public schools generally do not resell to families, so the model fails there regardless of thresholds. AMPAs and student associations are private associations and are not subject to the law: they are the workaround for public-centre demand.
- Buying moments (inference from academic calendars): late August–October (start of course, welcome packs, new scout "ronda"), December (gifts), May–July (graduations, galas, camps), plus anniversaries (25/50/100 years) any time. For a 4–7 week lead time, mockup approval is needed by early July for September and by late October for December.
- Decision makers and typical order sizes (estimates): private school director/gerente or AMPA board (100–500 units); international school bursar/uniform shop (200–500); private university marketing or student association (50–300); academy owner (50–300); scout group coordinator/treasurer (50–150); camp director (100–1,000).
- Ranking for Mediterrano based on product fit + buyer structure + data available: (1) private dance academies, (2) language academies, (3) private/concertado schools via AMPA and school shop, (4) international schools, (5) private universities/student associations, (6) scout groups, (7) business schools (gift use), (8) camps, then music, choirs, colegios profesionales, sports, esports, churches.
