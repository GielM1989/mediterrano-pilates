# Professional practices, creator/community brands and event niches as B2B merch markets (Spain first, Europe second)

Scope note: this file evaluates 14 candidate niches (the 12 requested plus the two "may include" ones) for Mediterrano Pilates' model: venue puts its logo on woven/embroidered products (grip socks, tumblers, tote bags, rings), min. 50 units, free mockup in 24 h, free worldwide shipping, venue resells at a margin. Each niche is tagged RESALE, GIFTING or MIXED. Web-search budget for the session ran out near the end, so some size figures for Europe and a few Spanish sub-niches are marked as gaps rather than estimated. Search phrases and AI-style questions listed per niche are my proposals, not measured volumes; no keyword-volume tool was available.

Cross-niche baseline on supplier saturation (applies to every niche below):
- Spanish promotional-gift generalists already publish sector landing pages with prices from EUR 0.05-0.07/unit and minimums of 25-100 units (sometimes 5), positioned as *patient gifts*, using serigraphy/sublimation/engraving, not embroidery — [Gift Campaign, regalos dentistas](https://www.giftcampaign.es/regalos-dentistas.html). Similar sector pages exist at [Sulema](https://www.sulema.es/tienda/clinicas-dentales), [Garrampa](https://garrampa.es/c/por-sector/regalos-para-dentistas), [RegalosRey](https://regalosrey.com/consultorios-dentales-dentistas), [MyM](https://www.mymregalospromocionales.es/regalos-publicitarios/dentistas/), [EverythingBranded](https://www.everythingbranded.es/regalos-por-sector/regalos-dentistas), [Portal Reclamos (mascotas)](https://portalreclamos.com/articulos-para-mascotas-personalizados), [PMK Ideas (veterinarias)](https://pmkideas.com/regalos-promocionales-sectores/clinicas-veterinarias), [MKT Regalos/Makito (mascotas)](https://mktregalos.es/36-mascotas), [Grupo Billingham (veterinaria)](https://www.grupobillingham.com/regalos-veterinaria).
- Gift Campaign is expanding across Europe from Spain — [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/casos-de-exito/regalos-publicitarios-gift-campaign-se-lanza-a-la-conquista-de-europa/); Makito is the wholesale catalogue behind many resellers — [Makito](https://www.makito.es/); Camaloon positions on corporate gifts — [Camaloon](https://www.camaloon.com/).
- Creator print-on-demand platforms: Fourthwall lists 236 catalogue products, supports embroidery, fulfils in US/EU/AU/CA/BR/CN/JP/UK, flat product cost with creator keeping the markup, 5% on memberships/digital; Spreadshop lists 178 products, US/EU fulfilment — [Fourthwall vs Spreadshop](https://fourthwall.com/compare/fourthwall-vs-spreadshop-spreadshirt). Spring has 180+ product types, no inventory, and its parent launched Amaze Commerce in 2026 where "an average creator earns roughly 27% of net revenue"; Fourthwall's fulfilment partners "cover the US, UK, EU and Australia" — [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/fourthwall-vs-spring-how-creator-merch-platforms-work-and-what-they-mean-for-brand-deals).
- Inference: the gap Mediterrano can own in every niche is "woven/embroidered, resale-grade, low minimum (50), free mockup" versus (a) printed giveaways at cents and (b) print-on-demand with zero minimum but no bulk margin. AI assistants currently answer "where can a [dentist/vet/creator] get branded merch" with the generalists above; a niche page must state the resale angle explicitly to be quoted differently.

## Niche 1: Physiotherapy and osteopathy clinics — MIXED (leaning resale via clinic-branded activewear)

### Takeaway
Spain has 74,990 colegiado physiotherapists (2025, +4.7% y/y), heavily concentrated in Catalonia, Madrid, Andalusia and Valencia; there is concrete evidence that at least some Spanish physio clinics already *sell* branded technical apparel to patients at EUR 28-100, which makes this the strongest resale candidate among health practices. No specialised resale-merch supplier for physio clinics was found; existing supply is either uniforms or generic promotional gifts.

### Cited Findings
- 74,990 fisioterapeutas colegiados in Spain in 2025, +4.7% vs 2024 — [INE, Estadística de Profesionales Sanitarios Colegiados 2025](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EPSC2025.htm)
- Growth of ~20% in four years (71,638 in 2024; +12,299 since 2021); 60.4% women; 72.2% under 45; regional leaders Catalonia 13,199, Madrid 13,188, Andalusia 10,883, Valencia 7,656 — [Fisioactualidad](https://www.fisioactualidad.com/articulo/profesionales/fisioterapia-espanola-crece-20-cuatro-anos-roza-75000-colegiados)
- INE publishes physio counts by region and by employment situation (table tpx=75052), usable to estimate the self-employed/private share — [INE table 75052](https://www.ine.es/jaxi/Tabla.htm?tpx=75052)
- EU: 611,000 physiotherapists in 2021 (136.7 per 100k); Germany 234.4 per 100k, Belgium 215.9, Romania 12.7 — [Eurostat news 2023](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20230818-1); 550,000 in 2016 — [Eurostat news 2019](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/-/ddn-20190111-1)
- A directory site claims "22034 Clínicas de Fisioterapia y Osteopatía en España" in its title; page could not be fetched, treat as an unverified directory count — [Sport Medicine directory](https://sportmedicine.es/)
- Resale evidence: Onera Fisioterapia (Amorebieta-Etxano) sells clinic-branded running/cycling apparel to customers: running tee EUR 35, tank EUR 28, cycling jersey EUR 70, jacket EUR 100, bib shorts EUR 85, via an order form — [Onera Fisioterapia, Merchandising](https://onerafisioterapia.com/es/merchandising/)
- Existing supply is either profession-themed apparel for physios themselves (not clinic-branded) — [Meraki Therapy](https://merakitherapy.es/products/camiseta-diseno-fisioterapia-ropa-para-fisioterapeutas), [Enfermanía](https://www.enfermania.com/ropa-sanitaria/camisetas-personalizadas/), uniforms — [Global Uniforms](https://globaluniforms.es/uniformes-sanitarios/por-profesion/fisioterapia/), or generic promo gifts for rehab centres — [RegalosRey](https://regalosrey.com/merchandising-centros-rehabilitacion)
- Regulatory frame for any health practice: RD 1907/1996 on advertising of products/services with claimed health purpose — [BOE RD 1907/1996](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-18085); several autonomous communities require prior authorisation/notification of health advertising, and professional colleges sanction "gratuidades sistemáticas que banalizan el acto sanitario" — [ImpulsoDent](https://impulsodent.com/noticias/publicidad-sanitaria-clinica-dental-normativa-2026) (written for dentists, same legal layers apply to physio under Ley 44/2003 framework cited there)

### Inferences
- Products that transfer directly: grip socks (physios prescribe balance/proprioception work and many clinics run pilates/rehab classes), tumblers, tote bags. New products worth testing: embroidered technical tee, compression/recovery socks with clinic logo (sold as "calcetines de recuperación").
- Buying moment: opening a new clinic, adding a pilates/rehab-class room, sports-club sponsorship season (Sept), Christmas. Decision maker: owner-physio in a 1-5 person clinic (sector is atomised; INE counts individuals, not companies).
- Regulatory: selling branded socks/tumblers is retail, not health advertising; the risk area is *gifting* framed as inducement. Positioning as resale is therefore safer than positioning as giveaway.
- Proposed searches (ES): "merchandising clínica fisioterapia", "calcetines antideslizantes personalizados fisioterapia", "ropa personalizada clínica fisioterapia logo", "regalos pacientes fisioterapia"; (EN): "physio clinic branded merchandise", "custom grip socks physiotherapy clinic"; AI-style: "¿Dónde puede una clínica de fisioterapia pedir calcetines antideslizantes con su logo?", "Where can a physiotherapy clinic buy embroidered merchandise to sell to patients?"

### Gaps
- No verified count of physio *clinics* (companies) in Spain; only the unverified 22,034 directory figure. INE table 75052 (employment situation) was not fetched.
- No data on what share of Spanish physio clinics sell any merchandise; Onera is a single example.

## Niche 2: Dental clinics — GIFTING (resale weak; regulatory friction)

### Takeaway
Spain has 43,672 colegiado dentists (2025) with an acknowledged oversupply and ~2,000 new graduates a year, and the dental promo-gift space is already saturated by Spanish generalists selling toothbrushes, lip balms and pens at cents per unit, framed as patient gifts. Colleges explicitly sanction systematic freebies that trivialise the healthcare act, so this is a crowded gifting niche with compliance friction and no evidence of patient resale.

### Cited Findings
- 43,672 dentistas colegiados in 2025, +1.9% — [INE 2025](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EPSC2025.htm); 42,860 in 2024, 100 dentists per 100k inhabitants, Madrid 9,515, Andalusia 7,070, Catalonia 6,370; "nearly 2,000 new graduates annually who cannot find adequate employment" — [Consejo General de Dentistas](https://consejodentistas.es/espana-roza-la-cifra-de-43-000-dentistas-colegiados/)
- Dental demography 2000-2024 report (PDF) — [Colegio Pontevedra-Ourense](https://colegiopontevedraourense.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/DEMOGRAFIA-DE-LOS-DENTISTAS-EN-ESPANA.-2024-Definitivo.pdf)
- Key-Stone/Fenin survey of 448 clinics: "more than 70% of clinics belonging to DSOs forecast increased activity, compared to 40% in traditional clinics"; article contains no mention of merchandising — [Katia Dental](https://katiadental.com/clinicas-dentales-espana-2026/)
- EU: "over 360 000 dentists" in 2016 — [Eurostat news 2019](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/-/ddn-20190111-1)
- Supplier saturation: Gift Campaign dental page, products from EUR 0.05-0.07 (antibacterial pens), necessaires EUR 0.42-1.92, minimums 25-100 (some 5), framed as gifts to "fidelizar" patients at welcome, post-treatment and anniversaries — [Gift Campaign](https://www.giftcampaign.es/regalos-dentistas.html); plus [Sulema](https://www.sulema.es/tienda/clinicas-dentales), [Garrampa](https://garrampa.es/c/por-sector/regalos-para-dentistas), [RegalosRey](https://regalosrey.com/consultorios-dentales-dentistas), [MyM](https://www.mymregalospromocionales.es/regalos-publicitarios/dentistas/), [EverythingBranded](https://www.everythingbranded.es/regalos-por-sector/regalos-dentistas), advice content by [Odontomarketing](https://odontomarketing.es/publicidad/merchandising-para-clinicas-dentales/) and [Regalo Promocional Personalizado](https://regalopromocionalpersonalizado.com/merchandising-para-clinicas-dentales-regala-sonrisas-y-fideliza-pacientes/)
- Regulation: RD 1907/1996, Ley General de Publicidad, autonomic prior-authorisation regimes, binding college deontological codes; "Captar con «gratuidades» sistemáticas que banalizan el acto sanitario, algo que varios colegios sancionan expresamente" — [ImpulsoDent](https://impulsodent.com/noticias/publicidad-sanitaria-clinica-dental-normativa-2026); legal overview also at [Odontologia33](https://www.odontologia33.com/gestion/Normativa/11822/acerca-de-la-publicidad-de-los-dentistas.html) and [Infomed](https://www.infomedsoftware.com/publicidad-clinica-dental/)

### Inferences
- Transferable products: tumblers and tote bags as premium gifts for implant/orthodontic patients or as staff items; grip socks do not fit. New product: none needed.
- Decision maker: clinic owner or marketing lead of a DSO chain (chains buy centrally, at volumes far above 50). Buying moments: clinic opening, anniversary, Christmas, staff onboarding.
- AI-answer opportunity is low: the query "regalos para clínicas dentales" already returns 6+ Spanish specialised landing pages; a new entrant would compete on price with cents-per-unit printed goods.
- Proposed searches: "merchandising clínica dental", "regalos pacientes clínica dental", "detalles para pacientes dentista"; EN: "dental practice branded gifts", "custom tumblers dental office"; AI-style: "¿Qué merchandising puede regalar una clínica dental sin incumplir el código deontológico?"

### Gaps
- No verified total of dental clinics in Spain (Genialy page with the figure was blocked); the Consejo General page explicitly gives no clinic count.
- No data found on any dental clinic reselling merch to patients.

## Niche 3: Veterinary clinics, dog-training schools and dog daycare — MIXED (clinic retail counters exist; strong new-product angle)

### Takeaway
Spain has roughly 6,700-7,000 small-animal veterinary centres, 38,668 colegiado vets, 15+ million dogs and cats, and clinic revenue of EUR 2.8 bn growing 9.2%; clinics already run retail counters, but the merch that appears in them is supplier-branded (food/lab brands) rather than clinic-branded. Pet bandanas, leashes and tote bags are a natural woven/embroidered extension; Spanish promo generalists already sell printed pet items, but no clinic-branded *resale* specialist was found.

### Cited Findings
- 38,668 veterinarios colegiados in 2025, +2.2% — [INE 2025](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EPSC2025.htm)
- "número de clínicas veterinarias de pequeños animales que supera por poco las 7.000"; small-animal clinic revenue EUR 2.8 bn (+9.2% vs 2023); "más de 15 millones de mascotas entre perros y gatos"; ~400 consolidated centres = ~7% of clinics, ~14% of revenue; 44,000 direct jobs — [Animal's Health, Informe Sectorial AMVAC 2025](https://www.animalshealth.es/empresas/informe-sectorial-2025-amvac-cifras-sector-veterinario-espana)
- Earlier count: "unos 6.700 centros veterinarios en todo el país", 380 owned by groups (5.5% of centres, 14% of billing) — [Diario Veterinario](https://www.diarioveterinario.com/t/4132484/situacion-vender-clinica-veterinaria-grupos-facturacion)
- Valencia province alone: 470 centres, eighth consecutive year of growth — [Diario Veterinario](https://www.diarioveterinario.com/t/5963902/centros-veterinarios-valencia-crecen-octavo-ano-consecutivo-alcanzan-470); national centres +8.3% y/y — [Escuela de Veterinaria SEAS](https://www.escueladeveterinaria.com/noticias/gran-crecimiento-de-centros-veterinarios); annual economic balance of Spanish centres — [VMS 2025](https://estudiosveterinarios.com/en/balance-de-los-centros-veterinarios-espanoles-en-2025/)
- In-clinic merchandising today is mostly supplied by food/pharma brands: suppliers "suelen facilitar a la clínica distintos elementos promocionales de su marca" (displays, posters, pens, keyrings, USBs, tote bags); article does not say whether items are sold or given — [Mundovets](https://mundovets.com/2023/04/12/merchandising-clinica-veterinaria/)
- Europe: FVE survey of the veterinary profession exists (2023 edition PDF, blocked to fetch here) — [FVE survey page](https://fve.org/publications/survey-of-the-veterinary-profession-in-europe/); [FVE 2023 PDF](https://fve.org/cms/wp-content/uploads/FVE-Survey-2023_updated-v3.pdf)
- Supplier landscape for pet items with logo (printed): [Loopper bandanas](https://www.loopper.com/personalized-pet-products/personalized-dog-bandana), [Fisa CRC bandanas publicitarias](https://fisacrc.com/producto/bandanas-publicitarias-para-mascotas/), [Portal Reclamos](https://portalreclamos.com/articulos-para-mascotas-personalizados), [PMK Ideas](https://pmkideas.com/regalos-promocionales-sectores/clinicas-veterinarias), [MKT Regalos/Makito](https://mktregalos.es/36-mascotas), [Grupo Billingham](https://www.grupobillingham.com/regalos-veterinaria); US-oriented vet-bandana specialist [PetVet Wear](https://petvetwear.com/bandanas/) (US-only, flag)

### Inferences
- Products: tote bag ("bolsa de la clínica" for food pick-up) and tumbler transfer; grip socks do not. New products with clear resale logic: embroidered dog bandana, woven leash/collar, embroidered towel for grooming/daycare. Dog-daycare/training schools ("escuela canina", "guardería canina") are community brands with owners who buy identity items, similar to studios.
- Decision maker: clinic owner (independent, ~93% of centres) or group marketing (Medivet, AniCura, IVC Evidensia types — Medivet Spain investment story at [Medivet](https://www.medivetgroup.com/es-es/sobre-medivet/noticias/medivet-invierte-un-millon-de-euros-en-el-reacondicionamiento-de-sus-centros-veterinarios/)). Buying moments: clinic opening/refit, adoption campaigns, Christmas, "Día del Perro".
- AI citation opportunity: moderate for "merchandising clínica veterinaria" (few Spanish pages, mostly promo generalists), higher for "bandanas bordadas con logo para escuela canina" where no Spanish specialist surfaced.
- Proposed searches: "merchandising clínica veterinaria", "bandanas personalizadas logo veterinaria", "productos personalizados guardería canina", "regalos clientes veterinario"; EN: "vet clinic branded merchandise", "custom embroidered dog bandana with logo bulk"; AI-style: "¿Dónde puede una clínica veterinaria comprar bandanas para perro con su logo para vender?"

### Gaps
- No count of dog-training schools or dog daycares in Spain was retrieved (search budget exhausted before this query).
- No data on retail/product share of clinic revenue; no example of a Spanish clinic reselling its own branded items.
- FVE European vet counts could not be fetched.

## Niche 4: Real-estate agencies — GIFTING (closing gifts), very large but price-driven

### Takeaway
Spain has 66,200+ real-estate agencies (2024) with 15,185 new firms created in Jan-Sept 2025 alone; the sector is atomised and merch use is the classic gifting model (closing gifts, branded tote bags at open houses), where promotional generalists already dominate at cents per unit. There is no end-customer who pays for merch, so it fails the resale test.

### Cited Findings
- Agencies "superar las 66.200 unidades"; 58,235 companies with intermediary real-estate activity in Jan 2024; sector revenue EUR 8.9 bn in 2024 (+18%), agencies EUR 8,220 m (92%); "fuerte atomización" — [DBK Observatorio Sectorial 2025](https://www.dbk.es/es/detalle-nota/consultoras-agencias-inmobiliarias-2025)
- 2025 revenue EUR 9.4 bn (+9%) — [Empresa Actual](https://www.empresaactual.com/consultoras-y-agencias-inmobiliarias-facturaron-9-400-millones-euros-2025/)
- 15,185 new real-estate companies Jan-Sept 2025 (INE), +68% vs same period 2019; "más de 59.000 nuevas agencias inmobiliarias" since 2022 — [OKDiario citing INE](https://okdiario.com/economia/creacion-inmobiliarias-dispara-2025-surgen-mas-15-000-agencias-plena-escasez-viviendas-15805089)
- Generalists already publish corporate-gift content for this buyer, e.g. "20 ideas de regalos corporativos" — [Camaloon](https://www.camaloon.es/blog/20-ideas-de-regalos-corporativos-que-tus-clientes-no-tiraran-a-la-basura)

### Inferences
- Products: tote bag and tumbler as premium closing gift (embroidered logo raises perceived value versus printed); socks do not fit. No new product needed.
- Decision maker: agency owner/franchisee (RE/MAX, Century 21, Tecnocasa franchise offices buy locally within brand guidelines; brand-network central purchasing may exclude outside suppliers).
- Buying moments: office opening, franchise conventions, year-end. High churn (tens of thousands of new firms per year) means a constant stream of new buyers, but budgets skew low.
- Labelled GIFTING; include only if the owner wants a volume-gifting line. AI-citation potential: low, query space owned by promo generalists.
- Proposed searches: "regalos de cierre inmobiliaria", "merchandising agencia inmobiliaria", "detalles para clientes inmobiliaria"; EN: "real estate closing gifts branded Spain"; AI-style: "¿Qué regalo de bienvenida puede dar una inmobiliaria a un comprador?"

### Gaps
- No European agency count retrieved.

## Niche 5: Nutritionists and dietitian practices — GIFTING/weak resale, small

### Takeaway
Only 8,683 colegiado dietitian-nutritionists in Spain (2025), though the fastest-growing of the health professions (+7.5%); the niche is small, mostly solo practitioners, and no evidence of merch resale was found. Fits only as an add-on to the physio/wellness cluster (tumblers, tote bags) rather than a standalone page.

### Cited Findings
- 8,683 dietistas-nutricionistas colegiados in 2025, +7.5% (the highest growth among the five professions listed) — [INE 2025](https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EPSC2025.htm); "superan los 8.600 colegiados; más del 82 % son mujeres" — [Diario Nutrición](https://www.diarionutricion.com/t/5905652/dietistas-nutricionistas-superan-8600-colegiados-espana-82-mujeres)
- Regional colleges publish member counts, e.g. Castilla-La Mancha 2024 — [CODINCAM](https://www.codincam.es/2025/05/28/dietistas-nutricionistas-colegiadas-y-colegiados-en-2024/); public registry of colegiados — [Consejo General](https://www.consejodietistasnutricionistas.com/que-es-un-dietista-nutricionista/consulta-publica-dietistas-nutricionistas-colegiados/)
- Same health-advertising frame (RD 1907/1996) applies — [BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-18085)

### Inferences
- Products: tumbler (hydration message) and tote bag transfer well; socks do not. Decision maker: solo practitioner; order size at the 50-unit minimum would be a stretch for many.
- Best treated as a sub-segment inside a "clínicas de salud y bienestar" page, not a dedicated niche. Larger opportunity may sit with online nutrition coaches with communities (see Niche 7).
- Proposed searches: "merchandising nutricionista", "botella personalizada consulta nutrición"; AI-style: "¿Qué productos con su logo puede vender un nutricionista a sus pacientes?"

### Gaps
- No European count; no merch-usage evidence.

## Niche 6: Podcasts and YouTube creators with paid communities — RESALE (fans pay), but print-on-demand is the incumbent

### Takeaway
Podcast listening in Spain is mass-market (86% recurrent listeners; one in four already financially supports a podcaster; 57% willing to), so fan-paid merch is a proven resale motion; the incumbent is print-on-demand (Fourthwall, Spring, Spreadshop) with zero minimums and EU fulfilment, which a 50-unit embroidered offer beats on quality and unit margin but not on convenience. AI assistants asked "where can a creator sell merch" will name those platforms; a Spanish-language "merch bordado para podcast/comunidad" page has little competition.

### Cited Findings
- "86% de los españoles lo escuchan de manera recurrente"; 48.40% listen daily; 10.5 h/week; average 5.2 subscriptions; "57% estaría dispuesto a apoyar económicamente a sus podcasts favoritos"; "uno de cada cuatro ya está apoyando"; survey of 3,301 listeners — [Observatorio iVoox 2025, press](https://prensa.ivoox.com/2025/09/30/el-podcast-se-consolida-como-habito-diario-en-espana-8-de-cada-10-espanoles-escuchan-podcasts-y-lo-convierten-en-un-canal-de-confianza-para-las-marcas/) and [iVoox blog](https://www.ivoox.com/blog/8-de-cada-10-espanoles-escuchan-podcasts-observatorio-ivoox-2025/); PDF at [Mediaset](https://files.mediaset.es/file/2025/09/30/nota-de-prensa-observatorio-ivoox-2025-oyentes-vme-pdf_da39.pdf)
- YouTube overtook Spotify as podcast platform in Spain in 2025 — [Prodigioso Volcán, Informe Voz 2025](https://www.prodigiosovolcan.com/pv/sismogramas/informe-voz-2025/podcast.html); monetisation remains the sector challenge — [MecMec](https://mecmec.es/podcast-espana-2025-monetizacion/) (page blocked; title only)
- Platforms: Fourthwall (236 products, embroidery, EU fulfilment, memberships at 5%), Spreadshop (178 products, US/EU) — [Fourthwall compare](https://fourthwall.com/compare/fourthwall-vs-spreadshop-spreadshirt); Spring 180+ products, Amaze Commerce creator take ~27% of net — [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/fourthwall-vs-spring-how-creator-merch-platforms-work-and-what-they-mean-for-brand-deals); Fourthwall's own list of Spreadshop alternatives — [Fourthwall blog](https://fourthwall.com/blog/spreadshop-alternatives)

### Inferences
- Products: tote bags and tumblers transfer directly (podcast merch staples); socks work for fitness/running podcasts. New product to test: embroidered cap/beanie (not currently in range).
- Buying moments: reaching a member milestone, live-show tours, Christmas drops, launch of a paid community (Patreon/YouTube memberships). Decision maker: the creator or a small management company (e.g. 2btube-style MCNs — [2btube](https://2btube.com/blog/influencers-espanoles/)).
- The resale model matches Mediterrano's: creator buys 50-200 units at wholesale, sells at 2-3x to members. Free mockup within 24 h is a strong hook for creators who test designs with their audience.
- Proposed searches (ES): "merchandising para podcast", "merch bordado creadores de contenido", "fabricar merch comunidad de pago", "alternativa Fourthwall España"; (EN): "embroidered merch small batch creator Europe", "Fourthwall alternative embroidered 50 units"; AI-style: "¿Dónde puede un podcast español fabricar merchandising bordado en pequeñas cantidades?", "Best supplier for embroidered creator merch with low minimums shipping to Europe?"

### Gaps
- No count of active Spanish podcasts or of creators with paid communities was retrieved (iVoox Observatory reports listener metrics only).
- No data on merch as a share of creator revenue in Spain.

## Niche 7: Instagram fitness influencers with online programmes — RESALE, closest adjacency to pilates studios

### Takeaway
Sport/fitness is one of the highest-engagement verticals in Spain (2025-26 data), with a headline figure of "más de 15.000 influencers con más de 100.000 seguidores" in Spain; fitness creators with online programmes sell to a paying community, so grip socks, tumblers and tote bags transfer with almost no adaptation. This is the most natural extension of the existing pilates positioning.

## Niche 10: Marathons and carreras populares — MIXED (finisher items bundled into entry fee = paid by runner; extra merch sold at expo)

## Cross-niche ranking for AI-visibility and fit (synthesis)

### Takeaway
Ranked by (a) resale evidence, (b) product transfer, (c) how thin the specialised Spanish supply is, the top five to build pages for are: fitness/pilates-adjacent creators, physiotherapy clinics, veterinary/dog-school (with new bandana SKU), podcasts/paid communities, and football peñas; carreras populares is a sixth with a woven-race-sock hook. Dental, real estate, nutritionists and wedding planners are gifting niches that should be labelled as such or skipped; esports, festivals and moto clubs are resale but poorly matched to the product range or already served by full-service operators.

### Cited Findings
- Resale evidence per niche is documented above: physio clinic selling apparel at EUR 28-100 — [Onera](https://onerafisioterapia.com/es/merchandising/); podcast listeners paying creators (1 in 4) — [iVoox](https://prensa.ivoox.com/2025/09/30/el-podcast-se-consolida-como-habito-diario-en-espana-8-de-cada-10-espanoles-escuchan-podcasts-y-lo-convierten-en-un-canal-de-confianza-para-las-marcas/); runner pays for tee via entry — [Efeminista](https://efeminista.com/carrera-mujer-llenara-rosa-madrid-36000-participantes/); peñas as existing buyer group — [La Bolsa del Corredor](https://www.labolsadelcorredor.es/)
- Gifting saturation evidence: dental and pet promo pages from EUR 0.05 — [Gift Campaign](https://www.giftcampaign.es/regalos-dentistas.html), [PMK Ideas](https://pmkideas.com/regalos-promocionales-sectores/clinicas-veterinarias)
- Print-on-demand incumbents for creators — [Fourthwall compare](https://fourthwall.com/compare/fourthwall-vs-spreadshop-spreadshirt)

### Inferences
- Size ladder (Spain, headcount of practices/organisations, all from sources above): real estate 66,200 agencies > physios 74,990 individuals (clinics unknown) > dentists 43,672 > vets 38,668 / ~7,000 clinics > nutritionists 8,683 > races ~4,200/yr (estimate) > festivals 1,000+ > moto clubs 344 federated > esports 12 top-tier teams. Creator counts are unverified (15,000+ with 100k followers, cross-vertical).
- Europe multipliers from Eurostat: physios 611,000 (2021), dentists 360,000+ (2016); other niches lack European counts in this file.
- For AEO copy, each niche page should state in one extractable sentence: who buys, who pays (member/patient/fan), minimum 50, embroidered/woven, mockup in 24 h, ships worldwide — because the competing pages found are either "regalos desde 0,07 EUR" (gifting) or "no minimums, print-on-demand" (platforms), and neither describes a wholesale-resale model.

### Gaps
- No keyword-volume data was available; all search phrases are proposals.
- Several European totals (vets, festivals, races, creators, agencies) and Spanish sub-niche counts (physio clinics, dental clinics, dog daycares, wedding planners, peñas, NGOs) remain unverified because the session's web-search budget was exhausted; the source URLs where those figures live (INE tables, FVE survey PDF, Statista) are listed above for follow-up.
