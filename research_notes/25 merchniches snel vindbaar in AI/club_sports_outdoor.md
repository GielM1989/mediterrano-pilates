# Club sports and outdoor-community niches (excl. padel) as B2B markets for branded resale merchandise, Spain and Europe

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: 12 candidate niches evaluated for the "venue puts its logo on product, members buy it" model used by Mediterrano Pilates (woven/embroidered logo, min. 50 pairs, free 24 h mockup, free worldwide shipping). Padel is excluded per assignment. All Spanish market-size figures below come from one primary source unless stated otherwise: the CSD table "Licencias y Clubes 2024" (published 2025-12) — [CSD PDF](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf), index page [CSD Licencias](https://www.csd.gob.es/en/federations-and-associations/spanish-sports-federations/licenses). Method note: the session's web-search budget was exhausted after ~20 searches; remaining work used direct page fetches of known URLs. Several supplier and Europe-level questions are therefore logged as gaps rather than guessed.

## Cross-cutting: Spanish market size per federation (CSD 2024) and Europe-level anchors

### Takeaway
Spain had 4,315,809 federated licences and 78,690 federated clubs in 2024. Among the candidate niches, cycling (3,536 clubs), mountain/climbing (2,994), basketball (3,074), athletics (1,330), tennis (1,176), triathlon (870), swimming (800) and hípica (827) have the most club entities; golf has few clubs (597) but the most licences (305,603) per club.

### Cited Findings
- Grand total 2024: 4,315,809 licences, 78,690 clubs — [CSD Licencias y Clubes 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf)
- Per federation 2024 (licences / clubs): Tenis 96,413 / 1,176; Golf 305,603 / 597; Atletismo 89,522 / 1,330; Ciclismo 68,720 / 3,536; Triatlón 24,446 / 870; Natación 70,146 / 800; Surf 6,065 / 290; Deportes de Invierno 3,695 / 202; Montaña y Escalada 287,072 / 2,994; Vela 19,566 / 144; Remo 14,771 / 226; Piragüismo 9,571 / 320; Fútbol 1,260,556 / 30,862; Baloncesto 440,427 / 3,074; Hípica 61,294 / 827; Pádel 109,040 / 1,326 (excluded niche, for reference); Salvamento y Socorrismo 15,957 / 90; Balonmano 102,933 / 871; Voleibol 121,711 / 774 — [CSD Licencias y Clubes 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf)
- Year-on-year 2024 (Anuario de Estadísticas Deportivas 2025): total licences −0.2 %; clubs +1,546 vs 2023; tennis +5.9 %; golf +2.8 %; basketball +6.9 %; athletics ~0 %; mountain/climbing ~0 %; 75.1 % of licences male — [Infobae/EFE summary of Anuario 2025](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2025/05/30/el-padel-el-que-mas-crece-en-numero-de-federados-78-entre-los-deportes-mas-populares/)
- 2023 baseline: 4,271,300 licences, 77,038 clubs (avg 55.4 licences/club); surfing was 1.8 % of licences in 2023 (≈77,000 by arithmetic on the total) versus 6,065 printed for 2024 — [dbSportpress 2023](https://dbsport.press/estadistica-del-deporte-federado-ano-2023/); [Servimedia 2023](https://www.servimedia.es/noticias/espana-tiene-mas-4-2-millones-deportistas-federados-nuevo-record-historico/1410171195); 2024 figure from [CSD 2024 PDF](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf). The two years are not comparable for surf; treat the 2024 surf licence figure with caution (likely a change in what FESURFING counts as a licence). Club count (290) is the more useful figure.
- Europe golf: ~4.72 million registered golfers in 2024 (+1 %) and, for Spain, 305,603 registered golfers and 497 nine-or-eighteen-hole facilities — [R&A/EGA European Golf Participation Report 2024 (PDF)](https://www.ega-golf.ch/sites/ega/files/2025-11/2025_european-golf-participation-report_2024-estimates%20(2).pdf); 2025 edition: >4.9 million registered golfers (+3 % vs 2024, +13 % since 2020), England 835,000+, Germany 695,600, Sweden 588,100, France 446,500, Netherlands 439,700, 478,000+ registered juniors — [EGA news, report published June 2026](https://www.ega-golf.ch/content/new-participation-report-reveals-continued-growth-golf-across-europe)
- Global tennis: just under 106 million players in 199 countries, 698,034 courts (91.2 % outdoor, 25.7 % clay), +25.6 % players since 2019; Great Britain has the highest share of population playing (13.4 %) — [ITF Global Tennis Report 2024](https://www.itftennis.com/en/news-and-media/articles/itf-global-tennis-report-participation-hits-106-million-in-five-years/)

### Inferences
- Licences per club is a proxy for how many potential merch buyers each B2B account brings: golf ≈ 512/club, hípica ≈ 74, tennis ≈ 82, swimming ≈ 88, basketball ≈ 143, athletics ≈ 67, triathlon ≈ 28, cycling ≈ 19, mountain ≈ 96 (arithmetic on CSD 2024 figures). A 50-pair minimum is easy for golf, tennis, swimming and basketball clubs, and hard for the median cycling or triathlon club unless the club sells across seasons.
- Federated club counts undercount the real B2B universe in running (informal run crews), surf/kite/SUP (commercial schools, not clubs), ski (commercial schools) and pickleball (courts inside tennis/padel clubs). Federated counts overcount in mountain/climbing (many licences are insurance-driven).

### Gaps
- No Europe-wide club counts were found for tennis, athletics/running, cycling, triathlon, swimming, sailing, rowing or equestrian in this session (search budget exhausted before European federation reports could be fetched). Golf and pickleball are the only niches with Europe-level numbers here.
- The CSD table gives clubs per federation but not how many of those clubs run a shop or sell merchandise.

## Niche 1: Tennis clubs

### Takeaway
1,176 federated clubs and 96,413 licences in Spain (2024, +5.9 %), plus a global sport of ~106 million players; tennis clubs are member-social clubs with a strong club-identity culture, and pickleball is being layered onto the same clubs, which makes tennis the natural first racket niche after padel is excluded.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: 96,413 licences, 1,176 clubs — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf); licences +5.9 % vs 2023 — [Infobae/EFE](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2025/05/30/el-padel-el-que-mas-crece-en-numero-de-federados-78-entre-los-deportes-mas-populares/)
- RFET licence framework and regulations — [RFET Licencias](https://www.rfet.es/es/licencias.html); [Reglamento de licencias RFET (PDF)](https://www.rfet.es/descargar/file-rfet-gobierno-reglamentos/26_Documento/reglamento-de-licencias.pdf)
- Global: ~106 million players, 698,034 courts — [ITF Global Tennis Report 2024](https://www.itftennis.com/en/news-and-media/articles/itf-global-tennis-report-participation-hits-106-million-in-five-years/)
- Pickleball is integrated into the RFET structure and the RFET 2025/2026 regulations include a specific pickleball licence; clubs such as Emilio Sánchez Academy (El Prat) added dedicated pickleball courts — [Libertad Digital, 2026-03-03](https://www.libertaddigital.com/deportes/mas-deporte/2026-03-03/los-clubes-se-adaptan-al-pickleball-el-nuevo-deporte-de-raqueta-de-moda-7368244/); [Pickleballovers](https://www.pickleballovers.com/p/boom-crecimiento-pickleball-espana)
- Generalist teamwear supplier owayo lists 16 sports on its Spanish FAQ and tennis is NOT among them; single-unit orders are possible, standard production 3–4 weeks — [owayo FAQ](https://www.owayo.es/futbol/faq-es.htm)
- Decathlon's club-kit platform lists 10+ sports (triathlon, cycling, football, basketball, rugby, handball, volleyball, athletics, beach volley, swimming), i.e. tennis is not a listed sport there either; it offers "Mi Tienda Club" member storefronts and 20–45 working-day lead times — [Decathlon Club](https://equipacion.decathlon.es/)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: tumblers and tote bags transfer directly (clubhouse/terrace culture); socks transfer if made as a crew/quarter sports sock rather than a grip sock (tennis socks are a visible, high-turnover item); rings do not transfer. New items that fit the club-shop model: caps/visors, towels, wristbands/sweatbands, polo shirts (embroidered), racket-bag tags.
- Saturation: low for tennis-specific club merch. The big teamwear platforms (owayo, Decathlon Club) do not list tennis, and search results for "ropa personalizada" were dominated by running, cycling and football. The evidence base for merch culture in Spanish tennis clubs is thin in this session (no club shop page could be fetched), so treat "strong merch culture" as plausible, not proven.
- Decision maker: club board (junta directiva) or club manager/gerente; for club-owned pro shops, the shop manager. Tennis clubs are large enough (≈82 licences/club, many with non-federated social members) to absorb 50-unit minimums.
- Buying moments (inference, no source): September season start and social tournaments/club championships; Christmas for gift items.

### Gaps
- No Spanish tennis club shop page was fetched to prove member purchasing of club-logo items (RCTB search could not run; budget exhausted).
- No Europe-wide count of tennis clubs (Tennis Europe report not fetched).

## Niche 2: Golf clubs

### Takeaway
Golf is the niche with the strongest proven "logo merchandise resold to members and visitors" culture (the pro shop) and the highest licences per club (305,603 licences / 597 clubs / 497 courses in Spain; 4.7–4.9 million registered golfers in Europe), but it is also served by an established corporate/pro-shop supply chain (Titleist, Callaway, TaylorMade via distributors such as Corporate Golf), so entry must be on a product gap (e.g. woven socks, tumblers) rather than on apparel.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: 305,603 licences, 597 clubs — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf); 497 nine/eighteen-hole facilities, +2 % registered golfers — [EGA report 2024 PDF](https://www.ega-golf.ch/sites/ega/files/2025-11/2025_european-golf-participation-report_2024-estimates%20(2).pdf)
- Europe: ~4.72 million registered golfers 2024; >4.9 million 2025; top countries England, Germany, Sweden, France, Netherlands — [EGA PDF](https://www.ega-golf.ch/sites/ega/files/2025-11/2025_european-golf-participation-report_2024-estimates%20(2).pdf); [EGA news](https://www.ega-golf.ch/content/new-participation-report-reveals-continued-growth-golf-across-europe)
- Existing Spanish supplier for pro shops and tournaments: Corporate Golf (corporategolf.es) sells logo'd Titleist caps, balls, towels, gloves, umbrellas, welcome packs, headcovers, bags; techniques include estampación, vinilo, sublimación, láser, serigrafía, bordado; it is a "distribuidor corporate" for Titleist, TaylorMade, Callaway, Srixon, Honma, with a dedicated "Campos y Pro-shops" segment; no minimums published — [Corporate Golf merchandising](https://www.corporategolf.es/merchandising-golf); [Corporate Golf campos y tiendas](https://www.corporategolf.es/camposytiendasgolf); [Corporate Golf material logo](https://www.corporategolf.es/material-golf-logo)
- Spanish golf clubs run their own pro shops with online sections (examples): Villa Padierna — [Pro Shop](https://www.villapadierna.es/en/pro-shop/); Baviera Golf — [Pro Shop](https://bavieragolf.com/en/pro-shop/); Santana Golf — [Pro Shop](https://santanagolf.com/pro-shop/); Golf d'Aro lists a "Producto Proshop" category (page returned 404 when fetched 2026-09-29) — [Golf d'Aro](https://www.golfdaro.com/categoria-producto/productoproshop/)
- Supplier directory for the Spanish golf industry lists "Pro Golf Spain" as a proveedor — [golfindustria.es](https://golfindustria.es/proveedor/pro-golf-spain/)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: tumblers (cart/course), tote bags (less core), socks (golf socks with woven club crest are a classic pro-shop SKU), rings do not transfer. New products with strongest pro-shop fit: towels, caps/visors, ball markers, gloves, umbrellas, polos — the latter already dominated by brand distributors.
- Saturation: high for apparel and hard goods (brand-licensed pro-shop channel); moderate/low for woven socks and drinkware sold under the club's own crest rather than a golf brand. Corporate Golf offers embroidery, so an "embroidered, never printed" claim is not unique here; the differentiator would be woven-in socks, fast free mockups and low friction.
- Decision maker: pro-shop manager / head professional, or club general manager; tournaments (corporate days, club championships) drive gift-pack orders in addition to shop stock.
- Buying moments (inference): spring season opening (March–April), club championships (summer), corporate tournament season, Christmas gift packs.

### Gaps
- No published minimum order quantities or price lists for Spanish golf pro-shop suppliers were found.
- No Europe-wide count of golf clubs/courses extracted (the EGA PDF has country tables but the fetch returned a garbled total).

## Niche 3: Running clubs and run crews

### Takeaway
1,330 federated athletics clubs (89,522 licences) in Spain plus an uncounted layer of informal run crews; club kit is universal but socks are already offered as an add-on by Spanish kit makers (42K Running: socks with no minimum; MBS: 20-pair minimum), so this niche is crowded for apparel and moderately crowded for socks.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: Atletismo 89,522 licences, 1,330 clubs; roughly flat year-on-year — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf); [Infobae/EFE](https://www.infobae.com/espana/agencias/2025/05/30/el-padel-el-que-mas-crece-en-numero-de-federados-78-entre-los-deportes-mas-populares/)
- Spanish custom running-kit suppliers found: 42K Running (Valencia, in-house manufacturing; minimum 10 units per model, technical socks no minimum, 30–35 days from design/payment, sublimation and DTF, €80 exclusive design fee) — [42K clubes](https://42krunning.com/en/equipaciones-personalizadas-clubes/); Inverse Teams (Spain; robots blocked fetch) — [Inverse running](https://www.inverseteams.com/es/custom/ropa-de-running-personalizada/); Numbi Sport — [Numbi](https://numbisport.com/ropa-running-personalizada/); Aimar Sport — [Aimar](https://aimarsport.com/equipaciones/atletismo/); Runnek — [Runnek](https://www.runnek.es/categoria/equipacion-club-atletismo/); Wade FC — [Wade](https://wadefc.com/pages/equipaciones-personalizadas-running); Negua (Euskadi, trail) — [Negua](https://www.negua.eu/es/module-blog?id=91); Teefactory — [Teefactory](https://teefactory.es/ropa-personalizada-clubes-deportivos); Decathlon Club (athletics listed; "Mi Tienda Club" member shops) — [Decathlon Club](https://equipacion.decathlon.es/)
- Custom socks for clubs (direct competitor to a sock-led entry): MBS Calcetines Personalizables (Bergondo, A Coruña): minimum 20 pairs, €9.00 (20–49), €8.00 (50–100), €7.50 (100+), €50 design fee credited, ~1 week after approval, mockup in 3–4 days, free shipping over €200; shows 50+ club logos, mostly cycling and trail-running clubs — [MBS](https://calcetinespersonalizables.com/). Other Spanish custom-sock vendors surfaced: Divin (woven logo classic socks) — [Divin](https://www.bedivin.com/es/calcetines-clasicos-con-logo/); Tendissocks — [Tendis](https://www.tendissocks.com/producto/calcetines-personalizados/); Sockcétera — [Sockcétera](https://sockcetera.com/); Cagüen Socks — [Cagüen](https://caguensocks.com/personalizados); GC Footwear — [GC Footwear](https://www.gcfootwear.com/es/imprimir-calcetines/); SneakerBranding — [SneakerBranding](https://www.sneakerbranding.com/es/calcetines-personalizados/)
- owayo lists Running/Atletismo with a US club rebate programme and Spanish club discount tiers (5 % to 20 % on cumulative spend from €0 to €20,000+, counting orders from the previous 5 years) — [owayo running FAQ](https://us-es.owayo.com/atletismo/faq-uses.htm); [owayo club discount](https://www.owayo.es/baloncesto/acuerdo_descuento_clubs-es.htm)
- Run-crew merch exists as a lifestyle category (e.g. OSOM "Running Club" tee; Etsy "run club crewneck" market) — [OSOM](https://somososom.com/product/osom-running-club-camiseta-deportiva/); [Etsy run club crewneck](https://www.etsy.com/market/run_club_crewneck)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: socks transfer strongly if technical (cushioned running sock), not grip socks; tote bags fine as race-day/gym bag; tumblers weak (bidons/soft flasks are the running vessel); rings none. New products required: technical running socks, caps, neck gaiters/bragas, bidons, crewneck sweatshirts for run crews.
- Saturation: high for kit; moderate for socks (MBS's 50+ cycling/trail club logos show the demand and the incumbent). A differentiated angle would be woven (not sublimated) socks plus lifestyle items for run crews rather than federated clubs.
- Decision maker: club president/secretary in federated clubs; founder/organiser in run crews (often a brand-sponsored individual).
- Buying moments: September (new season), January (licence renewal; e.g. Madrid triathlon federation opens its 2024 licence platform on 22 Dec 2023, illustrating calendar-year licence cycles in endurance sports) — [triatlonchannel](https://triatlonchannel.com/2023/12/22/la-fmtri-abre-la-plataforma-licencias-2024/); race season and club anniversaries (inference).

### Gaps
- No count of informal run crews in Spain or Europe was found; no European athletics-club total (England Athletics search could not run).
- Inverse Teams minimums/lead times not retrievable (robots.txt).

## Niche 4: Cycling clubs and bike shops with club teams

### Takeaway
Cycling has the most club entities of any candidate (3,536 federated clubs, 68,720 licences, ≈19 licences per club) and a mature, highly saturated custom-kit market (Bioracer, Kalas, Inverse, Gobik, Santini/Sportful custom, plus many Spanish "sin pedido mínimo" resellers); socks are already a standard accessory line, so this niche is the hardest to enter with a sock-led offer.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: Ciclismo 68,720 licences, 3,536 clubs — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf)
- Bioracer: minimum 10 pieces (accessories and base layers not included), ~6 weeks after design approval, own factories in Belgium, Macedonia, Romania, Colombia, Tunisia; covers cycling, triathlon, running (ski/nordic and speed skating in navigation) — [Bioracer custom](https://shop.bioracer.com/pages/custom-cycling-apparel); process page blocked by robots — [Bioracer process](https://www2.bioracer.com/en/custom-order-process/)
- Kalas (Spain office, European manufacturing): minimum 5 pieces main garments, 10 per accessory type; offers a dedicated club e-shop; 30 years' experience — [Kalas](https://www.kalas.es/camisetas-deportivas-y-ropa-personalizada/)
- Spanish resellers advertising no minimums: El Tío del Mazo — [eltiodelmazo](https://www.eltiodelmazo.com/equipacion-personalizada/); Clotmarket (Madrid) — [Clotmarket](https://clotmarket.com/ropa-ciclismo-personalizada-sin-pedido-minimo/); BKFIT — [BKFIT](https://bkfit.com/ropa-de-ciclismo-personalizada/); La Bicicleta — [La Bicicleta](https://labicicletashop.com/ropa-de-ciclismo-personalizada/); MJD Sport — [MJD](https://www.mjdsport.com/); Evernya — [Evernya](https://evernya.es/en/collections/ropa-ciclismo-personalizada); MyClubGroup — [MyClubGroup](https://myclubgroup.com/es/kit-ciclismo/); Inverse Teams — [Inverse cycling](https://www.inverseteams.com/es/custom/ropa-de-ciclismo-personalizada/)
- Buyer guides to custom cycling kit brands (evidence of a mature, well-documented category): [Cyclingnews](https://www.cyclingnews.com/features/best-custom-cycling-kit/); [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/group-tests/best-cycling-bespoke-custom-kit-brands-472747); [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/custom-cycling-jerseys-kit/)
- Custom socks for cycling clubs: MBS's client wall is mostly bike clubs; 20-pair minimum, €7.50–9.00 — [MBS](https://calcetinespersonalizables.com/)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: socks yes (cycling socks are a fashion/identity item) but as a thin, tall technical sock; tumblers no (bidons); totes marginal (musette bags would be the cycling equivalent); rings no. New products required: bidons, musettes, caps (casquettes), neck gaiters.
- Saturation: very high. Every kit maker bundles socks and accessories and many offer club web shops with per-member ordering, which removes the club's stock risk — a structural advantage over a 50-unit bulk model.
- Decision maker: club board or team manager; in bike shops, the owner. Typical order: one kit design per season with a per-member web-shop window (Kalas e-shop, Decathlon "Mi Tienda Club").

### Gaps
- Gobik, Santini and Sportful custom pages were not fetched this session; their minimums/lead times are unverified here.
- No Europe-wide cycling club count.

## Niche 5: Triathlon clubs

### Takeaway
870 clubs and 24,446 licences in Spain (≈28 per club); triathlon clubs order trisuits and kit from the same cycling/running suppliers (Bioracer, Inverse, Decathlon Club, SQUALI for swim items), so the apparel side is saturated, but the small club size makes a 50-unit minimum hard — this niche fits best as an add-on to cycling/running rather than a standalone target.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: Triatlón 24,446 licences, 870 clubs — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf)
- FETRI has a club finder, club rankings and a national clubs commission but publishes no club total on its clubs page — [FETRI clubes](https://triatlon.org/clubes/)
- Licence cycle is calendar-year; the Madrid federation opened its 2024 licence platform on 22 Dec 2023 — [triatlonchannel](https://triatlonchannel.com/2023/12/22/la-fmtri-abre-la-plataforma-licencias-2024/); regional licence pages: [FMTRI](https://triatlonmadrid.org/licencias/solicitud-de-licencia/), [Triatlón Aragón](https://triatlonaragon.org/Contenido/Federarse), [Castilla y León](https://www.triatloncastillayleon.com/apps/licencias/index.php), [Federación Vasca](https://triatloi.org/es/licencias-2/)
- Suppliers covering triathlon: Bioracer (cycling, triathlon, running) — [Bioracer](https://shop.bioracer.com/pages/custom-cycling-apparel); Decathlon Club (triathlon listed first among its sports) — [Decathlon Club](https://equipacion.decathlon.es/); SQUALI (Madrid) makes personalised triathlon wetsuits and club swim kit, no mandatory minimum, 4–6 weeks — [SQUALI](https://squali.es/equipaciones-de-natacion-personalizadas-para-equipos-y-clubes/)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: socks (technical), tote/transition bags, towels (transition-zone towel is a natural branded item), tumblers weak; rings no. New products: swim caps (see swimming), towels, bidons, neck gaiters.
- Saturation: high for trisuits/kit; medium for accessories; club size (≈28 licences) below the 50-unit threshold for most clubs.
- Decision maker: club board; orders usually placed once per year in Dec–Feb aligned with licence renewal (inference from licence cycle).

### Gaps
- Taymory or other Spanish tri-specific custom suppliers not verified (search budget).
- No European triathlon licence/club totals.

## Niche 6: Swimming clubs and masters swimming

### Takeaway
800 clubs and 70,146 licences in Spain (≈88 per club); the branded item every club buys is the swim cap, a low-price, high-volume product served by many Spanish printers (Pronens, Nadadores.com, gorrospersonalizados.es, SQUALI), while towels, ponchos and poolside bags are less contested and fit a resale-margin model.

### Cited Findings
- Spain 2024: Natación 70,146 licences, 800 clubs; Salvamento y Socorrismo 15,957 / 90 — [CSD 2024](https://www.csd.gob.es/sites/default/files/media/files/2025-12/Licencias%20y%20Clubes%202024.pdf); swimming = 1.6 % of licences in 2023 — [Servimedia](https://www.servimedia.es/noticias/espana-tiene-mas-4-2-millones-deportistas-federados-nuevo-record-historico/1410171195)
- SQUALI (Madrid): custom swimsuits, caps, tri wetsuits, club apparel for swimming, triathlon, water polo, lifesaving, artistic swimming, diving; no mandatory minimum; 4–6 weeks; no towels/ponchos/backpacks on the page; no member web shop — [SQUALI](https://squali.es/equipaciones-de-natacion-personalizadas-para-equipos-y-clubes/)
- Other Spanish custom-cap vendors: Pronens (lycra caps for pools, clubs, schools, gyms, hotels) — [Pronens](https://www.pronens.com/productos/colegioscolectivos/equipaciones-deportivaspetos-y-complementos/gorro-natacion-lycra); Nadadores.com (silicone or fabric caps for clubs; product page 404 on fetch) — [Nadadores](https://www.nadadores.com/categorias/gorros-de-natacion-personalizados.html); gorrospersonalizados.es (latex) — [gorrospersonalizados](https://gorrospersonalizados.es/gorros-natacion/latex); productospersonalizados.es — [productospersonalizados](https://www.productospersonalizados.es/gorros-natacion-personalizados/); 360swim — [360swim](https://shop.360swim.com/es/pages/gorras-natacion-personalizadas); Anchés Sports — [Anchés](https://www.anchessports.com/en/1099-personalised-swimming-caps.html)
- Decathlon Club lists swimming among its custom-kit sports with "Mi Tienda Club" member storefronts — [Decathlon Club](https://equipacion.decathlon.es/)

### Inferences
- Product transfer: tote bags (poolside/kit bag) strong; tumblers moderate (poolside hydration); socks weak (no sock use in pool; only for dryland/lifestyle); rings no. New products required: swim caps (commodity, low margin), microfibre towels, ponchos, mesh kit bags, embroidered parkas/robes (high value, strong club-identity item).
- Saturation: very high for caps; moderate for towels/ponchos/robes with embroidered crest; SQUALI does not offer towels/ponchos, a visible gap.
- Decision maker: club board / technical director; masters sections often self-organise a group order. Swimming clubs are large enough (≈88 licences) for 50-unit minimums.
