# Fitness & boutique-studio niches (non-pilates/yoga/padel) as B2B markets for branded resale merchandise — Spain first, then Europe

Research date: 2026-09-29. Method: web search + page fetches. Web-search budget was exhausted mid-task, so some niches (dance, running/treadmill, calisthenics) and some "merch culture" evidence are thinner than others; those gaps are flagged explicitly. All venue counts below are either quoted from the cited source or labelled "estimate". No Padel content.

Mediterrano's four current products for transfer scoring: grip socks (woven/embroidered logo, min 50 pairs), tumblers, tote bags, pilates rings.

---

## Cross-cutting market context (Spain + Europe) and generic supplier landscape

### Takeaway
Europe has ~67,500 fitness clubs and 75.5 M members (2025); Spain alone has 7.1 M members and boutique/"specialised concept" studios are the fastest-diversifying segment. Boutique studios earn per session rather than by fixed monthly fee, which makes ancillary revenue (retail/merch) structurally more important for them than for big-box chains. The Spanish supplier landscape for studio merch is dominated by generic textile printers (min 10–15 units, sublimation/screen print), not by niche resale specialists.

### Cited Findings
- European fitness market 2025: 67,515 clubs (+3 %), 75.5 M members (+5.8 %), €39.1 bn revenue (+9.1 %); Spain 7.1 M members (+8.3 %); penetration 9.3 % of total population — [EuropeActive press release, EHFMR 2025](https://www.europeactive.eu/blog/press-corner-4/strong-growth-in-members-and-revenues-for-european-health-fitness-market-in-2025-140)
- EuropeActive/Deloitte 2026 report: "specialised concepts – including Pilates, yoga and CrossFit studios" are "attracting smaller but distinct user groups, contributing to a more diversified and segmented market"; top-20 operators serve 20.9 M members across 7,607 clubs — [HCM, Record highs (EHFMR 2026)](https://www.healthclubmanagement.co.uk/health-club-management-features/Record-highs/38441)
- Barcelona: 80 new private gyms opened since 2020 (27 >500 m², 33 of 200–500 m², 17 of 100–200 m², 3 <100 m²); boutique centres operate on 200–400 m²; Barcelona penetration ≈20 % vs 11.6 % national; experts call boutiques "less rentable" than chains because customers pay per session/pack rather than monthly fee — [Metrópoli Abierta / El Español, 28-03-2024](https://metropoliabierta.elespanol.com/economia/20240328/apertura-masiva-de-gimnasios-boutique-en-barcelona/832166846_0.html)
- Spain: opening a boutique studio costs €50,000–€450,000 on 100–500 m²; pre-COVID, boutique facilities were "nearly half of all new club openings" in Spain — [Fit4Life blog, boutiques cycling](https://www.fit4life.es/blog/boutiques-cycling/)
- Generic Spanish supplier targeting boxes/gyms: Teefactory — tees, tanks, shorts, gym towels, backpacks, embroidered patches; minimum 10 units (towels/patches 50); price range €0.67–€32.01; messaging is "teambuilding, promocionar el box", not resale — [Teefactory, ropa personalizada CrossFit](https://teefactory.es/ropa-personalizada-crossfit)
- Generic Spanish supplier: Sudeporte "Equipamos tu Box" — sublimated polyester tees, cotton tees, hoodies, gym sacs; minimum 15 units, volume discounts up to 35 %, made in Spain, free shipping >€300 — [Sudeporte CrossFit](https://crossfit.sudeporte.com/)
- Other generic Spanish textile personalisers ranking for "camisetas crossfit personalizadas": [Aimar Sport](https://aimarsport.com/disena-tu-propia-camiseta-de-crossfit-personalizada-guia-paso-a-paso/), [BF Bordados](https://bfbordados.com/camisetas-de-crossfit-personalizadas/), [Baizinka](https://personalizacion.bzksport.com/productos/camiseta-de-crossfit-crop-top-personalizada/), [Ceres Textil y Publicidad](https://cerestextilypublicidad.com/productos/297-crossfit), [Tú Personalizas](https://www.tupersonalizas.es/producto/camiseta-crossfit/)
- Custom grip-sock competitor in Europe: Merchery — grip socks "perfect for yoga, Pilates, barre", made in Portugal, "2,000+ customers", price range not published — [Merchery grip socks](https://merchery.co/shop/grip-socks)
- Other grip-sock personalisers ranking in Spanish for "calcetines antideslizantes personalizados": [Regalo Publicidad (promo socks from €0.76)](https://www.regalopublicidad.com/ropa-personalizada/ropa-interior-personalizada/calcetines), [Seritec](https://www.seritec.com/articulos/21334_calcetines-antideslizantes-personalizados.htm), plus Chinese factories ([eesocks](https://www.eesocks.com/calcetin-antideslizante-mayoreo-personalizado), [GS Socks](https://www.gssocks.com/sports-socks/crew-sport-socks/anti-slip-custom-logo-socks.html), [Leeline](https://www.leelinesports.com/custom-sock-manufacturers/custom-grip-socks/), [iCompressionSocks](https://www.icompressionsocks.com/grip-socks-manufacturer/)) and wholesale marketplace [Faire (calcetines antideslizantes)](https://www.faire.com/es/discover/calcetines-antideslizantes)
- US reference list of "branded merch for gyms": hoodies, tanks/tees, leggings, hats, water bottles, duffels/totes, towels, gloves and socks, yoga mats; criterion "fun and functional", wearable "on repeat" — [Gemnote](https://www.gemnote.com/blog/branded-merch-for-your-gym-or-fitness-center) (US-oriented)

### Inferences
- Because boutiques monetise per visit, a "13th-month" retail line (see climbing) is more valuable to them than to Basic-Fit-type chains; the pitch "merch is revenue, not cost" travels directly.
- Every Spanish supplier found in this research sells printed/sublimated textiles with 10–15-unit minimums and frames merch as promotion. None positions itself as a "resale merch programme" supplier with woven/embroidered logo and mockup service; that positioning gap is the same one Mediterrano exploits in pilates.

### Gaps
- No European (EuropeActive/Deloitte) count of boutique studios by discipline exists in the public summaries fetched; segment counts must come from niche sources below.
- Statista's Spain CrossFit-by-region page and Valgo's "63 cadenas" list could not be fetched (paywall/403).
- Camaloon, Printful, Printify and Vistaprint were not fetched in this session (search budget exhausted); they are the well-known generic print-on-demand layer but no page was verified here.

## Niche 1 — Barre studios

### Takeaway
Spain has an estimated ~240 venues offering barre (directory count, likely including multi-discipline studios), concentrated in Madrid, Barcelona and Valencia, with a home-grown franchise (Club Barre, Valencia) now expanding. Grip socks are the natural product: the niche is the closest sibling to pilates and all four Mediterrano products transfer.

### Cited Findings
- Studiomaps lists 241 barre studios in Spain: Madrid 29, Barcelona 29, Valencia 17 — [Studiomaps, Estudios de barre en España](https://studiomaps.app/es/barre) (directory count; almost certainly includes pilates/yoga studios that also offer barre — treat as upper-bound estimate)
- Club Barre (founded Valencia 2020) is franchising: total investment ≈€142,250, entry fee €20,000; first franchised space "Club Barre Universidades" (Algirós, Valencia) — [Tormo Franquicias](https://tormofranquicias.es/club-barre-fitness-boutique-expansion/); also covered by [Cantabria Económica](https://www.cantabriaeconomica.com/patrocinado/informacion-al-dia/el-arte-de-crecer-sin-perder-la-esencia-club-barre-revoluciona-el-fitness-boutique/)
- Independent barre studios in Spain with own websites: [Boutique Barre Madrid (Goya, Chamberí)](https://www.boutiquebarre.me/), [Be Barre Barcelona](https://www.bebarrebarcelona.com/); ClassPass lists barre in Madrid — [ClassPass Madrid barre](https://classpass.com/search/madrid/barre)
- Pure Barre's parent Xponential Fitness: "more than 3,000 studios open" across all brands in 49 US states and 31 countries (no per-brand or European breakdown published) — [Wikipedia, Xponential Fitness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xponential_Fitness)
- A European custom grip-sock supplier explicitly markets to barre studios ("yoga, Pilates, barre") — [Merchery](https://merchery.co/shop/grip-socks)

### Inferences
- Barre is done in socks on a sprung floor; grip socks are functionally mandatory, so barre studios are the highest-transfer niche for Mediterrano's current product (socks, tumblers, totes; rings less so but small-equipment classes use balls/bands). This is an inference from practice; the Pure Barre/Xponential FAQ confirming "sticky socks required" could not be fetched (see Gaps).
- Spanish phrasing likely used by owners (inferred from supplier page titles, not from volume data): "calcetines antideslizantes personalizados", "calcetines barre con logo", "merchandising estudio barre". English: "custom barre grip socks", "barre studio merch".
- Buying moments (inference): September re-opening, January, and opening of new franchise units (Club Barre expansion).

### Gaps
- No official count of barre-only studios in Spain or Europe exists; Studiomaps is a crowd/directory figure.
- Pure Barre FAQ (grip-sock requirement) fetch was blocked (provenance approval needed); no in-studio sock-sales evidence gathered.
- No price/margin data for barre-studio merch found.

## Niche 2 — Indoor cycling / spinning boutiques

### Takeaway
Boutique cycling in Spain is a small, young cluster (roughly a dozen named studios in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia and regional cities, plus the Xperience Bike franchise) inside a European boutique-cycling scene of small chains (Rocycle 11 studios NL/DE, Dynamo 3 FR, Velocity/OpenRide CH, RYDE UK). Towels, tumblers and socks fit; grip socks do not (cycling shoes).

### Cited Findings
- Spanish boutique cycling studios named: Síclo (2 Madrid studios + a corner at The Principal hotel, expanding to Barcelona), Lapso Studios (Barcelona Diagonal, plans for Madrid/Valencia/Southern Europe), LoveCycle (Barcelona, pioneer 2016), Bam Labs (Madrid), Sessions Studios (pop-up Four Seasons Madrid), SoloCyclo (Valencia), Casa Sana (A Coruña), Psiquiko (Pamplona), Mambo (Girona) — [Fit4Life](https://www.fit4life.es/blog/boutiques-cycling/)
- European directory (personal blog, "initial lineup"): Madrid — BAM Labs, TopCycle, Síclo; Barcelona — Goodfit, Lapso, LoveCycle; Rocycle 6 studios/3 countries; ML City 2 (NL); Dynamo 3 (FR); Velocity 2 (CH); OpenRide 2 (CH); RYDE 2 (UK) — [Lauriëtte, Indoor cycling studios in Europe](https://lauriette.com/indoor-cycling-studios-in-europe/)
- Rocycle: "7 studios in The Netherlands, 4 in Germany", expanding to Belgium; Kinto Workout Bottle 480 ml €30 featured (editorial partnership, not co-branded) — [Kinto Europe journal](https://kinto-europe.com/blogs/journal/rocycle)
- Rostudios (Amsterdam; Rocycle parent) raised $40 M+ and is entering NYC with $25 reformer and cycling classes — [Fitt Insider](https://insider.fitt.co/press-release/rostudios-raises-40m-enters-nyc-with-25-reformer-cycling-classes/); [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/amsterdam-rostudios-to-enter-nyc-with-low-price-pilates-cycling-concept/)

- ClassPass indoor-cycling listings exist for Madrid and Barcelona — [ClassPass Madrid](https://classpass.com/search/madrid/indoor-cycling), [ClassPass Barcelona](https://classpass.com/search/barcelona/indoor-cycling)
- Barry's (which runs "Ride" cycling classes) sells logo socks at $25–48 (two-pack $48), hats $40–42, tops $58–108 in its own shop — [Barry's Shop](https://shop.barrys.com/)

### Inferences
- Venue count Spain: estimate ≈10–20 true boutique cycling studios (sum of named studios above) plus an unknown number of Xperience Bike units; Europe: low hundreds, mostly single-city micro-chains (estimate from the two directories).
- Product fit: sweat towels, tumblers/bottles (hydration is central), caps, tote bags; crew "ride socks" rather than grip socks. The SoulCycle/Barry's apparel culture is US-led; in Spain merch culture at cycling studios is unproven (no Spanish studio shop fetched).
- Phrasing (inferred): "toallas personalizadas gimnasio", "botellas personalizadas estudio ciclo indoor", "merchandising estudio spinning"; EN "cycling studio merch", "custom studio towels".

### Gaps
- No count of Spanish or European boutique cycling studios from an industry report; no Xperience Bike unit count fetched.
- No evidence gathered that Spanish cycling studios run web shops (Síclo/LoveCycle/Lapso shops not fetched — budget exhausted).

## Niche 3 — CrossFit boxes and functional-training gyms

### Takeaway
Spain has ~700 affiliated CrossFit boxes (2023; 663 in Q1 2023, target 1,000 within four years), the largest countable boutique network in this list, and box merch is a documented, drop-based resale culture (25–50 % member participation per drop in US data). Tees/hoodies dominate; Mediterrano's tumblers and totes transfer, grip socks do not.

### Cited Findings
- Spain: 663 affiliated boxes (Q1 2023), 28 added in Q1 2023, 635 at end-2022 (+17 % in 2022); Catalonia ≈128, Madrid/centre ≈125, Andalusia 95, Valencia ≈80; 5–9 openings/month; worldwide 14,000+ boxes in 155 countries, 72 % in the US (~7,500), Europe 23.1 % of global boxes — [CMD Sport, CrossFit supera los 660 boxes](https://www.cmdsport.com/esencial/cmd-fitnessgym/crossfit-supera-los-660-boxes-espana/)
- Spain ≈700 boxes (Sept 2023), 98 added Jan–Sept 2023; country manager Natalia Díez targets 1,000 boxes in four years; Spain "fourth-fastest growing CrossFit market" — [CMD Sport, proyecta alcanzar 1.000 gimnasios](https://www.cmdsport.com/fitness/actualidad-fitness/crossfit-espana-proyecta-alcanzar-1-000-gimnasios-cuatro-anos/); related: [CMD Sport, 700 gimnasios factible](https://www.cmdsport.com/fitness/actualidad-fitness/crossfit-espana-ve-factible-llegar-los-700-gimnasios-este-ano/)
- Statista tracks "número de gimnasios oficiales de CrossFit en España por comunidad autónoma" (paywalled) — [Statista ES](https://es.statista.com/estadisticas/1102084/numero-de-gimnasios-oficiales-de-crossfit-en-espana-por-comunidad-autonoma)
- Functional-training franchises entering Spain: BFT (Body Fit Training, Australia) opened in Eixample Feb 2024 and planned up to 10 studios in Spain in 2024; Trib3 opened its 5th Barcelona site March 2024 — [Metrópoli Abierta](https://metropoliabierta.elespanol.com/economia/20240328/apertura-masiva-de-gimnasios-boutique-en-barcelona/832166846_0.html)
- Box merch economics (US supplier guide): consistent programmes see "participation rates of 25–50 % per drop"; 4–6 drops/year (January, March/April Open, May Murph = "single highest-selling merch event", August, Oct/Nov holiday); hoodies priced $48–55 "with strong margins"; 24 pieces per design minimum; 2–3 weeks turnaround — [Forever Fierce, CrossFit Box Merchandise Guide](https://foreverfierce.com/blogs/news/crossfit-box-merchandise-guide) (US)
- Spanish suppliers explicitly targeting boxes: [Teefactory](https://teefactory.es/ropa-personalizada-crossfit) (min 10; towels/patches min 50; €0.67–32.01), [Sudeporte](https://crossfit.sudeporte.com/) (min 15, sublimation, made in Spain), [BF Bordados](https://bfbordados.com/camisetas-de-crossfit-personalizadas/) (embroidery), [Aimar Sport](https://aimarsport.com/producto/camisetas-premium-edicion-limitada/)
- Shopify case study of a CrossFit apparel brand ("WOD Gear") shows the model is well established — [Shopify success story](https://ru.shopify.com/success-stories/wod-gear)

### Inferences
- Europe estimate: 23.1 % of ~14,000 = roughly 3,200 boxes (derived from CMD Sport figures; not an official count).
- Spanish search phrasing (from supplier titles): "camisetas crossfit personalizadas", "ropa personalizada crossfit box", "merchandising box crossfit", "sudaderas personalizadas box"; EN: "crossfit box merch", "affiliate apparel", "gym merch drop".
- Seasonality transfers from US data: CrossFit Open (Feb–Mar), summer, autumn hoodie season, box anniversaries and local competitions; Murph (May) is US-specific.
- Product transfer: tumblers (strong; boxes sell shakers/bottles), tote/gym bags (medium), grip socks (no — but woven crew socks with box logo are plausible), rings (no).
- Supplier competition is high and cheap (10-unit minimums), so AI-answer visibility for a new entrant is harder than in pilates unless positioned on embroidered/woven premium goods.

### Gaps
- No Spanish or European data on box merch revenue share; Forever Fierce numbers are US.
- 2024–2026 official Spanish box count not found (latest sourced figure Sept 2023).

## Niche 4 — Boxing, kickboxing and fitboxing gyms

### Takeaway
Boxing is booming in Spain: federated clubs rose from 494 (2017) to 719 (2024) and licences from 10,108 to 15,315, while Brooklyn Fitboxing alone runs 200+ gyms in Spain (240–250 worldwide end-2024, target 350 in 2026) with 70,000+ users and an official shop proving members buy branded gloves, wraps, tees, towels, bottles and totes. Independent boxing gyms (not franchisees) are the addressable target.

### Cited Findings
- CSD data: 2017 — 10,108 licences / 494 clubs; 2022 — 7,997 licences; 2023 — 16,282 licences / 708 clubs; 2024 — 15,315 licences / 719 clubs; female licences 2,059 (2017) → 2,742 (2024); growth drivers: Topuria effect, fitboxing, streaming events (Velada del Año V 10.8 M concurrent viewers) — [Variación XXI, El auge del boxeo en España (Nov 2025)](https://variacionxxi.com/2025/11/04/auge-boxeo-espana/)

- Brooklyn Fitboxing: 240–250 gyms worldwide end-2024, target 300 in 2025, 12 clubs in Italy, Mexico opened, Brazil pending; revenue target €50 M (2025) from €40 M — [2Playbook](https://www.2playbook.com/fitness/brooklyn-fitboxing-pone-rumbo-50-millones-facturacion-con-300-gimnasios-en-2025_17359_102.html); 2026 targets 350 centres and >€52 M — [Franquicias Hoy](https://www.franquiciashoy.es/actualidad/noticias/brooklyn-fitboxing-objetivo-52-millones-y-350-centros-en-2026)

- RFEBox certifies clubs in a 1–3-star system (min 5/10/15 competition licences) — [RFEBox, Los clubes y sus certificaciones](https://feboxeo.es/los-clubes-y-sus-certificaciones/); club homologation — [RFEBox, Homologa tu club](https://feboxeo.es/homologa-tu-club/)

## Niche 5 — Brazilian jiu-jitsu and martial-arts academies

### Takeaway
Spain has roughly 180 countable BJJ academies (blog census; "many more" uncounted) inside a global niche where academy-branded uniforms are already the norm (Gracie Barra official uniform ecosystem) and a crowded supplier field (Bōa, Summo, Kimono BJJ, Fight Sport, Bruiser, Kano) sells custom gis and sublimated rash guards to clubs. Strong merch culture, but products are printed/sublimated and socks are irrelevant; only totes/tumblers and embroidered patches transfer.

### Cited Findings

Embroidered gi patches, tote/gi bags and tumblers are the realistic entry; grip socks and rings are irrelevant (barefoot mat sport).

- Phrasing (from supplier titles): "kimono bjj personalizado", "rashguard personalizado club", "parches bordados jiu jitsu"; EN "custom academy rash guards", "custom gi with logo".
- Seasonality: IBJJF European Open (January) and belt promotions/gradings (inference; no source fetched).

### Gaps
- No European academy count (IBJJF/UAEJJF registries not fetched); no Spanish price/margin data for academy-branded gear.
- No Reddit/owner-community evidence retrieved (search budget exhausted).

## Niche 6 — Climbing and bouldering gyms

### Takeaway
Spain has ~220 commercial climbing gyms (+~50 % in three years; SportCities data), part of a global dataset of 2,282 gyms in 84 countries where the UK is Europe's densest market. Climbing is the only niche with published retail economics: gym retail is a "13th month" of revenue at ~40 % margin, and gym-branded apparel, bottles and stickers are standard shop items; chalk bags and brushes are the niche-specific merch, served by a few specialist customisers (ChupaClimb, Custom Climbing International, Practical Climbing).

### Cited Findings

## Synthesis: ranking for Mediterrano (venue count × merch culture × product transfer × supplier gap)

### Takeaway
On the evidence gathered, the best expansion niches are (1) climbing/bouldering gyms, (2) barre (with Lagree as an add-on), (3) boxing/kickboxing independents, and (4) CrossFit boxes; dance schools are large but unverified; cycling is small; EMS, calisthenics and running are weak fits.

### Cited Findings
- Climbing: 220 Spanish gyms growing ~50 %/3 yrs ([Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/espana/deportes/2026/09/15/primero-fue-el-running-luego-el-gimnasio-y-ahora-la-escalada-el-nuevo-deporte-de-moda-aumenta-su-popularidad-y-los-rocodromos-crecen-casi-un-50-en-tres-anos/)), documented retail economics at 40 % margin ([CWA](https://www.cwapro.org/blog/the-13th-month-retail-in-climbing-gyms)), few specialist customisers ([ChupaClimb](https://chupaclimb.com/custom-shop-2/)).
- Barre: 241 listed venues ([Studiomaps](https://studiomaps.app/es/barre)), grip-sock product identical to pilates ([Merchery](https://merchery.co/shop/grip-socks)).
- Boxing: 719 federated clubs ([Variación XXI](https://variacionxxi.com/2025/11/04/auge-boxeo-espana/)), proven branded-accessory pricing at €5–24 ([Brooklyn Fitboxing Shop](https://shop.brooklynfitboxing.com/en/collections/all)).
- CrossFit: ~700 boxes ([CMD Sport](https://www.cmdsport.com/fitness/actualidad-fitness/crossfit-espana-proyecta-alcanzar-1-000-gimnasios-cuatro-anos/)), 25–50 % drop participation (US, [Forever Fierce](https://foreverfierce.com/blogs/news/crossfit-box-merchandise-guide)), but crowded cheap textile suppliers ([Teefactory](https://teefactory.es/ropa-personalizada-crossfit), [Sudeporte](https://crossfit.sudeporte.com/)).

### Inferences
- Product roadmap implied by the evidence: (a) grip socks → barre, Lagree, dance (contemporary); (b) tumblers + totes → every niche, strongest in climbing, boxing, cycling; (c) embroidered chalk bags → climbing (new SKU, high AI-visibility potential because specialist suppliers are few and mostly US/China); (d) embroidered towels → boxing, cycling, HIIT (new SKU); (e) pilates rings → no transfer outside pilates/barre.
- AEO angle: the niches where an AI assistant currently has few specialised European names to cite are climbing (chalk bags/brushes: ChupaClimb, Custom Climbing International) and barre/Lagree grip socks (Merchery); CrossFit and BJJ already have many named suppliers, boxing gloves are dominated by Asian OEMs.
- Season calendar across niches (mix of sourced and inferred): September (all), January (all; BJJ European Open), Feb–Mar CrossFit Open, June dance galas, autumn–winter climbing, plus continuous new-venue openings (CrossFit 5–9/month; Brooklyn Fitboxing ~50/yr by targets; climbing +50 %/3 yrs).

### Gaps
- No search-volume data for any phrase; all phrasings are inferred from supplier page titles and should be validated with a keyword tool.
- Web-search budget ran out before dance, running and calisthenics could be researched to the same depth; Sharma/Climbat/Síclo/LoveCycle shops, Pure Barre/solidcore sock policies and IBJJF/CrossFit Open calendars were not fetched (provenance restrictions or budget).
