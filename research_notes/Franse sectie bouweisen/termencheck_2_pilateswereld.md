Scope: second, independent verification. Only what was actually visible on fetched pages is reported, with URLs. Sources fetched: Decathlon.fr / Decathlon.ch (category pages, advice articles, product pages), Conseil Sport Decathlon, Virtuagym FR, Mindbody FR, Zenoti FR (translated), Kolonell FR blog, myPOS FR guide "ouvrir un studio de Pilates", modelesdebusinessplan.com, Team France Export (sourcing brief for custom pilates socks), Urban Sports Club FR venue page (Culte Reformer, Paris), FPMP event page (HelloAsso), On.com FR article, Tissus Print (tote bag supplier), GiftCampaign FR blog.

Blocked (permission not granted, could not read): pro.bsport.io/fr/pilates, label-chaussette.com (two blog articles). Not reached within budget: deciplus.fr blog, resamania.com blog, sissel.fr, Amazon.fr listings, Oysho.fr, Lululemon FR, Zalando.fr product pages, a French studio page with an explicit product boutique.

## Summary table (term → forms seen → URL → recommendation)

### Takeaway
Retail and studio-world French converges on "chaussettes antidérapantes" (+ "Pilates"), "Pilates Reformer"/"reformer", "anneau de Pilates", "gourde isotherme", "tote bag", "studio de Pilates", vouvoiement, "clients"/"membres"/"élèves" depending on the author, "fidéliser"/"fidélisation", "chiffre d'affaires"/"sources de revenus"/"marge", "BAT"/"aperçu" and "Demander un devis". Several items (welcome pack, Christmas phrasing, REACH, "maquette" in supplier use, Amazon/Oysho titles) remain unverified by this check.

### Inferences
- Studio-facing French B2B copy is uniformly vouvoiement; no page addressed owners with "tu" (the only "tu" seen was a freelancer gig title on comeup.com in search results, not a studio or supplier page).
- "boutique" is ambiguous in this niche (software e-shop module, "boutique fitness" = gym format); the studio world says "vendre des accessoires" / "la vente d'accessoires" when it means retail inside the studio.
- "chaussettes antidérapantes" is the head term everywhere; "Pilates" (capital P) is added as a qualifier, in either order ("Chaussettes antidérapantes Pilates" / "Chaussettes Pilates antidérapantes").
