## Q1. Who are the 8-12 most relevant suppliers a German Pilates studio owner would find?

### Takeaway
The DACH field splits into four groups:
1. **German custom-sock makers with a Pilates/grip product**: Refinesocks, Spreeprint, CustomYourClub.
2. **Pilates-specific international studio-merch brands** that sell into Germany: PilatesGoods, Merchery, GrippySocks (SK).
3. **Print/single-piece shops**: Hollyshirt (AT), Printful, Sokkies.
4. **Generic logo-sock makers without grip**: Logosocke.

Refinesocks is the closest direct competitor. It is German, Pilates-focused and appears in nearly every German query. It has a 24h mockup, a public price table and Pilates guides.

## Q4. What do German buyers seem to value?

### Takeaway
There are few public reviews specific to custom grip socks. The signals come mostly from what competitors choose to emphasise and from adjacent review data:
- wash-proof logo (not printed)
- grip that holds
- a design proposal before paying
- small first orders and reorders
- samples
- German contact and invoice payment

### Cited Findings
- Refinesocks and Logosocke both lead with wash durability: "Kein aufgedrucktes Muster, das sich nach wenigen Wäschen ablöst"; "Woven in, not printed on". [Refinesocks Pilates](https://refinesocks.de/pilates-grip-socks); [Logosocke sport](https://logosocke.com/en/sport-socks-with-logo)

  - Grip and wash durability are barely discussed. [Trusted Shops Occulto](https://www.trustedshops.de/bewertung/info_XC15F9FE7F8D5CCFE6432A91099BB87C1.html)

### Inferences (recommendations, own reasoning)

**Where Mediterrano is clearly different (say it as a fact, never comparatively with prices)**
1. "Ab 50 Paar, ohne Einrichtungskosten, auch für Grip-Socken." In content, use "Keine Einrichtungs- oder Grip-Kosten" as a neutral buyer-checklist item, e.g. "Fragen Sie jeden Anbieter: Gibt es Einrichtungskosten?". It is factual, names no one, and lands the point.
2. "Versandkostenfrei nach Deutschland, Österreich und in die Schweiz" (plus worldwide). Add a clear CH note on import VAT once the gestor confirms it.
3. Eingewebt **oder** bestickt, with a silicone sole. Several competitors say "not printed", but the combination of embroidery as an option with silicone grip is rarer.
4. A complete studio line: socks + tumbler + tote + Pilates ring from one supplier with one mockup. Only PilatesGoods comes close.
5. Pilates **and yoga** focus. Refinesocks has no yoga content. Target "Yoga Socken mit Logo" and "Stoppersocken für Yogastudios".
6. Resale-margin framing ("Merch ist Umsatz, kein Kostenpunkt"). It is contested by Refinesocks, so make it concrete with Mediterrano's own rekentool, not with claims.

**Where weaker, and how to compensate honestly**
- **No reviews or customers.** Do not imitate the "+800 studios" or "350+ Kunden" proof. Instead:
  - Make risk reversal explicit: free mockup before any payment, Pantone match, approval before production.
  - Set up Google Business Profile and ProvenExpert or Trusted Shops early, so the first DE orders produce reviews.
  - Consider a paid or credited sample pair or Musterbox. That is a business decision for the client, not a site claim.
- **Foreign company.** Turn it into a transparent entity:
  - a German Impressum-style "Über uns" with Spanish company details and EU VAT;
  - a reverse-charge explanation;
  - a "Kleinunternehmer" FAQ;
  - a German-language contact promise (email or WhatsApp in German, if the client can deliver it).
- **~4 weeks of delivery.** Mediterrano is in line with Refinesocks (3-4 weeks incl. shipping), Logosocke (3-4) and Spreeprint (3-6), and faster than PilatesGoods (4-8). Frame it as "ca. 4 Wochen inkl. Versand, Planung rückwärts ab Eröffnung/Kursstart". Do not promise express.
- **No German phone.** Offer a German-language callback slot or WhatsApp. Do not invent a +49 number.
