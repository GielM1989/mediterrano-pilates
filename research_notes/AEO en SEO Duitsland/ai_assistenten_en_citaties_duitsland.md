# AI-Assistenten und Zitationen in Deutschland: wie KI-Antwortmaschinen bei deutschsprachigen B2B-Anbieterfragen Quellen wählen und was einen kleinen ausländischen Anbieter zitierbar macht (Stand Oktober 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: Germany first, DACH secondary. Every number below has a source. Vendor studies are marked **[vendor study]**. Items older than 2025 are marked **[pre-2025]**.

Note for the writer: two primary pages could not be fetched in this session because the permission request was withdrawn: OpenAI's publisher FAQ (help.openai.com) and Bing's official IndexNow page (bing.com/indexnow). Claims that depend on them are listed under Gaps, not as findings.

## 1. Which AI assistants German users and professionals use, and whether AI Overviews and AI Mode are live in Germany

### Takeaway
In Germany, AI Overviews have been live in German since 26 March 2025 and AI Mode since 8 October 2025. In a Bitkom survey from October 2025, half of German internet users at least sometimes use an AI chat instead of a search engine, though only about 12% use it mainly or exclusively. Hard DACH data on B2B buyers using AI to find suppliers is thin. The only fresh figure found is Swiss, from a small sample: 27% overall and 54% at firms with more than 50 employees.

### Cited Findings
- **AI Overviews in Germany:** launched 26 March 2025 in Germany, Belgium, Ireland, Italy, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Spain and Switzerland. In Germany they are available in German and English, only to signed-in users aged 18 and over, and they appear "when Google's systems determine it is the most helpful". — [Google Blog DE, 26.03.2025](https://blog.google/intl/de-de/produkte/suchen-entdecken/uebersicht-mit-ki-start/)
- **AI Mode in Germany:** gradual rollout from 8 October 2025 to almost 50 additional countries, explicitly including Germany, Switzerland, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Poland and Sweden. The rollout added 36 new languages and runs on a search-optimised Gemini 2.5. AI Mode appears as its own tab in Google Search and in the Google app. — [WinFuture, 08.10.2025](https://winfuture.de/news,154115.html)
- **Before October 2025:** Germany and the EU were excluded from the August 2025 expansion of AI Mode to 180+ countries, which was English-only at first. — [onlinemarketing.de, 01.09.2025](https://onlinemarketing.de/technologie/google-ai-mode-deutschland-start-eu-rollout)
- **Bitkom Research, fielded October 2025** (reported 24.11.2025; sample size not stated in the report):
  - Among German internet users: 50% use an AI chat at least sometimes instead of a search engine. 5% use only AI, 7% mainly AI, 13% both equally, 25% mainly search engines and 47% only search engines. (The source reports these shares exactly this way, so they do not add up cleanly.)
  - Ages 16–29: 5% only AI, 11% mainly AI and 20% both equally.
  - 42% of AI search users have received false or made-up information, and 57% check AI answers before using them.
  - Only 36% say AI answers give enough links.
  - On AI Overviews: 24% often use the summary without opening any result, 43% click through to check for themselves, and 26% have seen them but ignore them.
  — [ChannelPartner, 24.11.2025, citing Bitkom](https://www.channelpartner.de/article/4095125/suchmaschinen-verlieren-nutzer-an-ki-chats.html)
- **Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025, Germany** (fieldwork 16–30 January 2025, YouGov): only 4% of adult German internet users use a generative AI chatbot weekly *for news*, and 9–10% in the under-35 groups. This covers news use only and says nothing about product or supplier search. — [Leibniz-HBI, 17.06.2025](https://leibniz-hbi.de/en/hbi-news/pressinfo/german-findings-of-the-reuters-institute-digital-news-report-2025/)
- **B2B purchasing, Switzerland (Carpathia B2B-Monitor 2026)** **[vendor study]**: about 27% of respondents use AI for product and supplier search, and 54% at companies with more than 50 employees. Online survey, April–July 2026, n=146. The question wording is not published, the sample is small and its selection is unclear. — [Netzwoche, 21.08.2026](https://www.netzwoche.ch/news/2026-08-21/groessere-unternehmen-setzen-beim-einkauf-staerker-auf-ki)

### Inferences
- A German studio owner searching Google for "Stoppersocken mit Logo" may now see an AI Overview, provided they are signed in and over 18. Since October 2025 they can also open AI Mode. Mediterrano's Google presence in German therefore feeds two AI surfaces directly.
- The Bitkom split suggests classic search still dominates in Germany, with about 72% mainly or only using search engines. AI chat is a growing parallel channel, not a replacement. For a small supplier, SEO and AEO are the same investment, not a choice between them.
- Pilates and yoga studio owners are typically micro-businesses. The Swiss data points to lower AI use for supplier search at small firms (overall 27% versus 54% at firms with more than 50 employees). That is a reason to temper expectations about lead volume from AI in Germany.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025–2026 source found that gives **market shares of individual assistants (ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Copilot vs Perplexity) in Germany**. The Bitkom article does not break them down. Check Bitkom's original press release, Statista, or the Reuters DNR 2026 (likely published June 2026, not retrieved).
- No German or Austrian figure (as opposed to Swiss) found for the share of B2B buyers using AI for supplier search. Search results such as "51% der B2B-Käufer starten ihre Recherche bei KI" (drweb.de) were not verified and appear to be based on US vendor data.
- Microsoft Copilot usage in Germany: no data found.

## 2. Which search index each assistant uses, and how a German user's location and language affect results

### Takeaway
ChatGPT search shows a strong overlap with Bing's organic results: in a small 2025 study, 87% of its citations appeared in Bing's top 20. Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode run on Google Search. Google and ChatGPT cite largely different domains in Germany: only 18 of their top 50 domains overlap.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT and Bing** **[vendor study, small sample]**: more than 87% of ChatGPT search citations matched Bing's top 20 organic results for the same question, mostly in the top 10. Only 56% matched Google results, with a median Google rank of 17. Some Wikipedia citations did not rank on Bing and may come from training data. Method: about 100 questions and 500+ citations, published 6 February 2025. The authors call the findings "directional". — [Seer Interactive, 06.02.2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- In the same study, the share of queries where ChatGPT triggers a web search was 46% according to Semrush and closer to 30% according to Seer's own research. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- **Google's AI surfaces:** AI Mode runs on a search-optimised version of Gemini 2.5 inside Google Search. — [WinFuture](https://winfuture.de/news,154115.html). AI Overviews link prominently to websites for further reading. — [Google Blog DE](https://blog.google/intl/de-de/produkte/suchen-entdecken/uebersicht-mit-ki-start/)
- **The two engines diverge in Germany** **[vendor study]**: in Ahrefs Brand Radar data for Germany (September 2026), only 18 of the top 50 cited domains are shared between Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT. 32 domains appear only in AIO's top 50 and 32 only in ChatGPT's. 11 of ChatGPT's top 50 were new compared with July 2026, so the lists are volatile. — [Ahrefs DE, AIO vs ChatGPT](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/)
- **Google rankings and ChatGPT mentions** (cited in Ahrefs): Seer found a correlation of 0.65 between Google keyword rankings and ChatGPT brand mentions, and 0.50 for Bing. Domain Rating (DR) correlated at 0.25 and backlinks at 0.10. — [Ahrefs, AI Overview brand correlation](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)

### Inferences
- For a German ChatGPT user, Bing.de ranking for German queries is the most direct lever. Bing Webmaster Tools is already verified, so the next step is to check that the /de/ pages are indexed in Bing and actually rank for "Stoppersocken mit Logo".
- Because ChatGPT seems to reformulate long questions into short search queries (Seer's caveat), the /de/ pages need to rank for short head terms such as "Stoppersocken mit Logo", "Grip Socken bedrucken" and "Pilates Socken Logo". Matching the full natural-language question is not enough.
- Language and location: the Ahrefs German data reflects German-language prompts and shows predominantly .de domains, such as adac.de, test.de and idealo.de. German-language prompts therefore pull mainly German-language sources. That suggests the German pages on mediterranopilates.com/de/ must be the citation target. Spanish or English pages are unlikely to be picked up for German queries. This is an inference, not a measured result.

### Gaps
- **Perplexity's index:** no source retrieved in this session. It is commonly described as having its own crawler (PerplexityBot) plus third-party search results, but that is not verified here.
- **Claude and Brave Search:** not verified in this session.
- **Microsoft Copilot runs on Bing:** generally accepted, but no primary source retrieved.
- **OpenAI's own documentation** (OAI-SearchBot, third-party search providers) could not be fetched. Retry help.openai.com ("Publishers and developers FAQ") and platform.openai.com/docs/bots.
- No study found that measures how geolocation (a German IP) versus prompt language changes the citations ChatGPT shows.

## 3. Which source types and domains get cited for German commercial and supplier queries

### Takeaway
For German-language ChatGPT answers, Wikipedia, reference works, test and consumer portals (test.de, Verbraucherzentrale, idealo, Chip, Computerbild, testberichte.de), retailer and brand sites with plenty of advice content, and Trustpilot dominate. Google AI Overviews in Germany lean much more on YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, Amazon and Kleinanzeigen. Business directories (wlw, Europages, Gelbe Seiten, Kompass) appear in none of the German top-50 lists found.

### Cited Findings
- **ChatGPT Germany, top 50 cited domains** **[vendor study]**. Ahrefs Brand Radar, German prompts with web search, September 2026, 477,276 citations in the top 50:
  - Leaders: de.wikipedia.org 18.56%, adac.de 6.21%, duden.de 5.08%, en.wikipedia.org 4.38%, test.de 3.84%, verbraucherzentrale.de 2.84%, faz.net 2.82%, idealo.de 2.71%.
  - Also in the list: de.trustpilot.com 2.10%, reddit.com 1.80% (#13), computerbild.de 1.75%, chip.de 1.73%, testberichte.de 1.17%, kununu.com 0.78%, otto.de 0.98%.
  - Retailers: dm.de, rewe.de, ikea.com, obi.de, hornbach.de, rossmann.de and edeka.de.
  - YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook are absent from ChatGPT's top 50.
  - The article does not mention business directories.
  - Ahrefs notes that a high DR does not guarantee citations.
  - Article dates are inconsistent: the title has carried "Juli 2026" and "August 2026" in different versions, metadata says published 7 August 2026, and the body says updated 1 October 2026.
  — [Ahrefs DE, Top-50 ChatGPT Deutschland](https://ahrefs.com/blog/de/chatgpt-deutschland-meistzitierte-domains/)
- **Reddit is volatile in ChatGPT DE:** #2 with 10.11% in July 2026, down to #13 with 1.80% in September 2026, with no explanation given. In AIO Reddit stays stable at #4 with 4.51%. — [Ahrefs DE, AIO vs ChatGPT](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/)
- **Google AI Overviews Germany, top 10** **[vendor study]**, September 2026, 6.31 million citations:
  - youtube.com 23.73%
  - de.wikipedia.org 15.70%
  - google.com 12.17% (mostly redirect links)
  - reddit.com 4.51%
  - instagram.com 3.70%
  - facebook.com 3.38%
  - en.wikipedia.org 2.46%
  - ndr.de 1.98%
  - amazon.de 1.85%
  - adac.de 1.78%

  AIO's top 50 also includes kleinanzeigen.de (#21). — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/)
- **Commercial queries:** test and review sites (test.de, verbraucherzentrale.de, idealo.de, trustpilot, computerbild.de, testberichte.de) and eight German retailers are only in ChatGPT's top 50, not AIO's. Ahrefs suggests Google handles many commercial queries through Shopping, local packs and product results rather than AIO citations. Ahrefs' advice for product content is "testing reports, comparisons with clear verdicts, fact-dense text". It also says ChatGPT appears to favour shops with extensive product and advice content. — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/)
- **"Best" queries draw affiliates and listicles:** in Seer's ChatGPT sample, affiliates, news sites and aggregators dominated the citations, probably because most questions contained "best" or "top". — [Seer Interactive, 02/2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)

### Inferences
- The Ahrefs lists are averages across all topics, dominated by consumer, health and automotive questions. For a niche B2B question like "Stoppersocken mit Logo für Pilatesstudio", the top-50 list is not the competitive field. In niche queries ChatGPT probably cites whatever ranks on Bing for the short query: supplier sites, a few comparison articles and possibly marketplaces such as Amazon or Etsy offering custom grip socks. This is an inference and should be verified with our own prompt tests (see section 6).
- Two content types are evidenced for German commercial queries: comparison or test content with a clear verdict, and fact-dense advice pages on shop sites. For Mediterrano this means: (a) the /de/ site should include fact-dense comparison and advice pages, such as minimum order quantity, price per pair, lead time and material, comparable in table form; (b) an external German "Anbieter im Vergleich" article that mentions Mediterrano would carry more weight than a directory listing.
- YouTube is the strongest single source for Google AIO in Germany (23.73%). A short German-language YouTube video, for example "Stoppersocken mit Studio-Logo: so läuft die Bestellung", is a realistic AIO lever. For ChatGPT it does almost nothing, since YouTube ranks #163 there.
- Directories (wlw.de, Europages, Gelbe Seiten, Kompass) do not appear in any German top-50 list. Their likely value is indirect: entity consistency and brand mentions, plus Bing ranking for long-tail B2B queries. They are probably not a direct citation source. This is an inference, and no German directory citation data was found.

### Gaps
- No German citation data was found specifically for **B2B supplier queries** ("Anbieter", "Hersteller", "Lieferant"). The Ahrefs lists mix all intents.
- No data was found on citations of **wlw.de, Europages, Gelbe Seiten or Kompass** in AI answers. Directories are not named in any source found.
- No German citation data was found from **Sistrix, Peec AI, Profound or Otterly** within the call budget. Peec AI, a Berlin company, is likely to have DE data; worth checking in a follow-up.
- No data on **gutefrage.net** in AI answers. It is not in ChatGPT's top 50, and the AIO top 50 was only partly retrieved.

## 4. Evidence on what increases citations, ranked by strength of evidence

### Takeaway
The strongest available signal is not backlinks but **brand mentions on the web**, combined with **ranking in the underlying search index** (Bing for ChatGPT, Google for AIO). Content-level levers such as statistics, quotations and source references are supported by a peer-reviewed lab study. llms.txt shows no measurable effect. All of the large data studies are correlational vendor studies.

### Cited Findings
**Stronger evidence (large datasets or peer review; still correlational or lab-based):**
- **Brand mentions over backlinks** **[vendor study, correlation]**. Ahrefs, 75,000 brands, Spearman correlations with AI Overview brand visibility (published 26.05.2025):

  | Factor | Spearman correlation |
  |---|---|
  | Branded web mentions | 0.664 |
  | Branded anchors | 0.527 |
  | Branded search volume | 0.392 |
  | Domain Rating | 0.326 |
  | Referring domains | 0.295 |
  | Backlinks | 0.218 |
  | Number of pages | 0.17 |

  Brands in the top quartile for web mentions averaged 169 AIO mentions. The next quartile averaged 14, and the bottom two quartiles averaged 0–3. Sample limits: DR above 40 only, and about 26% of brands with zero mentions were excluded. Ahrefs itself warns "correlation ≠ causation". — [Ahrefs, 75K brands](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- **ChatGPT:** Google rankings correlate at 0.65 with ChatGPT brand mentions and Bing rankings at 0.50; backlinks only at 0.10 (Seer, via Ahrefs). Brand search volume correlates at 0.334 with ChatGPT mentions (Kevin Indig, via Ahrefs). — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- **Ranking in the search index:** 87% of ChatGPT citations come from Bing's top 20, and a source ranked 5th still gets cited. — [Seer, 02/2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- **Content-level GEO (Princeton/IIT Delhi, peer-reviewed at KDD 2024)** **[pre-2025]**:
  - Adding quotations, adding statistics and citing sources gave 30–40% relative improvement in "Position-Adjusted Word Count" and 15–30% in "Subjective Impression".
  - Keyword stuffing scored below the baseline, and about 10% worse on Perplexity.
  - Lower-ranked sources gained most: "Cite Sources" lifted a 5th-ranked source by 115.1%, while the top-ranked source lost 30.3% on average.
  - Combining fluency optimisation with statistics beat any single method by more than 5.5%.
  - Limitations: a lab benchmark (GEO-bench, 10k queries) plus a test on Perplexity, English-language, measured in 2023–2024.
  — [arXiv 2311.09735 (v3, 28.06.2024; KDD '24)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)

**Weak or no evidence:**
- **llms.txt** **[vendor study]**:
  - Of about 38,000 domains with a valid llms.txt, about 97% received zero requests in May 2026.
  - Retrieval bots from ChatGPT and Perplexity accounted for about 1% of requests, and PerplexityBot fetched less often than Slackbot.
  - An SE Ranking analysis of 300,000 domains found no relationship between having llms.txt and how often a site is cited.
  - Google's John Mueller says llms.txt is "not done for search" and called it a "temporary crutch" for coding tools.
  — [Search Engine Journal, 16.06.2026, on Ahrefs data](https://searchenginejournal.com/97-of-llms-txt-files-got-no-requests-ahrefs-data-shows/579478)
- **Number of pages on the site:** correlation only 0.17 with AIO visibility. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)

### Inferences
Mediterrano's levers in Germany, ranked by expected speed of effect. The ordering is an inference from the evidence above, not a measured result:
1. **Days to 2 weeks:** get the /de/ pages indexed in Bing and Google. Submit URLs through IndexNow or Bing Webmaster Tools URL Submission and GSC URL inspection, then check they are indexed. Without an index entry no citation is possible, so this is a precondition (see section 5).
2. **2–6 weeks:** content edits to existing /de/ pages: fact-dense blocks (minimum order quantity, price per pair, lead time, shipping to DE, material, all from the brand-facts file), a comparison table, Q&A structure, and clear source references. Evidence: GEO paper (lab) and Ahrefs' advice "comparisons with clear verdicts, fact-dense text". Effect depends on Bing and Google re-crawling and on ranking.
3. **1–3 months:** Bing ranking for short German head terms. This is the most direct lever for ChatGPT, per Seer.
4. **1–6 months:** German brand mentions outside our own site: inclusion in German "Anbieter im Vergleich" articles, Pilates and yoga trade media, studio software partners, a Trustpilot DE profile (de.trustpilot.com is #11 in ChatGPT DE), and German directories for entity consistency. This is the strongest correlational signal (0.664), but the slowest to build. Mediterrano currently has zero German mentions.
5. **No expected effect:** llms.txt. Keep it, since it costs nothing, but expect nothing from it.
- **Schema / JSON-LD:** no study found that isolates schema as a citation factor. Treat it as a hygiene factor for entity clarity, not as a proven lever. This is an inference; see Gaps.
- **Constraint from the project rules:** no testimonials and no client numbers. Trustpilot reviews can only be collected once real customers exist.

### Gaps
- No controlled study found on **schema/JSON-LD → AI citations** in 2025–2026.
- No study found on **freshness** (publish or update date) as a citation factor with hard numbers.
- The **Ahrefs mentions study** covers AIO, not ChatGPT, and only brands with DR above 40. Whether it transfers to a small brand with almost no mentions is unknown.
- No German-language version of the GEO experiment was found.
- **Local signals** (Google Business Profile, a German address) for B2B supplier queries: no data found.

## 5. How fast a new page gets into Bing and Google, and therefore into ChatGPT and Perplexity, and what IndexNow actually speeds up

### Takeaway
The key mechanism is evidenced: ChatGPT cites what ranks in Bing, so a page must first be in Bing's index. Hard, independent numbers on days versus weeks were **not found** in this session, and Bing's own IndexNow page could not be fetched.

### Cited Findings
- ChatGPT citations largely follow Bing's organic top 20 (87%). Bing indexing is therefore a precondition for ChatGPT citation of new pages. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- ChatGPT probably also draws some answers, such as certain Wikipedia citations, from training data rather than from live search. — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results)
- In Ahrefs' May 2026 logs, retrieval bots from ChatGPT and Perplexity were a small share of requests for llms.txt. That says nothing about how often they crawl normal pages. — [SEJ/Ahrefs](https://searchenginejournal.com/97-of-llms-txt-files-got-no-requests-ahrefs-data-shows/579478)

### Inferences
- IndexNow (an inference based on the protocol's general function, not verified against a primary source in this session) only signals to participating engines (Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver and others; **not Google**) that a URL is new or changed. It speeds up *discovery and re-crawling*, but it guarantees neither indexing nor ranking. For Mediterrano the concrete gain is that new or changed /de/ pages reach Bing faster, and through Bing reach ChatGPT and Copilot. For Google, use the GSC URL inspection tool and the sitemap instead.
- Mediterrano has the IndexNow key file but submits nothing automatically on deploy. That is the cheapest, fastest-acting technical fix available. Possible approaches: a deploy hook that POSTs to api.indexnow.org, or manual bulk submission through Bing Webmaster Tools.

### Gaps
- **Typical time from publication to Bing index and to a first ChatGPT citation:** no reliable measurement found. The search results were mostly agency blogs without methodology and were not used.
- **Bing's official IndexNow documentation** (bing.com/indexnow) and Microsoft statements on IndexNow and Copilot freshness could not be fetched. Retry, or have someone read it manually.
- Google indexing speed for new pages from a small domain: no 2025–2026 data found.

## 6. How to measure AI visibility in German cheaply and repeatably, and how to recognise AI-referred leads

### Takeaway
ChatGPT appends `utm_source=chatgpt.com` to cited links, and since 13 May 2026 GA4 has a native "AI Assistant" channel. Visits without a referrer or UTM tag still show up as Direct. AI Overviews and AI Mode traffic is **not** separable in GA4; it stays under Organic Search. For citation measurement, a fixed German-language prompt set tested manually each month is the cheapest repeatable method.

### Cited Findings
- **utm_source on ChatGPT links:**
  - ChatGPT Search launched on 31 October 2024. OpenAI documents the `chatgpt.com` source tag.
  - Since 13 June 2025 the tag also appears on links under "More" sources, not only on inline citations.
  - The tag is part of the destination URL, so it survives a hidden referrer. A redirect that does not pass on parameters can drop it.
  — [Clickport, 23.04.2026, updated 18.09.2026](https://clickport.io/blog/chatgpt-direct-traffic-ga4)
- **GA4 native AI channel:**
  - Since 13 May 2026 GA4 has a default channel "AI Assistant" with medium `ai-assistant`.
  - AI Overviews and AI Mode traffic remains under Organic Search.
  - Custom channel groups are possible: Admin → Data settings → Channel groups. Use "Source matches regex", place the rule above Referral, and don't require a medium, because ChatGPT only sets `utm_source`.
  - Example domains for the regex: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com.
  - A custom channel cannot recover visits that recorded no source.
  — [Clickport](https://clickport.io/blog/chatgpt-direct-traffic-ga4)
- AI answers are volatile: 11 of ChatGPT DE's top 50 domains changed between July and September 2026, and Reddit fell from #2 to #13. Measure repeatedly; a single check is not enough. — [Ahrefs DE](https://ahrefs.com/de/blog/ai-overviews-vs-chatgpt-quellen/)

### Inferences
**Manual monthly protocol (free).**
- **Prompt set:** 15–20 fixed German-language prompts in three groups.
  - (a) Direct supplier questions: "Wo kann ich Stoppersocken mit dem Logo meines Pilatesstudios bestellen?", "Welcher Anbieter macht Grip Socks mit Logo für Pilatesstudios ab kleiner Menge?"
  - (b) Comparison questions: "Beste Anbieter für Pilates-Socken mit Logo".
  - (c) Informational questions: "Lohnt sich Merchandise für ein Pilatesstudio?"
- **Engines:** ChatGPT (search on, logged out or a fresh account), Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google with AIO and AI Mode. Run from a German connection or with a VPN set to DE, in a fresh session, 2–3 runs per prompt.
- **Log for each run:** whether Mediterrano is named (yes/no), whether it is cited with a link (yes/no), position, competitors named, and the cited domains. The cited domains are the outreach list for lever 4.
- Extend the existing `claude/aeo-signalen-log.md` with a DE section.

**GA4.**
- Turn on the "AI Assistant" channel or a custom AI channel group.
- Check that all form and landing paths pass on query parameters, so that utm_source=chatgpt.com survives.
- Store `utm_source` and the referrer as hidden fields in the mockup form. AI leads then become visible at lead level, not just as sessions.
- Add a free-text field "Wie haben Sie uns gefunden?" with the option "ChatGPT / KI-Assistent". That catches AI influence that only shows up as Direct traffic or a brand search.

**Paid tools.**
- Ahrefs Brand Radar has DE data (it was the source of the studies above). Peec AI, Profound and Otterly offer prompt tracking per country.
- No pricing or DE-feature check was done in this session.

### Gaps
- No verification of **Peec AI, Otterly or Profound** DE support and pricing in 2026.
- No reliable source found on how often **ChatGPT app traffic** arrives without a referrer. Clickport explicitly does not confirm this.
- Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot: no source verified on whether they append their own UTM parameters.
