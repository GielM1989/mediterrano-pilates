# Off-site listing and mention channels in Germany / DACH for Mediterrano Pilates (as of 8 October 2026)

Scope: where a small Spanish B2B supplier (Ecomtierra Group SL, no German address, no customers yet) can get German-language listings or mentions quickly, to reach Pilates/yoga studio owners and to build brand mentions that Google, Bing and AI assistants pick up. Germany first, Austria/Switzerland second.

Method note: about 40 searches and fetches on 8 Oct 2026. Several primary pages could not be fetched (IHK München blocked, Kompass registration page 404, Wikidata and the Faire blog needed permissions that timed out). Those points are listed under Gaps and are not presented as facts.

## 1. B2B directories: wlw/Visable, Europages, Kompass, Gelbe Seiten / Das Örtliche, Bing Places, Google Business Profile, Crunchbase, Wikidata

### Takeaway
None of the big generic directories confirmed a quick free listing for a Spanish S.L. without a German address. wlw (Visable) only accepts companies with a branch in DE/AT/CH, and others have to ask Customer Care. Google Business Profile excludes online-only and shipping-only businesses. Europages (run by Visable) offers "free registration" for suppliers but publishes no tier details. Gelbe Seiten offers a free basic entry and states no German-address rule, but the entry is address-based. Crunchbase lets any authenticated user add a profile. The most useful quick, free and confirmed DACH listing is in a trade-media directory (BODYMEDIA Branchenbuch, see section 2), not in a generic directory.

### Cited Findings

Provided, however, is that your company has a branch in one of the following countries" (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). ### Inferences
- **wlw:** worth one email to info@visable.com asking for a listing as a foreign supplier without a DACH branch. The reply decides the matter, and it costs about 15 minutes.
- **Europages:** the most plausible generic B2B directory for a Spanish company, since it is pan-European and supplier registration is labelled "free". Whether a useful free profile exists has to be checked by registering.
- **Google Business Profile:** a German GBP is not available, because Mediterrano does not meet customers face to face in Germany. A German one should not be attempted (a fake address would breach the guidelines). Any GBP has to stay tied to a real Spanish location that meets the eligibility rules.
- **Gelbe Seiten / Das Örtliche:** low value. They are local consumer directories, the entry is built around a German street address, and studio owners do not look for B2B merch suppliers there. Skip unless it turns out to accept a Spanish address at no cost.
- **Crunchbase:** a free, quick entity signal (company name, CIF-backed legal entity, website, description). It helps entity consistency across languages but does not reach DACH studio owners.

### Gaps
- Whether Europages has a free supplier tier in 2026, and what it includes: the pricing page was not found.
- Kompass free-tier details and foreign-registration rules: the registration page returned 404.
- Whether Bing Places for Business accepts a listing with a Spanish address for a business that ships across Europe: no primary source found.
- Wikidata notability criteria: the official page (wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Notability) could not be fetched. From prior knowledge, not verified this session: an item needs a sitelink, or must be "clearly identifiable" and described by "serious and publicly available references", or must fulfil a "structural need". With zero independent external mentions, a Mediterrano item would probably be at risk of deletion until independent coverage exists (for example a trade-media press notice).
- Das Örtliche: its relationship to Gelbe Seiten data and its requirements were not verified.

## 5. Direct outreach: is cold email to German (and AT/CH) studios legal, and what is the safe route?

### Takeaway
In Germany, advertising emails to businesses require **prior express consent**, the same as for consumers. This also covers messages via social media and messengers, including LinkedIn InMail. Even an email asking whether the recipient wants to receive advertising counts as advertising. **Personally addressed postal letters are generally permitted.** B2B phone calls need "presumed consent" based on a concrete business connection. The safe route: postal mailings, inbound-led contact (directory and trade-media listings, website forms), LinkedIn connection requests without a sales pitch, and asking for consent before sending any offer.

### Cited Findings
**Germany: email and electronic messages**
- Email advertising without prior express consent is "grundsätzlich unzulässig", and this applies to B2B as well as B2C. The same rule covers SMS, MMS and messages sent through social media or messaging services. — [IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben](https://www.ihk.de/bodensee-oberschwaben/recht/werbung-und-vertrieb/was-ist-erlaubt-1943228)
- The legal concept of advertising is very broad. Even asking "ob ein Kunde künftig Werbung erhalten möchte" can itself count as advertising. — [IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben](https://www.ihk.de/bodensee-oberschwaben/recht/werbung-und-vertrieb/was-ist-erlaubt-1943228)
- Existing-customer exception (§7 Abs. 3 UWG): email advertising for similar goods is allowed if the customer was told about this use when the address was collected and an opt-out notice is in every email. — [IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben](https://www.ihk.de/bodensee-oberschwaben/recht/werbung-und-vertrieb/was-ist-erlaubt-1943228)

**Germany: phone and post**
- Phone: "genügt für Werbeanrufe gegenüber Gewerbetreibenden (B2B) eine mutmaßliche Einwilligung". That presumed consent needs a concrete business connection; a phone number listed on a website is not enough, the caller bears the risk of misjudging it, and suppressed caller ID is not allowed. — [IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben](https://www.ihk.de/bodensee-oberschwaben/recht/werbung-und-vertrieb/was-ist-erlaubt-1943228)
- Post: personally addressed advertising letters and unaddressed mailings are generally permitted unless the recipient objected (for example a "keine Werbung" sign). Letters should be recognisable as advertising. — [IHK Bodensee-Oberschwaben](https://www.ihk.de/bodensee-oberschwaben/recht/werbung-und-vertrieb/was-ist-erlaubt-1943228)

**Germany: LinkedIn and XING**
- Advertising via InMail to other members of a business network is an "unzumutbare Belästigung und daher unzulässig" without prior express consent. Being connected is not automatically consent ("Die bloße Zustimmung zur ... Vernetzung ... kann nicht zwingend als Zustimmung" gelten). The article cites **AG Düsseldorf, judgment of 20 Nov 2025, Az. 23 C 120/25**, confirming that presumed or implied consent is not sufficient. Its suggested approach is connection requests and personal messages first, with consent for advertising asked for later. — [IT-Recht Kanzlei: Werbe-Mails via XING/LinkedIn](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/werbe-mails-xing-linkedin.html)
- Further reading, not fetched: IHK Darmstadt on a court drawing a "klare Grenze" for cold email advertising, and a report on an Amtsgericht banning automated LinkedIn mails to contacts. — [IHK Darmstadt](https://www.ihk.de/darmstadt/servicemarken/news/service/kalte-werbung-per-e-mail-gericht-zieht-klare-grenze-7046700); [boerse-express](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/linkedin-werbung-amtsgericht-verbietet-automatische-mails-an-kontakte-924704)

**Switzerland**
- BAKOM: automatic mass advertising by email, SMS, fax or phone is "allowed only if recipients have explicitly given their consent". There is an exception for same-type products after a purchase, and the sender must be identifiable and offer a free opt-out. The page does not say whether business recipients are covered or whether individual (non-mass) emails are. — [BAKOM: When is mass sending allowed](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/when-is-mass-sending-allowed)

### Inferences
- **For Germany, no unsolicited sales emails or Instagram/LinkedIn DMs containing offers.** That includes "may I send you our catalogue?" emails.
- **Lower-risk options, in order:**
  1. Inbound: listings and press, so studios contact Mediterrano first.
  2. Personally addressed postal mailings to studios, for example a card with a sample or a mockup offer and a QR code to the /de/ form. The card must be recognisable as advertising.
  3. LinkedIn or Instagram connection or follow without a pitch. Engage with their content, and only send an offer after they ask or explicitly agree.
  4. B2B phone calls only where a concrete business connection exists. For a foreign supplier with no relationship this is risky.
- **Austria:** treat it like Germany (consent first) until verified.
- **Switzerland:** mass email requires consent. Individual emails are a grey area; treat them conservatively.
- This is not legal advice. The IHK and law-firm pages are secondary summaries, and a lawyer should confirm before any campaign.

### Gaps
- Austria (§174 TKG 2021): the WKO page fetched did not contain the rule. From prior knowledge, not verified this session: Austria requires prior consent for advertising emails to businesses as well. Verify on RIS or the WKO DSGVO4KMU page.
- Whether a single, individually researched, non-promotional email (for example a press or partnership enquiry to an association or magazine) is treated differently. Editorial pitches to media are generally not "advertising" to customers, but no source was found confirming this.
- Swiss rules for individual (non-mass) B2B emails.

## 6. Which of these show up in Google results or AI answers for German supplier queries?

### Takeaway
There is no DACH- or German-language-specific evidence. Two 2026 studies (one by a directory vendor with a conflict of interest, one about local businesses in the US/UK/AU) suggest that directories and list pages are a large share of what AI engines cite for supplier and business queries. The suggested drivers are a structured, one-entity-per-entry format and deep coverage of a category, more than domain authority. Bing data feeds several AI assistants, which raises the value of Bing-indexed listings.

### Cited Findings
**Citations.press study (vendor press release)**
- Directories supplied **41% of citations on supplier-selection prompts**, against 18% for brand-owned pages. 68% of directory citations came from list pages rather than individual profiles.
- Directories with one entity per entry and consistent attributes were cited **3.1 times more often** than free-text listings.
- The correlation with domain authority was weak (Spearman 0.19); coverage depth within a category was a stronger predictor.
- Method: 3,850 prompts on ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity and AI Overviews, run 10 Jun to 22 Aug 2026.
- **Caveat:** Citations.press operates the test directories, the release is a vendor press release, and the results are not independently replicated.
- Source: [Directories supply 41% of AI citations (press release)](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/pr-news/directories-supply-41-of-ai-citations-on-supplier-selection-prompts-new-geo-and-aeo-study-finds)

**BrightLocal study (local businesses in US, UK and Australia, Aug 2026)**
- Google Business Profile was the most-cited source (about 28.6% of citations) on Google AI Overviews and AI Mode. Yelp was top for ChatGPT.
- Facebook was cited for 93% of businesses. LinkedIn accounted for only 0.23% of citations but appeared for about 30% of businesses.
- The study covers local consumer businesses, not B2B suppliers or DACH. It also contains internal discrepancies (1,355 vs 1,365 locations; GBP 28.5% vs 28.63%).
- Source: [BrightLocal: AI directory sources](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/)

**DACH-specific signals**
- A DACH SEO guide (2026) says several AI systems use Bing data, which raises the importance of a Bing Places listing. — [blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/de/wissen/seo-geo/lokales-seo/branchenverzeichnisse-deutschland-die-30-wichtigsten-eintraege.md)
- The BODYMEDIA online advertorial includes an "SEO check", and the website has about 365,400 unique visitors a year, so its pages are built to be indexed. — [BODYMEDIA Mediadaten 2026](https://www.bodymedia.de/fileadmin/service/bodymedia-mediadaten-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Sector directories with structured supplier entries are the format most likely to be cited for "Lieferant für Grip-Socken mit Logo" type prompts.** For DACH that means the BODYMEDIA Branchenbuch, Europages and wlw (if accepted). This is extrapolated from the studies above, not from DACH data.
- **Consistent entity data across all listings** (legal name Ecomtierra Group SL, brand Mediterrano Pilates, website, products, minimum 50 pairs, free mockup in 24 hours, free shipping, ships to all of Europe and worldwide) is the main lever. The listing text should state the facts as extractable German sentences.
- **Google Business Profile is the top source for local AI answers but is not available for a German presence** (see section 1). Its weight should not be expected to carry over to German supplier queries.

### Gaps
- No German-language test of which domains Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT or Perplexity actually cite for queries such as "Grip Socken mit Logo bestellen", "Pilates Studio Merchandise Lieferant" or "Stoppersocken mit Logo B2B". This could be checked manually by running these prompts and logging the citations (not done in this session).
- Whether wlw, Europages or BODYMEDIA Branchenbuch pages rank in German Google results for these queries: not checked.
