# English-for-Europe opportunity for Mediterrano Pilates (custom grip socks for studios), October 2026

Scope note: this research covers English-language Google results and AI answers for studios in European countries that have no dedicated Mediterrano language version (i.e. not ES/DE/NL). Research date: 14-10-2026. Several findings come from earlier internal project research (project docs dated 29-09 to 07-10-2026), which are cited as such and which themselves cite external sources. Live SERP and AI-answer checks for the exact English queries could NOT be run in this session (no SERP tool, no VPN). Competitor positions for those queries therefore rest on earlier project research plus vendor pages, not on a fresh ranking check.

Internal lead evidence (from the brief, not externally verifiable): in the last two weeks one mockup request came from a pilates studio in Sweden (landed on /en/) and one from a studio in Cyprus (in English). That is n=2; it proves the channel works, not its size.

## 1. In which European countries do studio owners search and use AI in English for business purchases?

### Takeaway
English proficiency is "very high" in the Netherlands, the Nordics, Portugal, Austria/Germany, Croatia, Belgium, Poland, Slovakia and Romania, and "high" in Greece and Latvia, while Cyprus, France, Spain and Italy are "moderate". Generative-AI use by individuals is well above the EU average (32.7%) in Norway, Denmark, Estonia, Malta, Finland, Ireland, the Netherlands and Greece. No source measures what share of B2B purchase searches in these countries are done in English, so "searches in English" is an inference from proficiency, AI use and the two leads.

### Cited Findings
- EF EPI 2025 (released Nov 2025, test data 2024), scores and bands. Very high: Netherlands 624 (#1 globally), Croatia 617, Austria 616, Germany 615, Norway 613, Portugal 612, Denmark 611, Sweden 609, Belgium 608, Slovakia 606, Romania 605, Finland 603, Poland 600. High: Latvia 599, Bulgaria 594, Greece 592, Hungary 590, Czechia 582, Switzerland 564, Estonia 561. Moderate: Lithuania 543, Spain 540, France 539, Cyprus 537, Italy 513. Ranks were derived by position in the table — [Wikipedia, EF English Proficiency Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EF_English_Proficiency_Index); official edition: [EF EPI 2025](https://www.ef.com/in/epi/)
- Ireland, Malta, Slovenia and Luxembourg are not in the EF EPI 2025 table (Ireland and Malta have English as an official language, so they are not tested) — [Wikipedia, EF EPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EF_English_Proficiency_Index)
- Eurostat 2025: 32.7% of EU individuals aged 16-74 used generative AI tools in 2025; 15.1% used it for work and 25.1% for personal purposes (as cited by a secondary source; not re-checked against Eurostat tables) — [Adapt Worldwide, citing Eurostat Dec 2025](https://www.adaptworldwide.com/2026/06/22/ai-model-usage-in-europe-2025-2026-which-countries-use-chatgpt-claude-and-generative-ai-most/); Eurostat release referenced there: ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251216-3
- Generative-AI use by individuals in 2025, by country (same secondary source citing Eurostat): Norway 56%, Denmark 48%, Estonia 47%, Finland 46%, Malta 46%, Netherlands 45%, Ireland 45%, Greece 44%, Spain 38%, France 37%, Germany 32%. No figures were given for Sweden, Cyprus, Portugal, Latvia or Lithuania; Sweden only appears at enterprise level (35% of enterprises use AI) — [Adapt Worldwide](https://www.adaptworldwide.com/2026/06/22/ai-model-usage-in-europe-2025-2026-which-countries-use-chatgpt-claude-and-generative-ai-most/); see also [Our World in Data, share of Europeans using generative AI](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-europe-using-generative-ai) and [Euronews, AI use at work in Europe (Mar 2026)](https://euronews.com/business/2026/03/19/ai-use-at-work-in-europe-which-countries-use-generative-ai-tools-most-and-why)
- Language preference when buying online (B2C, not B2B): CSA Research/Kantar 2020, 8,709 consumers in 29 countries. 39% prefer native-language sites (67% among weak English readers); English-confident respondents were 6.81 times more likely to buy on English sites than those with no English; 66% would choose a product with information in their language; 75% more likely to rebuy with native-language customer care, including 60% of the most English-confident. No country breakdown public; vendor (language-services) research — [CSA Research](https://csa-research.com/Blogs-Events/Blog/customer-online-buying-experience)
- Old agency study (Greenlight, 2012, n=500, vendor research): over three quarters of European respondents searched in more than one language. Too old and too small to rely on — [Travolution](https://www.travolution.com/news/three-quarters-searching-in-more-than-one-language-claims-study/)

### Inferences
- Best English-language candidates (high proficiency + high AI use + no Mediterrano language version): Nordics (SE, DK, NO, FI), Ireland, Malta, Estonia/Latvia, Greece. Cyprus scores only "moderate" on EF EPI, but the Cyprus lead arrived in English, and English is widely used in Cypriot business (general knowledge, not sourced here). Treat Cyprus as English-addressable for B2B, with lower certainty.
- Portugal is a special case: EF EPI 612 ("very high", #6), so English pages can reach Portuguese studios. But earlier project research found Portuguese and Spanish quotes rejected on price (see Q4), so English reach in Portugal does not equal conversions.
- The CSA data suggests even English-confident buyers prefer native-language support. English pages can win the first contact, but a reply in the studio's own language (or at least an offer to do so) may help conversion. This is an inference from B2C data.
- Netherlands is very English-proficient, but Mediterrano already has /nl/ and earlier research put NL in maintenance mode because local suppliers sell at 1.85-4.95 € (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No source found for the share of B2B purchase searches done in English per country (Google Trends or Search Console per-country query language would be the real measure; recommend checking the /en/ GSC country report).
- Eurostat individual gen-AI figures for Sweden, Cyprus, Portugal, Latvia and Lithuania were not retrieved; the primary Eurostat table should be checked.
- CSA B2B "Can't Read, Won't Buy" country data is behind a paywall; not retrieved.

## 2. Who ranks and gets cited for English supplier queries, and is there room for an EU-based supplier?

### Takeaway
The English niche is crowded with US-based specialists (PilatesGoods, Makers Garments, Sock Club, hipSwan, Studio Grip Socks, Sockrates, Deadsoxy, Gabby & Threads, Ethos Merch) plus marketplaces (Etsy, Faire, Alibaba). Few EU-based suppliers present themselves in English to studios, and none found positions explicitly on "EU shipping, no customs, VAT handled". That is a real positioning gap, but it was not confirmed with a live ranking check for the exact queries.

### Cited Findings
- Earlier project research (01-10-2026) found about ten established English-language specialists: PilatesGoods, Makers Garments, Made by Cooper, hipSwan, Studio Grip Socks, Sockrates, Deadsoxy, plus Etsy/Faire/Alibaba. An English ChatGPT query from a US test user never named Mediterrano. The brand had zero English or German external mentions. The same research cites Ahrefs (75,000 brands, vendor study) on brand mentions correlating more strongly with AI visibility (0.66) than backlinks (0.22) — [project doc: ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01](claude/ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01.md)
- PilatesGoods: 50 pairs, 48 h mockup, free worldwide shipping; called "the direct international benchmark" in earlier research — [project doc: onderzoek-25-merchniches](claude/onderzoek-25-merchniches-ai-vindbaar-2026-09-29.md), citing [PilatesGoods](https://pilatesgoods.com/)
- Merchery markets grip socks explicitly to "yoga, Pilates, barre" and was classified as EU-based in earlier research; Gabby & Threads and Ethos Merch (US) are present for barre — [project doc: onderzoek-25-merchniches](claude/onderzoek-25-merchniches-ai-vindbaar-2026-09-29.md), citing merchery.co/shop/grip-socks and [Ethos Merch barre](https://ethos-merch.com/pages/studio-barre)
- Sock Club (custom.sockclub.com): minimum 120 pairs; $11.94/pair at 120-239 down to $6.71 at 7,500+; ships 15 business days after approval plus transit; free design proof in 1-3 business days; made in USA (Austin area per reviews). Page says nothing about EU shipping, duties or VAT — [Sock Club custom grip socks](https://custom.sockclub.com/products/custom-grip-socks)
- Price benchmarks gathered earlier (per pair, excl. VAT): Makers Garments (US) $13.99 at 50; Sockrates from $9.75, min. 300; blaenk (CH) min. 200; Spreeprint (DE) 6.80 € at 100 + 70 € setup; LV Merch (AT) from 9.90 €, min. 100; APIC (FR) 11.34 € at 100 — [project doc: landenkeuze-prijsbereidheid-2026-10-05](claude/landenkeuze-prijsbereidheid-2026-10-05.md)
- Generic EU sock makers also appear in English results: logosocke.com (embroidered crew socks "from 50 pairs") and an EU Enterprise Europe Network listing of a company producing promotional logo socks with silicone grip — [Logosocke](https://logosocke.com/en/embroidered-crew-socks); [EEN partnering listing](https://een.ec.europa.eu/partnering-opportunities/company-specialized-production-promotional-logo-socks-and-silicone-grip)
- Content farms rank for wholesale grip-sock queries with 2026 "buyer's guides" (closo.co), showing that informational buyer-guide formats are being targeted in English — [Closo, Pilates socks wholesale 2026](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/pilates-socks-wholesale); [Closo, wholesale grip socks](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/wholesale-grip-socks)

### Inferences
- US suppliers rarely state EU shipping times, import VAT or customs handling on product pages (seen at Sock Club). For an EU studio, ordering from the US means import VAT and possibly duties. Within the EU customs union there are no customs formalities. For B2B sales with a valid VAT ID, intra-community supplies are normally zero-rated, with the buyer self-accounting VAT (often loosely called "reverse charge"). This is general EU VAT knowledge, not verified from a primary source in this session; earlier project notes flag it for confirmation by the gestor. If confirmed, "EU supplier, no customs, no import VAT surprises, VAT-ID invoicing" is a factual differentiator US competitors cannot claim.
- Mediterrano's 50-pair minimum, 24 h mockup and free EU shipping match or beat PilatesGoods (50 / 48 h) and beat Sock Club (120) and Sockrates (300) on minimums. Low minimums plus EU logistics is the defensible angle. The angle is not price, because US benchmarks are dollar-priced and EU local rivals undercut in NL/PL.
- For AI answers, the gap is mostly external evidence: Mediterrano has no English third-party mentions, and the AI search layer relies on them.

### Gaps
- No live Google or Bing SERP was captured for "custom grip socks for pilates studio", "pilates studio merchandise supplier Europe", "branded grip socks EU" or "custom grip socks low minimum". Positions and AI citations for these exact queries are unverified. Recommended next step: run them in Google (gl=se, ie, dk) and in ChatGPT/Perplexity with a Swedish or Irish VPN, then record who is cited.
- Studio Grip Socks, hipSwan, Deadsoxy, Gabby & Threads and Merchery terms (MOQ, EU shipping) were not re-fetched in this session.
- No English-language EU supplier was found that explicitly addresses VAT ID / reverse charge for studios; absence of evidence is not proof of absence.

## 3. Which English content types would be most useful: country pages, an "EU studios" page, comparison/buyer guides, listicles?

### Takeaway
Google's spam policy names "pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse. Templated "custom grip socks Sweden/Denmark/Finland" pages are therefore risky unless each carries country-unique substance (VAT/invoicing, delivery time, currency, local retail-price context, local FAQ). One substantive "EU studios" hub plus a few genuinely distinct country pages for proven-lead markets, plus buyer-guide and comparison content, is the safer, more citeable mix.

### Cited Findings
- Google defines doorway abuse as "when sites or pages are created to rank for specific, similar search queries" that "lead users to intermediate pages that aren't as useful as the final destination". Examples include "having multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" and "creating substantially similar pages that are closer to search results than a clearly defined, browseable hierarchy" — [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- "Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings" and not helping users — [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)
- Earlier project plan (01-10) already scheduled "country pages UK/IE/DACH with unique FAQ" and an EN/DE buyer guide in days 31-60, plus external listings (Europages, Faire, Crunchbase, Wikidata, LinkedIn in English) — [project doc: ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01](claude/ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01.md)
- An English supplier-choice guide already exists at /en/blog/choose-a-custom-grip-socks-supplier/ (built v31), along with an EN pricing/margins page — [project doc: zoekwoorden-en-clusters](zoekwoorden-en-clusters.md)
- Competitors and content sites publish English "buyer's guide 2026" content on wholesale grip socks, confirming the format is contested in SERPs — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/grippy-socks-wholesale); [Sock Club blog, custom grip socks launch](https://custom.sockclub.com/blogs/custom-grip-socks-launch)

### Inferences
- Highest-value page (inference): an English "Custom grip socks for studios in the EU" hub. It should cover the shipping countries list, delivery time per region, VAT ID / zero-rated invoicing (once confirmed), no customs inside the EU, non-EU notes (UK, CH, NO: import VAT), currency, and the 50-pair minimum. It answers the exact question a Swedish or Cypriot owner asks an AI, and it is extractable.
- Country pages only where there is unique substance and demand evidence: Sweden (lead + 133 studios in a directory + 16-27 € member retail), Ireland (EU, native English, 386 pilates in a scrape, high price level), possibly Cyprus/Malta combined as "island EU" logistics. A Denmark/Norway page makes sense only if Norway's non-EU VAT handling is explained. Each page needs real local facts (VAT rate, delivery days, currency, local retail-price context) or it falls under doorway risk.
- Comparison content: a factual "EU vs US grip sock suppliers: customs, VAT, delivery time, minimums" guide fits the AEO question format and the differentiator. It must avoid naming competitor prices on the site; an earlier brand rule says never show competitor prices.
- English listicles ("best custom grip sock suppliers in Europe") on Mediterrano's own site carry little weight for AI. Being included in third-party English listicles and directories is what matters, per the brand-mention correlation cited in Q2.

### Gaps
- No study found that measures whether country-specific English pages (e.g. "X Sweden" in English) rank for Swedish users versus a single EU page; the recommendation relies on Google's policy text and general practice.
- English search volumes for country-modified queries ("grip socks Sweden", "custom pilates socks Ireland") were not measured; likely very low (estimate). The client's Ubersuggest account could check this.

## 5. How do AI assistants choose suppliers for English supplier queries from non-English European countries?

### Takeaway
ChatGPT search uses approximate IP-based location by default, shares general location (not the IP) with third-party search providers, and may rewrite queries to include the location. An English query from Stockholm can therefore be localised to Sweden/Europe even though the language is English. Language handling is not documented. Being found therefore depends on (a) having pages that match "Sweden / EU / Europe + grip socks" intent, and (b) appearing in the search index the assistant uses (Bing for ChatGPT, Google for Gemini/AI Overviews).

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI: "ChatGPT may use an approximate location based on your IP address to provide relevant local results"; device location sharing is "optional and off by default"; ChatGPT "collects general location information based on your IP address and may share that general location with third-party search providers" but "does not share the IP address itself"; search queries may be rewritten to include location (example: "top restaurants San Francisco"); "A VPN or network location may affect the approximate location inferred". The help page says nothing about language — [OpenAI Help: Searching the web with ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt)
- Earlier project research: a US test user asking English questions got US results and never Mediterrano; Spanish questions from Spain regularly named Mediterrano (GA4: 9 chatgpt.com sessions, 23-30 Sep). Bing is the best-supported proxy for ChatGPT (Seer: 87% overlap, vendor study), Google for Gemini/AI Overviews, Brave for Claude. The brand is not in any training set, so everything runs through the live search layer — [project doc: ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01](claude/ai-zichtbaarheid-engels-duits-onderzoek-2026-10-01.md)

### Inferences
- A Swedish owner typing "custom grip socks for my pilates studio" in English is likely to get results biased toward Sweden/Europe (IP-derived location added to the query). Pages explicitly naming "Sweden", "EU", "Europe", "ships to Sweden in X days" are more likely to match that rewritten query. This is reasoning from the OpenAI documentation, not a tested result.
- Perplexity and Gemini location handling was not documented in sources retrieved here; assume similar IP localisation (unverified).
- The query language probably determines which language pages are retrieved (English query → English pages), so /en/ is the page set that can be cited for non-Spanish/German/Dutch Europeans. Inference only.

### Gaps
- No documentation found on how Perplexity, Gemini or Google AI Overviews weight user location versus query language for supplier queries.
- No empirical test from SE/IE/CY IPs was run in this session; the existing measurement protocol (VPN in target country, 3-4 runs per prompt) remains the way to verify.

## 6. Is English-for-Europe a better next step than a new language version (e.g. French)?

### Takeaway
On evidence available, English content for the EU is the cheaper, faster next step for incremental leads: /en/ already exists and already converts from SE and CY, and high-English, high-AI-use, high-price-level countries (Nordics, IE, plus MT/EE) have no competitor positioned as an EU supplier. French covers a single larger market (≈1,200 studios, strong growth, higher local price benchmark) but needs a full new build and native QA. These options are not mutually exclusive. The project's 07-10 plan already started /fr/, so the realistic question is sequencing.

### Cited Findings
- Leads from Sweden (/en/) and Cyprus (English) in the last two weeks without English-market marketing (brief, internal).
- France ≈1,200 pilates studios, growth ≈12%/yr; local benchmark 11.34 € at 100 pairs; Club Pilates scaling there (7 studios in Mar 2026, target 30-35 end 2026) — [project doc: studiotellingen-pt-fr-2026-10-02](claude/studiotellingen-pt-fr-onderzoek-2026-10-02.md)
- Earlier plan put Ireland as "cheap test via /en/ (EU, no customs)" and Denmark/Sweden "later as English test" — [project doc: landenkeuze-prijsbereidheid-2026-10-05](claude/landenkeuze-prijsbereidheid-2026-10-05.md)
- French EF EPI is "moderate" (539), so English pages reach French owners poorly; Nordic/NL/PT/AT/DE scores are "very high" — [Wikipedia, EF EPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EF_English_Proficiency_Index)
- 66% of consumers would choose a product whose information is in their language (B2C, 2020) — [CSA Research](https://csa-research.com/Blogs-Events/Blog/customer-online-buying-experience)

### Inferences
- Priority countries for English content, ranked on lead evidence × English/AI use × price level: (1) Sweden, (2) Ireland, (3) Denmark, (4) Cyprus + Malta, (5) Norway and Finland (Norway needs non-EU VAT handling), (6) Estonia/Latvia, (7) Greece and Portugal (reachable in English but lower price level; PT already rejected 6.95 €).
- Recommended English work (inference): one EU hub page; Sweden and Ireland pages with unique local facts; an EU-vs-US supplier guide (customs/VAT/delivery); English external mentions (Europages EN, LinkedIn EN, Faire, directories, English listicle inclusion). Measure via GSC country filter on /en/ and the VPN prompt protocol.
- French should not be dropped. It is the larger single prize and English cannot reach it. But English-for-EU work is mostly incremental pages on an existing section, so it can run in parallel at lower cost.

### Gaps
- Lead-to-order conversion for SE/CY is unknown (n=2 leads, no closed orders reported).
- No measured English search volume for country-modified B2B queries; no live SERP/AI-citation snapshot for the target queries.
- Intra-EU B2B VAT treatment for Mediterrano's specific case (and Norwegian/UK import VAT) needs confirmation by the gestor before it is used as a site claim.
