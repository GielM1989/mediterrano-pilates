Scope note: research done with web search plus fetches, 14 Oct 2026. The fetch tool refused chaussettes-pilates.fr and pylates.studio ("URL not in provenance set"), and neither site appeared in any search result, so their MOQ, price, lead time and technique could **not** be re-verified here. APIC did not surface either. Competitor facts given in the brief (APIC 11,34 €/pair at 100 pairs; French studio counts; Club Pilates FR ~16 studios) come from earlier internal project research, not from this pass. ## 1. French search landscape and competition

### Takeaway
For the target French queries, the open web search used here (not Google.fr itself) returned no dedicated B2B "custom logo grip socks for studios" supplier. Results were taken up by retail (Decathlon, Urban Outfitters), sourcing marketplaces (Alibaba FR, Accio) and a Team France Export sourcing request. This points to a thin specialist field, which fits the earlier internal finding of only two small French specialists. It does not replace a live Google.fr SERP check.

### Cited Findings
- Query "chaussettes antidérapantes personnalisées logo studio pilates": results were only Team France Export listings (one opportunity syndicated under 6 URLs), plus Accio sourcing pages for "chaussette antidérapante pilates" and "chaussette personnalisé logo". No specialist supplier and no promo shop (Vistaprint, Pandacola, Cadoétik) appeared. — [Team France Export](https://www.teamfrance-export.fr/opportunites/recherche-dun-fabricant-de-chaussettes-de-pilates-antiderapantes-sur-mesure-pour-la-suede); [Accio FR](https://fr.accio.com/plp/chaussettes-antid%C3%A9rapantes-pilates)
- Query "chaussettes pilates personnalisées logo minimum commande studio": Alibaba FR plus the same Team France Export page. — [Alibaba FR](https://french.alibaba.com/f/socquettes.html)
- Query "chaussettes-pilates.fr": the domain did not appear. Results were Decathlon FR/CH category pages, Urban Outfitters and On. — [Decathlon FR](https://www.decathlon.fr/vc/chaussettes-pilates); [Decathlon CH-fr](https://www.decathlon.ch/fr/vc/chaussettes-de-pilates)
- Query "APIC chaussettes antidérapantes personnalisées": no APIC result. Results were medical and workwear socks (RS Online, Medline, UGAP) and Decathlon. — [Medline FR](https://www.medline.eu/fr/single-tread-slipper-socks-blue)
- Demand signal: on 8 Jul 2026 Team France Export published a request from a Swedish start-up for a manufacturer of custom anti-slip Reformer Pilates socks (silicone sole, colour, design and packaging customisation, low MOQ valued). It closes 8 Dec 2026, ref. BRSE20260622007. This is a brand-builder lead, not a studio lead. — [Team France Export](https://www.teamfrance-export.fr/opportunites/recherche-dun-fabricant-de-chaussettes-de-pilates-antiderapantes-sur-mesure-pour-la-suede)
- Internal prior research (02-10-2026): France has two small specialists (chaussettes-pilates.fr, Pylates.studio) and over 1.200 studios. France scored 200/625, second after ES (400) and ahead of IT (160). — project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`

### Inferences
- Under this search engine, the B2B niche phrases have little dedicated content. A French page set that answers them directly (price per pair, MOQ 50, mockup in 24 h, delivery to France) could plausibly rank, because the competing pages are retail category pages that do not match B2B intent. This is inference: no search-volume data was available.
- Promo-goods shops (Vistaprint, Pandacola, Cadoétik) likely rank on the generic "chaussettes personnalisées logo" head terms. They did not appear for the pilates/studio-specific long tail in this test. "merchandising studio pilates" and "goodies studio yoga" were not tested separately, which is a gap.
- The Swedish sourcing request shows a second B2B segment, small pilates sock brands, that a French or English page could also capture.

### Gaps
- No live Google.fr SERP (localised, incognito) and no keyword volumes (Google Keyword Planner, Semrush FR). Needed before final prioritisation.
- chaussettes-pilates.fr and Pylates.studio were not verifiable (fetch blocked, not indexed by this engine). MOQ, price, lead time and technique remain unknown from this pass.
- APIC's 11,34 €/pair at 100 pairs is not re-verified.

## 2. AI answers in France

### Takeaway
Google AI Overviews and AI Mode went live in France only on 22 July 2026, roughly 2 years after the US and later than most of Europe. About half of the French population uses generative AI, mainly ChatGPT and Gemini. AI answers are a real channel in France, but a very new one there on Google's side.

### Cited Findings
- Google activated AI Overviews ("Aperçu IA") and AI Mode in France on Wednesday 22 July 2026, rolled out gradually over several days. — [The Media Leader FR](https://fr.themedialeader.com/google-active-ai-overviews-dans-son-moteur-de-recherche-en-france/)
- Before launch, Google committed to French publishers (letter of 29 June 2026, to about 450 publishers) to offer an opt-out, separate reporting of AI impressions and neighbouring-rights compensation. Ranking criteria for AI features are said to stay the same as classic search (quality, authority). The delay was linked to the DMA and to the neighbouring-rights dispute. — [Solutions Numériques](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/?p=248801); [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/06/30/google-va-bouleverser-la-recherche-en-france-avec-ses-resumes-ia-des-cet-ete/)
- AI Mode had previously launched in Europe while excluding France. — [La Réclame](https://lareclame.fr/google-ai-mode-europe-324203); [Siècle Digital Oct 2025](https://siecledigital.fr/2025/10/10/pourquoi-la-france-est-exclue-du-grand-deploiement-mondial-de-lia-de-google/)
- Baromètre du numérique 2026 (Arcep/CGE/Arcom/ANCT, CREDOC fieldwork June 2025, published Feb 2026): 48% of the French population uses generative AI, up 15 points in a year. The rate is 76% among cadres (managers and professionals) and 85% among young adults. More than one in three users use it daily. Usage is concentrated on ChatGPT and Gemini; Le Chat and Perplexity are named as known. No per-tool percentages were given. — [Univers Freebox](https://www.universfreebox.com/article/591439/chatgpt-gemini-le-chat-lia-generative-explose-en-france-pres-dun-francais-sur-deux-deja-utilisateur/amp)
- A US study cited by France Inter (and Pew, July 2025) found users click links about half as often when an AI summary appears. — [Solutions Numériques](https://www.solutions-numeriques.com/?p=248801); [The Media Leader FR](https://fr.themedialeader.com/google-active-ai-overviews-dans-son-moteur-de-recherche-en-france/)

### Inferences
- Studio owners are mostly self-employed professionals, close to the "cadres" profile, so their AI usage is probably above the 48% average. This is inference, not measured.
- Le Chat (Mistral) is not dominant per the Arcep barometer, so optimising for ChatGPT, Gemini/AI Overviews and Perplexity covers most French AI usage.
- Because AI Overviews is only about 12 weeks old in France, the citation landscape for niche B2B queries is probably still forming. That is an early-mover argument for French content now.

### Gaps
- Which sources ChatGPT, Gemini or AI Overviews actually cite for French supplier queries was not tested. Running the FR prompt set (already planned internally) in Peec or Otterly is needed.
- No French per-tool share data (ChatGPT vs Le Chat) with percentages was found.

## 3. Coverage bonus: Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Quebec

### Takeaway
A French section reaches Wallonia/Brussels, Romandie and Luxembourg with the same pages. Decathlon runs French-language pilates sock category pages for Switzerland, which confirms the term set carries over. No studio counts were found for these markets in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Decathlon Switzerland has a French-language "Chaussettes de Pilates" category, so the same terminology is used in Romandie. — [Decathlon CH-fr](https://www.decathlon.ch/fr/vc/chaussettes-de-pilates)
- On.com publishes a fr-BE pilates content page ("what to wear to pilates"), a sign of a French-language pilates audience in Belgium. — [On fr-BE](https://www.on.com/fr-be/stories/what-to-wear-to-pilates)
- Internal plan (02-10-2026): Belgium/Luxembourg to be served via /fr/. BE scored 120 and CH 48 on the internal scoring. — project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`

### Inferences
- BE and LU are inside the EU, so free EU shipping and VAT logic apply. Switzerland is outside the EU and customs applies, so the FAQ should state shipping to Switzerland explicitly rather than "Europe" only.
- Quebec is reachable linguistically, but delivery and pricing would be a separate offer ("wereldwijd leveren mogelijk"). It is low priority, and an fr-CA hreflang is not needed at start.

### Gaps
- No studio counts for Wallonia/Brussels, Romandie, Luxembourg or Quebec were found.

## 4. Price acceptance in France

### Takeaway
French retail grip-sock prices are well above the 7,50 € per-pair supply price. Decathlon.fr lists pilates socks from 5,99 € to 24,90 €, with a median of about 12,45 € and branded pairs at 15-25 €. That supports the "merch is revenue" margin argument at the internal advice price of 17,95-19,95 €.

### Cited Findings
- Decathlon.fr "Chaussettes pilates": range 5,99 € to 24,90 €, typical 9-20 €, median about 12,45 €. Examples: Myga Gripped 14,99 €, adidas 5-toe 15,00 €, Mewave 19,99 €, Revenge 1-finger up to 24,90 €. — [Decathlon FR](https://www.decathlon.fr/vc/chaussettes-pilates)
- A Bordeaux reformer studio lists grip socks as "fortement conseillées" (strongly recommended), not mandatory. The listing gives no on-site price. — [Urban Sports Club, Pilates Park Reformer](https://urbansportsclub.com/fr/venues/pilates-park-reformer-1)
- Internal margin table: at 20% French VAT, an order of 50 pairs at 7,50 € sold at 17,95-19,95 € gives roughly 373-456 € margin per order. — project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`
- Internal: APIC at 11,34 €/pair at 100 pairs (internal only, not re-verified). — brief / prior project research

### Inferences
- If APIC's price holds, 7,50 € is clearly below the visible French B2B alternative. The Spanish price objection ("too expensive") is less likely to recur in France, given the higher retail anchor of 15-25 € for branded pairs. This is inference.
- "Strongly recommended" rather than "mandatory" at some studios means the copy should sell socks as a hygiene, grip and branding item, not assume every studio requires them.

### Gaps
- No direct data on what French studios charge at the front desk for branded socks. A sample of 10-20 French studio websites or Instagram shops would settle it.
- No purchasing-power comparison (FR vs ES vs IT) was sourced in this pass.

## 5. Practical requirements (French B2B)

### Takeaway
The legal risk for a B2B site in French is low. The practical bar is high: native-quality French with vouvoiement, complete company identification (mentions légales style) and French terminology. Machine translation without native review is a reputational risk with French buyers.

### Cited Findings
- The Loi Toubon (1994) requires French for selling or advertising goods and services in France. The one well-known website case (Georgia Tech Lorraine, 1997) was decided on procedure, not on whether websites must be in French, so how it applies to foreign websites remained untested in that case. — [Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/12570)
- French-language obligations also apply to employment relationships (DREETS), which is not relevant for a Spanish employer. — [DREETS Normandie](https://normandie.dreets.gouv.fr/l-emploi-obligatoire-de-la-langue-francaise-dans-les-relations-de-travail)
- Internal: cold B2B e-mail in France is opt-out if job-related and with an unsubscribe link (CNIL). — project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`

### Inferences
- Publishing a French section reduces Toubon exposure rather than creating it, because offering in France in Spanish or English is the riskier position. Not legal advice.
- B2B expectations: vouvoiement throughout, prices shown HT (excl. VAT) with TVA mentioned, company identification (Ecomtierra Group SL, CIF/NIF and intra-EU VAT number, address) and a clear delivery promise to France, Belgium, Luxembourg and Switzerland.
- Native review is required for terminology. For example, "chaussettes antidérapantes" vs "chaussettes grip", and "goodies" vs "merchandising" vs "objets personnalisés".

### Gaps
- No authoritative source found on mentions légales requirements for an EU (non-French) company's French-language site. The EU e-Commerce Directive identification duties of the home country (Spain, LSSI) apply regardless. Not verified here.

## 6. Italy: brief comparison

### Takeaway
Italy now has strong and fast-growing AI usage (ChatGPT 14,7 million monthly users in Oct 2025). Its SERP for "calzini antiscivolo personalizzati pilates" was dominated by Decathlon retail and generic print-on-demand, with no studio-B2B specialist surfacing. That makes it an open niche too. France stays first: larger studio base, higher internal score, a 20% VAT margin story, and francophone spill-over to BE, CH and LU. Italy is a solid second, not a better first.

### Cited Findings
- Comscore (Oct 2025, published 8 Dec 2025): over 16 million monthly AI-app users in Italy (35% of the online population, +89% y/y). ChatGPT had 14,7 million monthly users, nearly triple Oct 2024. Perplexity had 745.000 (+2.351%). Gemini had 2,8 million and Copilot 2,7 million (Apr 2025). — [Orizzonte Scuola / Comscore](https://www.orizzontescuola.it/lintelligenza-artificiale-raggiunge-16-milioni-di-utenti-in-italia-chatgpt-domina-il-mercato-perplexity-registra-una-crescita-del-2-351-in-un-anno-secondo-i-dati-comscore/)
- Conflicting measure: Eurostat puts Italy among the lowest in the EU for generative AI use (19,9% vs 32,7% EU average). — [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/it/?p=465); [9colonne](https://www.9colonne.it/580736/generative-ai-adoption-remains-low-in-italy-eurostat-reports)
- Italian queries "calzini antiscivolo personalizzati logo pilates studio" and "calze antiscivolo personalizzate logo studio pilates ordine minimo" returned Decathlon.it categories, ManoMano, Shein "customized athletic socks", Printful IT and Qstomize. No studio-B2B grip-sock specialist appeared. — [Decathlon IT](https://www.decathlon.it/tutti-gli-sport/pilates/calze-e-calzini-antiscivolo); [Printful IT](https://www.printful.com/it/calzini-personalizzati); [Qstomize IT](https://www.qstomize.com/it/temi/custom-socks)
- Italian brand Revenge (R-EVENGE, Revenge Srl) sells pilates socks at up to 24,90 € on Decathlon FR, so Italy has domestic pilates sock brands. — [Decathlon FR](https://www.decathlon.fr/vc/chaussettes-pilates)
- Internal: one fitness-wide Italian specialist (Pgoplay). Italy scored 160/625 vs France 200. VAT is 22%, margin roughly 361-443 € per 50-pair order. — project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`

### Inferences
- Italy's AI adoption among actual users is high (ChatGPT reach close to that of France's top tools), but Eurostat suggests the general population lags. For B2B studio owners the Comscore reach data is the more relevant signal.
- The Italian niche looks at least as open as the French one. The main reasons to sequence France first are market size (internal studio counts) and the multi-country reach of French.

### Gaps
- No Italian studio count was re-verified. No Italian price anchors at studios. No AI citation test for Italian prompts.

## 7. Time to first results and minimum viable section

### Takeaway
No authoritative timeline exists. Inferring from the site's own track record (leads within about 1 month of launch), from the fact that new language folders on an established domain inherit its crawl and authority, and from the thin French SERP, a 6-8 page /fr/ section can plausibly be indexed within days to weeks and produce first long-tail impressions within 2-8 weeks. Treat this as an estimate, not a measured fact.

### Cited Findings
- Google's documentation on multilingual sites covers subdirectory structures (e.g. /fr/), the use of hreflang, and the use of distinct URLs per language version. — [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)
- Internal: the site went live about 1 month ago and already gets 1-3 organic leads/day (ES, DE, SE, CY). IndexNow, the Bing URL Submission API and hreflang/JSON-LD/llms tasks are already planned per language. — brief; project doc `claude/europese-expansie-pt-fr-it-onderzoek-en-plan-2026-10-02.md`

### Inferences
- A minimum viable /fr/ (6-8 pages) is: home/offer, chaussettes antidérapantes personnalisées (product, price per pair, MOQ 50), prix et marge (20% TVA), maquette gratuite, FAQ (shipping to FR/BE/LU/CH and worldwide), à propos (entity facts), and 1-2 niche pages (yoga, barre). This covers the commercial intent clusters. Blogs can follow.
- Effort (estimate): translating and adapting the existing ES/EN templates into 6-8 FR pages, plus native review, hreflang and schema, is comparable to one existing language build. Native review is the bottleneck.
- Deepening existing languages (ES, EN, DE, NL) has diminishing returns in DE and NL, where internal research found the niche crowded. A new open-niche language likely has a higher marginal return than more ES/DE pages. ES remains the core and should keep a small content cadence.

### Gaps
- No published study gives typical indexing or ranking times for a new language subfolder. Measure via GSC (/fr/ property filter) and the AI tracker from week 1.
