# Spain: Google (google.es) and AEO opportunity for Mediterrano Pilates (October 2026)

Method note, applies to all sections: the only search tool available was a US-based web search. It does not return a live google.es results page, related searches or People Also Ask boxes. "Who ranks" below means "who the search index returns for the Spanish query". This is a proxy for google.es, not a screenshot of it. No search volumes were available from any source. All volume statements are relative inferences. The project's existing keyword file says the same: volumes are estimates, and the client's Ubersuggest account has not been used yet ([project doc zoekwoorden-en-clusters.md], internal). A search for the brand's own domain plus its main term returned no mediterranopilates.com result ([search results](https://www.decathlon.es/es/deportes/pilates/calcetines)). This fits a one-month-old site and is not proof that it lacks Google rankings.

## 1. Which Spanish search terms do studio owners use?

### Takeaway
Studio-owner searches split into three groups: (a) transactional terms like "calcetines antideslizantes personalizados (con logo)" and "calcetines pilates con logo", where results mix B2B promo shops and Alibaba; (b) owner-business terms like "abrir/cuánto cuesta abrir un estudio/centro de pilates", "requisitos", "aumentar ingresos", where software vendors and franchise portals rank; (c) gift and season terms like "regalos pilates (navidad)", which are dominated by consumer retail (Decathlon). Consumer terms such as "mejores calcetines antideslizantes pilates" carry the most volume but bring the wrong buyer.

### Inferences
- The search terms the brief listed are the right families. The evidence is the result mix, not volume data:
  - High buyer intent, studio searcher: "calcetines antideslizantes personalizados (con logo)", "calcetines pilates con logo", "calcetines antideslizantes con logo para estudio/gimnasio", "calcetines pilates personalizados pedido mínimo", "fabricante calcetines pilates".
  - Owner-business, upper funnel: "abrir estudio/centro de pilates", "cuánto cuesta abrir un estudio de pilates (reformer)", "requisitos para abrir un centro de pilates", "aumentar ingresos estudio", "qué vender en mi estudio".
  - Gift and season: "regalos para alumnas de pilates", "regalos navidad estudio pilates", "pack de bienvenida".
- Searchers seem to use "centro de pilates" and "estudio de pilates" side by side. Virtuagym and L'Express both use "centro" in titles. Mediterrano pages that mention only "estudio" may miss half of these phrasings. Using both is a cheap content fix.
- "Barre" in Spain looks like a very small, branded niche (Barrefit® dominates the results). It is still worth a page because nobody covers it, but expect low volume.

### Gaps
- No google.es search volumes, related searches or PAA data were available. Before ranking by volume, the client should run the terms in Ubersuggest/Google Keyword Planner (country = Spain).
- The ranking of mediterranopilates.com for its own terms could not be checked. Google Search Console is the only reliable source.
- No Google Trends seasonality data was retrieved.

### Inferences
- Transactional terms are winnable in 1–3 months because the incumbents have thin, generic product pages. A specific page with process, MOQ, mockup, REACH and price/margin outperforms them on relevance. Mediterrano already has this page (/calcetines-antideslizantes-personalizados/). The work now is reinforcement (internal links, "con logo"/"para estudios"/"pedido mínimo" phrasing, fresh date, FAQ) plus off-site mentions, not a new page.
- "Abrir estudio de pilates" is a strong-incumbent SERP (Virtuagym has four articles). A generic guide will not outrank it in 1–3 months. A narrow angle Virtuagym does not cover can win both long-tail rankings and AI citations, for example "qué incluir en la apertura: recepción, pack de bienvenida, merchandising" or "checklist de apertura de un estudio de barre/reformer".
- The Pilates10 shop shows that chains already sell logo socks, towels and totes to members. This works as a proof point in copy only as a general statement ("algunas cadenas ya venden calcetines con su logo"), and the competitor's prices must not be cited externally.
- Merchery is the most direct Europe-wide specialist seen. It is English-first, so Spanish-language depth is Mediterrano's defensible edge.

### Gaps
- Domain authority and backlink counts of competitors could not be measured (no SEO tool access).
- The search did not surface Spanish specialists that sell grip socks to studios, such as Spanish brands selling own-label grip socks wholesale. This does not prove that none exist. The project doc already says no Spanish specialist was found ([zoekwoorden-en-clusters.md], internal).

### Inferences
- Rising worry about competition among studios (24.5%→56.9%, Eversports via El Debate) fits the "differentiate and retain" angle that Mediterrano's loyalty and branding content already takes. This angle can be argued in an article without citing these numbers on the site.
- Many new studios, including franchise openings, create an "opening checklist" audience. A short specialist piece on "qué necesitas en recepción el día de la apertura" could rank and get cited where Virtuagym's long guide is silent. The project has a planned "abrir estudio pilates checklist" page (project doc `claude/nieuwe-pagina-abrir-estudio-pilates-checklist-v37.md`), but the brief's list of live pages does not include it. Its status should be checked before building anything new.
- Topics most likely to be cited by AI Overviews or ChatGPT in Spanish, because they have answerable, specific questions and no Spanish competitor:
  - "¿Qué productos vender en la recepción de un estudio de pilates?"
  - "¿Cómo fijar el precio de venta del merchandising de tu estudio?" (Mediterrano's own 7,50 € → 17,95 € example is allowed, since it comes from merk-en-feiten)
  - "Merchandising para estudios de barre: qué tiene sentido"
  - "Branding de un estudio de pilates: colores, logo y productos"
  - "Calendario anual de campañas de un estudio (Navidad, enero, primavera, septiembre)"

### Gaps
- Whether AI Overviews actually appear for these Spanish queries could not be observed.
- No Spanish forum or Reddit-style threads from studio owners were found. Spanish owner communities seem to live on Instagram, WhatsApp groups and teacher-training schools, which search does not index.

## 4. Seasonality: what to publish now, and what to prepare for spring?

### Takeaway
Two windows are open now. Christmas has a hard production deadline (mockup approval by mid-November, ~4 weeks delivery). January new-member intake needs content live in October–November so it can be indexed and cited by December. Spring (Easter, summer-body and "vuelta" campaigns, and September as the second intake) should be drafted in January–February.

### Cited Findings
- Production facts from the brief: ~4 weeks delivery, mockup in 24 h, min. 50 pairs, free shipping in Europe. Mid-November mockup approval is therefore the practical Christmas cut-off (brief; consistent with project doc merk-en-feiten, internal).
- The project's keyword doc assumes buying peaks around January and September and recommends content 6–8 weeks before, labelled "Dit is een aanname, nog niet bevestigd in Trends". — [zoekwoorden-en-clusters.md, internal]
- Consumer gift demand for pilates is large enough that Decathlon maintains a dedicated "Regalos de pilates" category with ~770–868 items. — [Decathlon regalos](https://www.decathlon.es/es/regalos/regalos-de-pilates)

### Inferences
- **Now (mid-October to mid-November)**, upgrade the existing /blog/regalos-de-navidad-alumnas-pilates/ with:
  - an explicit "fecha límite" block: mockup approval before roughly 15 November to receive before Christmas, phrased as an estimate based on the ~4 weeks;
  - a "regalo para el equipo/instructoras" angle;
  - a short answer box: "¿Qué regalar a las alumnas de un estudio de pilates en Navidad?"
  The consumer SERP is Decathlon, but studio-owner phrasings ("regalo de navidad para alumnas", "detalle navidad clientas estudio") have no owner-facing result.
- **Now, for January**, refresh /blog/pack-de-bienvenida-alumnas-nuevas-pilates/ as a "campaña de enero / propósitos de año nuevo" piece, or write a new article "Campaña de enero para estudios de pilates: captar y retener a las alumnas nuevas". Order deadline for early-January delivery: mockup approval by early December.
- **Prepare for spring (draft January–February)**: "merchandising de verano/primavera" (tumbler and tote as summer products), "Día de la Madre (first Sunday of May in Spain) gift ideas for studios", and September "vuelta a la rutina" as the second intake.
- The Oviedo barre lead (opening studio, burgundy accessories) shows that "opening a studio" is itself a trigger moment with no season. Opening-checklist content should be evergreen.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends curves were retrieved for "regalos pilates", "calcetines antideslizantes" or "abrir estudio de pilates". The season assumptions above are unverified and should be checked in Trends (Spain, 5 years).
- Spanish studio-opening seasonality (when studios open) is unknown.
