What other studios ask us.

See all frequently asked questions →

01Can I start with a small quantity?

Yes. Grip socks start at 50 pairs, so even a small studio can begin without a big stock risk. For tumblers, totes and rings, tell us how many units you have in mind and we will prepare a tailored quote.

02How long does the whole process take, from order to delivery?

You get your mockup within 24 hours. Once you approve the quote and complete payment, production and shipping together take around 4 weeks until it arrives at your studio. We keep you posted at every step, so you never have to guess where your order is.

03How much does it cost?

You buy at wholesale price, excluding VAT, and resell with a margin of between 40% and 50% after deducting VAT. Socks, for example, cost you around €9 a pair and studios across Europe sell them for between €17.95 and €21.95 including VAT, which is approximately €5.85 to €9.15 margin per pair. A first order usually starts at a few hundred euros. Request your mockup with no commitment and you will receive the full price list.

04How often do studios reorder?

Several times a year. Socks wear out with heavy use, new clients arrive every month, and a fresh seasonal colour lets you refresh the range again and again. That turns merchandise into recurring income, not a one-off.

05Can I choose the colours?

Absolutely. We match your brand colours by Pantone code, the standard colour system in the graphics industry. That way your sock colour is exactly the same as your logo. Nothing "approximate": the real colour of your studio.

06What about shipping?

Always free, anywhere in the world. Wherever you are, shipping is on us, with no hidden costs.

07Isn't this just a nice extra?

It's more than that. The margin at reception is nice, but the real value is elsewhere: every client wearing your logo is advertising you don't pay for, and a consistent brand helps people stay longer. Increasing retention by just 5% can mean up to 95% more profit (Bain & Company). The merchandise isn't the cherry on top, it's part of why people choose you, and why they stay.

08Can I see the quality before ordering?

Yes, and we recommend it before a first order. We send a sample set (grip sock, tumbler and tote) for a small fee that is fully credited against your first order, so the samples cost you nothing when you go ahead. Just ask for it when you request your mockup, or reply to the mockup we send you.

09Are the materials safe?

Yes. We verify that every material meets REACH, the European chemical safety regulation, including the anti-slip sole, something most generic suppliers don't check separately from the fabric. Certificates are available if you need them.

10Do you ship outside Spain?

Yes, to any country in the world, always for free. We're based in Spain but work with studios across Europe and beyond.

Who we are
I don't practise Pilates, but I find it a beautiful sport.

One thing has always struck me: the world around it is just as beautiful as the sport itself. The calm of a well-kept studio, that morning light, the way everything is thought through down to the last detail. At one point I thought: shouldn't I invest in this?

What I did notice, from my background in branding and e-commerce: everywhere I looked, I saw the same pattern. Beautiful spaces, good classes, and then, at reception, a sock that didn't match anything else.

So instead of investing, I did something else: I solved that detail for studios that simply don't have the time or the craft to deal with it themselves. You focus on your community, I make sure what they take home lives up to the studio they just walked out of.

And yes, the margin you make on those products is nice, but it isn't the point. What really counts: every tumbler, tote or sock with your brand that your community carries is free advertising for your studio, one more reason to come back, and sometimes the start of a new client you would never have reached otherwise.
