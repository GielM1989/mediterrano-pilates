What data do we collect and why?

Through our request form we collect your name, your email address, your studio name, the products you are interested in, your message, the logo you upload and (optionally) your Instagram account and phone number. We also record which ad or link brought you to the site (for example, an Instagram ad).   • respond to your request and send you a mockup and a quote;

  • measure which ads and pages are effective, but only if you consent to this via the cookie banner.

  • Your request and order: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

We do not send newsletters without your consent and we never sell your data to third parties. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

How long do we keep your data?

If your request does not lead to us working together, we delete your data no later than two years after our last contact. If we do work together, we keep your data for as long as we work together. We keep invoices and accounting records for as long as Spanish law requires (generally six years). We use your logo only for your products.

Cookies

This website uses analytics and advertising cookies only with your consent. On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline them. If you decline, the site works in exactly the same way. Without your consent, we only store what is strictly necessary.

  • _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics 4): distinguish visitors and sessions. Retention: up to 2 years. Only with consent.
  • _fbp (Meta Pixel): measures the results of ads. Retention: 3 months. Only with consent.
  • mp_cookie_consent (local storage in your browser): remembers your cookie choice, so that we do not need to ask you again. Functional.
  • mp_lang_choice (local storage in your browser): remembers your language choice. Functional.

You can change your choice or withdraw your consent at any time via the Cookies link at the bottom of the homepage, by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by emailing us at hola@mediterranopilates.com.
